2. **`<<INSTALLER_LEGAL_NAME>>`** (ABN `<<INSTALLER_ABN>>`) trading as **`<<INSTALLER_TRADING_NAME>>`** (**Installer**)

## 1. VEU Accreditation and Licensing Warranty
1.1 The Installer warrants that it holds current Victorian electrical/plumbing licences (`<<LICENCE_TYPE_AND_NUMBER>>`) and either:
- (a) is an Accredited Person under the Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) program, or
- (b) has a current written arrangement with VEU Accredited Provider **`<<ACCREDITED_PROVIDER_NAME>>`** to create Victorian Energy Efficiency Certificates (VEECs) for the activities covered by this Agreement (`rcac` / `hotwater`), entered into **before** any installation work commences.
1.2 The Installer must notify the Publisher immediately if its licence or relationship with its VEU Accredited Provider is suspended, terminated, or changed.

## 2. Express Prior Consent and VEU Code of Conduct Obligations
2.1 Each Enquiry delivered by the Publisher includes an auditable **Express Prior Consent Record** containing the customer's selected contact channels (`phone`, `email`, and/or `site_visit`), the timestamp consent was given, the 3-month expiry timestamp, the exact consent wording shown to the customer, and its SHA-256 hash.
2.2 The Installer strictly agrees that it and its employees, subcontractors, and Accredited Provider:
- (a) **Must only contact the customer via the specific channel(s) ticked** in the Express Prior Consent Record;
- (b) **Must never make unsolicited telemarketing calls or doorknock** any person;
- (c) **Must cease all contact immediately** upon the earlier of: (i) the 3-month consent expiry date (unless the customer separately agrees to a longer period directly with the Installer), or (ii) receiving a Consent Withdrawal Notice from the Publisher or the customer;
- (d) **Must never resell, forward, or share** any Enquiry with any third party (other than the named Accredited Provider solely for creating certificates for that customer's installation);
- (e) Must comply in full with the *Victorian Energy Efficiency Target Act 2007*, the **VEU Code of Conduct**, the *Spam Act 2003* (Cth), the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth), and the *Australian Consumer Law*.

## 3. Lead Fees, Invoicing, and Dud Enquiry Policy
3.1 **Lead Fee:** `<<FEE_AUD_INC_GST>>` (benchmark: A$39.00 inc GST) per Accepted Enquiry delivered to `<<CONTACT_EMAIL_FOR_LEADS>>`.
3.2 **Dud Enquiry criteria:** An Enquiry may be returned as a `dud` within 5 business days of delivery (with no fee payable) if and only if:
- (a) the telephone number and email address are both disconnected or invalid;
- (b) the property postcode is outside the Installer's agreed postcode list;
- (c) the customer already has installation work underway before the Enquiry was submitted; or
- (d) the customer is a duplicate of an Enquiry delivered to the Installer within the preceding 30 days.

## 4. Audit Records and Termination
4.1 Either party may suspend or terminate lead delivery immediately by written notice.
4.2 The Publisher retains the Consent Audit Record (which contains personal information) for 24 months following consent expiry or withdrawal to assist with any Essential Services Commission (ESC) audit enquiry, and handles it under its privacy policy.
