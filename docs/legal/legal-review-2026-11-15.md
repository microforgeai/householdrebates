## What's already good (no change needed)

- **Alerts consent.** The checkbox is unticked, the list is separate from the quote form (no bundling with the quote form), and double opt-in uses a 72-hour link. A consent snapshot, its SHA-256 hash and a timestamp are stored. This matches ACMA's express-consent and record-keeping expectations.
- **Confirmation email.** It's only sent after someone submits the form, so it doesn't fall under ACMA's ban on "asking for consent" by email. It contains no promotion and says "If you didn't ask for this, ignore it". Keep it that way.
- **Unsubscribe.** It works without logging in, the tokens never expire (more than the 30 days required), it supports RFC 8058 one-click, and email requests are handled within 5 business days.
- **Sender block.** A build error is raised if any sender placeholder is unresolved, and every email states that Household Rebates is not a government website.
- **Quote form.** It has separate phone, email and visit tick boxes, a 3-month expiry and a withdrawal route. The installer and accredited provider are named, the fee is disclosed at the point of collection, and the form doesn't ask for an address, date of birth or income.
- **Figures and copy.** Figures are date-stamped with a 45-day staleness block. Banned-phrase checks in `check-copy.mjs` block wording such as "guaranteed", "you qualify" and "official".
- **Go-live gates.** `legalReviewed`, `ALERTS_REVIEWED_BY` and a per-send SHA-256 approval all have to be set before anything goes live.

## Needs a real lawyer

Only these items genuinely need a lawyer:

1. **Installer Lead Supply Agreement** (`docs/installer-agreement-draft.md`). This covers lead fees, the dud-lead policy, termination, indemnities for installer misuse (calls after withdrawal, reselling), and whether Agenor is a "scheme participant" under the VEU Code of Conduct. Also: whether the 24-month consent-record period matches ESC audit and record-keeping rules.
2. **The trading-in-personal-information question.** Should Agenor rely on the s6D(7) consent exception or opt in under s6EA? What does opting in mean for Agenor's other businesses? Is a Malaysia-hosted VPS (plus a possibly US-based mail provider) a "use" or a "disclosure" under APP 8, and what contract terms does Hostinger need to meet?
3. **The liability cap and the enforceability of the Terms.** Does a website-only "terms of use" with no contract need a cap at all? If it does, how should it be worded so it isn't an unfair contract term and doesn't cut across ACL consumer guarantees?
4. **VEU express prior consent.** This is Q1 in the existing lawyer pack: does `CONSENT_TEMPLATE_V1`, once fixed under #6 and #7, meet ESC requirements?

**Cheap path:**
- Fix #1–#11 (and as many of #12–#28 as you can) first, so the lawyer's time goes on judgement, not typos.
- Get a free initial consultation of up to 30 minutes through the [LIV Find Your Lawyer Referral Service](https://www.liv.asn.au/referral) and ask for a **fixed-fee quote** to review four documents: the privacy policy plus the two notices, the terms, the installer agreement, and the s6D/s6EA question.
- Ask for written mark-ups, not a memo.
- Do this after the 28 Nov 2026 election re-check, so the review doesn't need repeating.
- The alerts list can launch on items 2 and 3 alone. Items 1 and 4 only matter before the quote form opens.
