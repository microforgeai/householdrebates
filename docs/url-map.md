## Rule changes and corrections found (13–14 Nov 2026)

1. **New VEU co-payments from 30 Sep 2026.** **Every ducted reverse-cycle system, of any capacity,** now needs at least **$3,000 incl GST** from the customer (was $1,000). Multi-split ≥ 10 kW: $3,000. Multi-split < 10 kW: $1,000. Single split < 10 kW: still $200. Source: [VEU Specifications v25.0](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0041/795488/Victorian-Energy-Upgrades-Specifications-2018-Version-25.pdf), Part 6, and the [Energy Vic specifications page](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades/installers/industry-specifications). Energy Vic's consumer page doesn't mention it yet. **Existing pages `/victoria/air-conditioner-rebate/` and `/victoria/reverse-cycle-vs-gas-heater/`, and the checker's ducted results, need this added.**
2. **2027 gas hot water exemptions are now verified.** solution-design.md §1 lists them as UNVERIFIED (third-party). I read the regulation text: [SR 140/2025 reg 6, new reg 45E (AustLII)](https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/num_reg/bapar2025n140o2025463/s6.html); authority: [legislation.vic.gov.au](https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/as-made/statutory-rules/building-and-plumbing-amendment-regulations-2025). Full list in the `/victoria/gas-hot-water-ban-exemptions/` row. It includes contracts signed before 1 Mar 2027 if the work is done within 3 months.
3. **New VEU product: ceiling insulation**: up to **$1,500** for a 150 m² house in metro Melbourne (mild) and **$2,100** in regional Victoria (cold). Stage 2 is open to all eligible homes. Sources: [Energy Vic](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades/products/insulation-discounts), [Solar Victoria](https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/victorians-cut-energy-bills-ceiling-insulation-discounts). Added as a Wave 1 money page. **Geelong (answered 14 Nov):** neither figure applies cleanly. The spec formula is GHG savings by *climate region* × a *regional factor* by *metro/regional area* ([v25.0 Part 48, Tables 48.4–48.5](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0041/795488/Victorian-Energy-Upgrades-Specifications-2018-Version-25.pdf)). Geelong 3212–3227 is Regional but Mild, so it gets Melbourne's Mild savings value with the Regional factor (1.04 instead of 0.98), about 6% above the metro example: roughly **$1,590** (ASSUMPTION: same 150 m² house and certificate price). Don't quote $2,100 for Geelong; say 'about $1,500–$1,600, provider confirms'.
4. **Solar Homes income cap cut to $150,000** (from $210,000) on 1 Jul 2026, for PV and hot water. [Source](https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/solar-homes-program-continuing-provide-targeted-assistance). Existing `/victoria/heat-pump-rebate/` should state it.
5. **Solar PV support moves into VEU from 1 Jul 2027.** Current PV rebates (up to $1,400 plus a loan) continue to 30 Jun 2027. [Source](https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/solar-support-continue-through-victorian-energy-upgrades-program). This affects the reserved `/solar-battery/` section.
6. **Stale figures on official pages. Don't use these:** Energy Vic's ['Latest discounts and rebates'](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/households/latest-discounts-and-rebates) page (updated 24 Jul 2026) says ducted savings 'up to $1,410' a year (the product page says $1,140) and PV 'up to $2,800' (the PV page says $1,400). The [Solstice page](https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/supporting-solstice-customers) also says $2,800. Rule: product pages beat summary pages.
7. **Solar for Apartments is mid-change.** The news (26 Oct 2026) says buildings of up to 150 lots and no property value limit, but the [program page](https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/apartments) still says 3–50 lots.

[ESC legislation page](https://www.esc.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades-program/about-victorian-energy-upgrades-program/veu-program-legislation).

## Method notes for Indie Builder

- **postcodes.csv (14 Nov):** all 719 Table A postcodes. Columns: postcode, veu_area, reticulated_gas, veu_climatic_region, heat_pump_zone, localities (top 10 by dwellings), primary_lga, primary_lga_share_pct, other_lgas (with %), dwellings_2021, lga_locality_source, notes. Locality and council come from the [ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/standards/australian-statistical-geography-standard-asgs/edition-3-july-2021-june-2026/access-and-downloads/allocation-files): POA 2021 × SAL 2021 × LGA 2025 by mesh block, weighted by 2021 Census dwellings. 25 PO-box or campus postcodes have no dwellings; their locality is from the community dataset matthewproctor/australianpostcodes and their council is inferred from the same town's residential postcode, flagged 'indicative'. Metro hubs group postcodes by primary LGA into the [Plan Melbourne regions](https://planning.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0026/628361/plan-melbourne-map-metro-melbourne-regions.pdf).
- **VEU postcode table:** SPEC25's 'Location Variable List' (Table A) gives every postcode as Metropolitan/Regional, reticulated gas yes/no, climatic region Mild/Cold/Hot, and heat pump climatic zone 4/5. I parsed 719 rows from the v25.0 PDF. Area pages should quote this table for their postcodes, and the size guide can use it for 'cold area' notes.
- **NCC climate zones:** checked by point-in-polygon against the [ABCB climate zone shapefile](https://data.gov.au/data/dataset/australian-climate-zone-map) (CC BY 4.0, attribution required) at town-centre coordinates. Results: Ballarat 7, Yarra Ranges (Lilydale, Healesville, Olinda, Warburton) 7, Shepparton 4, Bendigo 6, and Melbourne, Geelong, Frankston, Mornington, Wyndham, Melton, Casey and Whittlesea all 6. Added 14 Nov: Diggers Rest, Sunbury, Craigieburn, Mernda, Caroline Springs, Werribee, Bacchus Marsh, Wallan, Kilmore, Seymour, Pakenham, Officer, Cranbourne, Traralgon, Wodonga and Warrnambool are 6; Gisborne, New Gisborne, Macedon, Riddells Creek, Woodend, Kyneton, Romsey and Lancefield are 7; Mildura is 4. On the page, tell readers to confirm by postcode on the [ABCB Climate Map](https://www.abcb.gov.au/abcb-climate-map).
- **Provider counts** (Energy Vic regional lists, 13 Nov 2026), hot water / heating-cooling: Barwon South West 30/50, Gippsland 19/38, Grampians 31/46, Greater Melbourne 79/116, Hume 27/60, Loddon Mallee 26/42. They change often; re-check monthly or drop the numbers.
- **Checker:** fully re-specified as a cascading Residential/Business form in `eligibility-checker-spec.md` (14 Nov). It adds insulation, induction, weather sealing, business products, Solar Homes and federal batteries, and is driven by postcodes.csv.
- energy.vic.gov.au is behind Cloudflare and rate-limits parallel fetches. `check-sources.mjs` should fetch one page at a time.
