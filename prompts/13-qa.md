### 2. Compliance wording (blocker)
- Every page shows the independence bar. `check-copy` passes.
- No page says or implies we're the government or part of VEU; no crest-like graphics.
- Consent text (rendered with a test installer) names the installer and its accredited provider, discloses the fee, separates phone/email/visit, states 3-month expiry and how to withdraw, links the privacy policy and `#your-rights`.
- `#your-rights` links the VEET scheme consumer factsheet and the ESC statement of rights.
- Checker never says "you qualify"/"you will get"; every result shows "indicative" + checked-on + source.
- Privacy policy, terms and about agree with each other on who gets data and how we're paid.

### 3. Functional
- `npx playwright test` against the preview (`BASE_URL=https://householdrebates.com.au`, `HTTP_CREDENTIALS` from env): every page 200, no console errors, no CSP violations, checker paths, form closed state (no inputs while `PUBLIC_LEADS_ENABLED=false`).

### 4. Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)
