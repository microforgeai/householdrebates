## Cold-climate ceiling insulation and heating upgrades in Ballarat

Long, frosty Central Highlands winters make thermal shell and space-heating upgrades the biggest energy savers in Ballarat:

- **Ceiling insulation (Regional Cold classification):** Because Ballarat's postcodes sit in VEU's **Regional** and **Cold** columns (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />), eligible uninsulated or under-R2.0 ceilings align with Energy Victoria's regional cold example of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-regional" /> (compared with <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-metro" /> in mild metropolitan postcodes) (<SourceLink id="energyvic-insulation" label="Energy Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts" />). Read mandatory electrical safety rules on our <a href="/victoria/ceiling-insulation-rebate/">ceiling insulation guide</a>.
- **Replacing ducted gas heating:** Whole-home ducted gas heaters are common across Ballarat houses—compare 10 kW, 16 kW, and 22.4 kW replacement scenarios and mandatory co-payments in <a href="/victoria/ducted-gas-to-ducted-reverse-cycle/">ducted gas to ducted reverse-cycle</a>.

## Heat pump hot water sizing and frost protection (Zone 5 vs Zone 4)

Seven of Ballarat's eight council postcodes (`3350`, `3352`, `3355`, `3356`, `3357`, plus PO-box postcodes `3353` and `3354` which have no reticulated gas) sit in **heat pump climatic zone 5** in VEU Table A, while **Winter Valley (`3358`)** sits in **zone 4** (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS 2021 postcode allocation" />). Energy Victoria advises households in cooler climates to look for **frost protection** when selecting a heat pump water heater (<SourceLink id="energyvic-existing-homes" label="Energy Victoria — Existing residential buildings" />)—use our <a href="/victoria/heat-pump-hot-water-size-guide/">heat pump hot water size guide</a> to check tank volume and zone notes for your household.

## City of Ballarat program and Grampians provider counts

The City of Ballarat launched its **Electrify Your Home** initiative in partnership with Geelong Sustainability's Electric Homes Program (<SourceLink id="ballarat-electrify" label="City of Ballarat — Electrify Your Home initiative" />; compare neighbouring <a href="/victoria/geelong/">Geelong</a>)—note that Electrify Your Home is an advice and partner-installer program rather than an open council cash grant.
