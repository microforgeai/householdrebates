## Solstice CNG shutdown support in Marong (3515) and Heathcote (3523)

Unlike urban Bendigo (3550, 3551, 3555, and 3556), Marong and Heathcote were supplied by Solstice Energy's compressed natural gas (CNG) networks—scheduled for decommissioning **after 2 August 2026 in Marong** and **after 16 August 2026 in Heathcote** (<SourceLink id="bendigo-solstice" label="City of Greater Bendigo — Support for Solstice Energy impacted customers" />).

Under Solar Victoria's Solstice support package (<SourceLink id="solarvic-solstice" label="Solar Victoria — Supporting Solstice customers" />; see all 10 towns on <a href="/victoria/solstice-gas-shutdown-towns/">Solstice gas shutdown towns</a>):

- **Waived Solar Homes rules:** No <Money id="vic-solar-homes-income-cap" /> household income cap, hot water systems of **any age** can be replaced, and **rental providers** can apply.
- **Top-up when VEU criteria are not met:** A <Money id="vic-solstice-topup" /> Solar Victoria top-up applies where a newer home (under 2 years old) or recent appliance cannot access VEU certificates.
- **City of Greater Bendigo Marong study:** In a Marong study published by the City of Greater Bendigo, switching to bottled LPG was estimated to carry **at least 2.5 times** the running cost of a partly or fully electric home, and partial electrification of a 4-bedroom Marong home was estimated to pay back in **about 3 to 4 years** compared with LPG (<SourceLink id="bendigo-solstice" label="City of Greater Bendigo — Solstice support and Marong study" />). Those figures reflect the council's Marong study rather than a statewide average.

## Council energy advice and Loddon Mallee VEU providers

Across the municipality, the City of Greater Bendigo operates its **Home Energy Empowerment Program**, offering **In-Home Energy Snapshots** and hands-on DIY efficiency workshops (<SourceLink id="bendigo-saving-energy" label="City of Greater Bendigo — Saving energy" />).

Because Bendigo postcodes sit in VEU's **Regional** and **Cold** climatic classification (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />), check cold-region examples on our <a href="/victoria/ceiling-insulation-rebate/">ceiling insulation guide</a>, <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">gas hot water to heat pump guide</a>, and <a href="/victoria/get-off-gas-planner/">Get-Off-Gas Planner</a>. Energy Victoria's **Loddon Mallee** regional lists show <Money id="vic-veu-providers-loddon-mallee-hw" /> accredited providers for hot water (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />) and <Money id="vic-veu-providers-loddon-mallee-hc" /> for heating and cooling (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />). Test your Bendigo postcode in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
