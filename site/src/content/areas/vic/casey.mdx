## City of Casey DIY draught-proofing rebate and home audit kits

Unlike most metropolitan councils, the City of Casey runs a direct household product rebate (<SourceLink id="casey-home-energy-rebates" label="City of Casey — Home energy rebates" />):

- **DIY draught-proofing rebate:** <Money id="casey-diy-draught-rebate" /> for eligible DIY draught-proofing materials, limited to **one claim per household** with itemised receipts dated **within 6 months**, lodged via the council's **rebately.co** portal (check the council page before buying, as rounds can pause once the municipal budget is exhausted).
- **What is excluded from the council rebate:** You **cannot** claim Casey's rebate for paying a tradesperson, nor for ceiling insulation batts, double glazing, or heating upgrades.
- **Home Audit Kit:** Residents can also hire an **Energy and Water Efficiency Home Audit Kit** from the council to find draughts around doors, skirting, and vents first.

## How Casey's DIY rebate fits with VEU discounts

Casey's municipal DIY product rebate and **Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU)** are separate programs:

- **VEU weather sealing (provider-installed):** When an accredited provider's installer fits approved weather-sealing products across a detached house, Energy Victoria lists an indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-sealing-house-5yr" /> for 5-year warranty products (<SourceLink id="energyvic-weather-sealing" label="Energy Victoria — Weather sealing discounts" />). Always read the mandatory carbon monoxide gas-heater safety rules first on our <a href="/victoria/draught-proofing-rebate/">draught proofing (weather sealing) guide</a>.
- **Ceiling insulation and heating swaps:** Because Casey postcodes (`3805`, `3806`, `3976`, `3977`, `3978`) sit in VEU's **Metropolitan / Mild** classification like <a href="/victoria/melbourne/">Melbourne</a> (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />), compare metropolitan examples on our <a href="/victoria/ceiling-insulation-rebate/">ceiling insulation guide</a> and <a href="/victoria/gas-room-heater-to-split-system/">gas room heater to split system guide</a>, or check your home in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
