## City of Casey DIY draught-proofing rebate and home audit kits

Unlike most metropolitan councils, the City of Casey runs a direct household product rebate (<SourceLink id="casey-home-energy-rebates" label="City of Casey — Home energy rebates" />):

- **DIY draught-proofing rebate:** <Money id="casey-diy-draught-rebate" /> for eligible DIY draught-proofing materials, limited to **one claim per household** with itemised receipts dated **within 6 months**, lodged via the council's **rebately.co** portal (check the council page before buying, as rounds can pause once the municipal budget is exhausted).
- **What is excluded from the council rebate:** You **cannot** claim Casey's rebate for paying a tradesperson, nor for ceiling insulation batts, double glazing, or heating upgrades.
- **Home Audit Kit:** Residents can also hire an **Energy and Water Efficiency Home Audit Kit** from the council to find draughts around doors, skirting, and vents first.

## How Casey's DIY rebate fits with VEU discounts

Casey's municipal DIY product rebate and **Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU)** are separate programs:

- **VEU weather sealing (provider-installed):** When an accredited provider's installer fits approved weather-sealing products, Energy Victoria's indicative discount depends on your home type (apartment, townhouse, or house) and whether the products carry at least a 2-year or 5-year warranty (<SourceLink id="energyvic-weather-sealing" label="Energy Victoria — Weather sealing discounts" />). Homes with an open-flued gas space heater, an open-flued ducted heater in a cupboard, an indoor open-flued gas water heater, or a flueless space heater cannot claim VEU weather sealing due to carbon monoxide risk—the provider checks your appliances first. Read the full rules on our <a href="/victoria/draught-proofing-rebate/">draught proofing (weather sealing) guide</a>.
