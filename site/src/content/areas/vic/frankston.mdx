## Frankston postcodes, council boundaries, and local guidance

Under Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) 2021 Census dwelling allocations (<SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files" />), all five postcodes whose primary council is Frankston City sit **100% within Frankston City Council**: `3198` (Seaford), `3199` (Frankston and Frankston South), `3200` (Frankston North), `3201` (Carrum Downs), and `3910` (Langwarrin). If your address is in `3911` (Baxter or Langwarrin South, where 70% of dwellings sit in Mornington Peninsula Shire and 30% in Frankston City), see our <a href="/victoria/mornington-peninsula/">Mornington Peninsula rebate guide</a>—its VEU Table A settings are identical (Metropolitan, reticulated gas, Mild climatic region, heat pump zone 4).

Frankston City Council's <SourceLink id="frankston-climate-action" label="Saving Energy household guidance" /> encourages residents across these suburbs to improve home thermal performance and transition away from ageing fossil gas appliances using state-wide VEU discounts.

## Air conditioner rebates for Frankston, Seaford, and Langwarrin

In Frankston (`3199`), Seaford (`3198`), Frankston North (`3200`), Carrum Downs (`3201`), and Langwarrin (`3910`), many brick-veneer family homes run gas wall furnaces or ducted gas heaters installed between the 1970s and 2000s. Under Energy Victoria's household heating and cooling schedule (<a href="/victoria/air-conditioner-rebate/">see all split and ducted capacities</a>):

- **Room gas wall furnace or space heater swapped to one 3–9 kW split system:** Energy Victoria lists <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" />.
- **Ducted gas unit swapped to a 16 kW ducted reverse-cycle unit:** Energy Victoria lists <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-16kw" />.
- **Ducted gas unit swapped to a 22.4 kW ducted reverse-cycle unit:** Energy Victoria lists <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" />.

Engage a VEU accredited provider **before** any plumber or electrician disconnects your existing heater so pre-upgrade photos and compliance paperwork are captured first.

## Heat pump hot water upgrades across Frankston City

For bayside and inland Frankston homes built more than two years ago, swapping an ageing storage or instantaneous hot water cylinder to an approved VEU heat pump (with at least a 5-year household warranty) unlocks point-of-sale discounts alongside a mandatory <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> minimum customer co-contribution:

- **Old electric storage tank to heat pump:** Energy Victoria lists <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" />.
- **Old mains gas water heater to heat pump:** Energy Victoria lists <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> (see stacking rules on our <a href="/victoria/heat-pump-rebate/">heat pump hot water rebate guide</a> and replacement timing in our <a href="/victoria/gas-to-electric-hot-water-2027/">gas-to-electric hot water rule guide</a>).
