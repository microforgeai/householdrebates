## Air conditioner rebates for Frankston, Seaford, and Langwarrin

In Frankston (3199), Seaford (3198), Frankston North (3200), and Langwarrin (3910), many family homes have ageing gas space heaters or ducted gas units installed in the 1980s–2000s. Under Energy Victoria's VEU heating and cooling schedule:

- **Single split system (3–9 kW) replacing a room gas heater:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" />.
- **16 kW ducted reverse-cycle replacing a ducted gas heater:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-16kw" />.
- **22.4 kW ducted reverse-cycle replacing a ducted gas heater:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" />.

Remember that your installer must work with a VEU accredited provider **before** disconnecting your old gas heater so pre-installation photos and compliance evidence can be recorded.

## Heat pump hot water discounts in Frankston

When replacing an existing hot water unit in a building at least two years old:

- **Replacing an inefficient electric water heater with a heat pump:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" />.
- **Replacing an inefficient mains gas water heater with a heat pump:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" />.
- **Minimum customer co-contribution:** <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" />.

From **1 March 2027**, existing mains gas water heaters that reach the end of their life in Frankston homes must generally be replaced with an efficient electric system. Read the full details in our <a href="/victoria/gas-to-electric-hot-water-2027/">1 March 2027 Gas Hot Water Rule guide</a>.
