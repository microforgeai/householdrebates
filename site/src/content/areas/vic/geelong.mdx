## Reverse-cycle air conditioning discounts in Greater Geelong

Many established homes across Geelong West, Newtown, Belmont, Highton, and Corio rely on ageing ducted gas or room gas heaters. Under Energy Victoria's household heating and cooling scenarios:

- **Replacing a room gas heater with a single split system (3–9 kW):** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" />.
- **Replacing a ducted gas heater with a multi-split system:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-multisplit" />.
- **Replacing a ducted gas heater with a 22.4 kW ducted reverse-cycle system:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" /> (smaller 10 kW and 16 kW ducted systems have lower indicative amounts; see our <a href="/victoria/air-conditioner-rebate/">Air Conditioner Rebate Victoria guide</a>).

New growth-corridor estates in Armstrong Creek or Lara must check their occupancy date: if you are replacing an existing heater, the home must be **more than 2 years old** to qualify for a VEU replacement discount.

## Hot water upgrades in Geelong (mains gas vs electric vs LPG)

For Geelong homes at least two years old replacing an existing hot water unit:

- **Replacing an inefficient electric storage unit with a heat pump:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" />.
- **Replacing an inefficient mains gas unit with a heat pump:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" />.
- **Minimum customer co-contribution:** <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" />.

In VEU Specifications v25.0 Table A, **13 of Greater Geelong's 14 postcodes** are listed as having reticulated gas, while **postcode `3213` (Lovely Banks, Batesford, Anakie, and Moorabool — 83% Greater Geelong and 17% Golden Plains by 2021 Census dwellings)** is the single Greater Geelong postcode listed with **no reticulated gas** (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS 2021 postcode allocation" />). If your property is on reticulated mains gas, remember that from **1 March 2027** a mains gas water heater needing replacement must generally switch to an electric unit (<a href="/victoria/gas-to-electric-hot-water-2027/">see the 1 March 2027 rule</a>). Properties in `3213` or outer Bellarine fringes using **bottled LPG** are not affected by the 2027 reticulated gas replacement rule (<SourceLink id="vicplanning-gas-roadmap" label="Victoria's Gas Substitution Roadmap" />).

## Ceiling insulation discounts in Geelong

Greater Geelong's 14 council postcodes (`3212`–`3227`, excluding `3221` in Surf Coast and `3225` in Queenscliffe; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS 2021 postcode allocation" />) are all classified in VEU Specifications v25.0 Table A as **Regional** with a **Mild** climatic region (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />). Because Energy Victoria only publishes ceiling insulation examples for Metropolitan + Mild and Regional + Cold postcodes, neither published example fits Geelong directly.

Applying the VEU Specifications v25.0 Part 48 calculation (Mild greenhouse gas savings coefficient `0.00576` × 150 m² × 25 × Regional factor `1.04`, scaled to Energy Victoria's published examples) gives an indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-est-regional-mild" /> for an uninsulated or under-insulated 150 m² Greater Geelong ceiling—**the accredited provider confirms** the exact discount in your quote after checking your ceiling area and existing R-value. Read our full <a href="/victoria/ceiling-insulation-rebate/">ceiling insulation rebate guide</a> for safety checks and eligibility rules.

  Energy Victoria's two published household ceiling insulation examples for a 150 m² home are <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-metro" /> in metropolitan Melbourne (Mild climate region) and <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-regional" /> in regional Victoria (Cold climate region) (<SourceLink id="energyvic-insulation" label="Energy Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts" />). ## Electric Homes Program 2026–27 across the Geelong region

Alongside state-wide VEU discounts, Geelong Sustainability runs the **Electric Homes Program 2026–27** for households in Greater Geelong, Surf Coast, Golden Plains, Colac Otway, and Queenscliffe, with expressions of interest open from **5 October 2026** (<SourceLink id="geelong-electric-homes-launch" label="Geelong Sustainability — Electric Homes Program 2026–27" />).
