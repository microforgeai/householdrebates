## SEC's heat pump hot water pilot in Latrobe City

Latrobe City is one of the Victorian municipalities included in the state-owned **SEC heat pump hot water one-stop-shop pilot**, covering **Morwell (`3840`)**, **Moe and Newborough (`3825`)**, **Traralgon (`3844`)**, and **Churchill (`3842`)** (<SourceLink id="premier-latrobe-pilot" label="Premier of Victoria — Latrobe Valley households media release" />; <SourceLink id="sec-latrobe-pilot" label="SEC Victoria — Latrobe City heat pump pilot" />).

- **How the SEC pilot works:** Households enter home details in SEC's online planner to compare vetted heat pump models and request quotes from SEC-endorsed installers, with state rebates applied upfront. Because SEC is a state-owned installer-matching service, you can compare an SEC quote directly against independent registered providers—see <a href="/victoria/find-an-accredited-provider/">how to find a genuine VEU accredited provider</a>.
- **Stacking VEU and Solar Homes hot water support:** Replacing an existing mains gas water heater in a home more than two years old lists an indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> (<Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> minimum contribution), while eligible owner-occupiers can also apply for Solar Victoria's hot water rebate of <Money id="vic-solar-homes-hw-rebate" />—or <Money id="vic-solar-homes-hw-rebate-local" /> for an eligible locally made system (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />; <SourceLink id="solarvic-hot-water-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Hot water rebate" />). Compare eligibility steps in <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">gas hot water to heat pump</a>.
- **Zone 5 in Moe (`3825`) vs zone 4 elsewhere:** In VEU Table A, **Moe, Newborough, and Yallourn North (`3825`, 90% Latrobe, 10% Baw Baw)** sit in **heat pump zone 5**, whereas **Morwell (`3840`)**, **Churchill (`3842`)**, **Traralgon (`3844`)**, and the rural postcodes sit in **zone 4** (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />).

## Regional + Mild ceiling insulation and space heating

Every one of Latrobe City's **9 postcodes** is classified as **Regional** and **Mild** in VEU Specifications v25.0 Table A (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files" />).

Neither of Energy Victoria's two published 150 m² ceiling insulation examples fits directly (one assumes metro + mild, the other regional + cold). Scaling VEU Part 48's **Mild** climate factor (`0.00576`) and **Regional** multiplier (`1.04`) gives our labelled estimate of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-est-regional-mild" /> for an uninsulated or under-R2.0 150 m² ceiling (minimum <Money id="vic-veu-copay-insulation" /> customer co-payment)—**the accredited provider confirms** the exact quote after inspecting your ceiling (<SourceLink id="energyvic-insulation" label="Energy Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts" />).

For gas space-heating replacements in homes older than two years, Energy Victoria lists indicative VEU discounts of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> (room gas heater to single split system) and <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-16kw" /> (ducted gas to 16 kW ducted reverse-cycle, with a <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" /> minimum co-payment from 30 September 2026; <SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />). Eligible cardholders switching from gas to electricity should also check how the year-round Annual Electricity Concession compares with the six-month Winter Gas Concession on <a href="/victoria/concession-card-holders/">concession card holders in Victoria</a>.

## Off-gas towns and the completed Morwell Electri-fair-cation trial

Five Latrobe postcodes—**Gippsland Mail Centre (`3841`, PO boxes)**, **Glengarry (`3854`, 84% Latrobe, 16% Wellington)**, **Toongabbie (`3856`, 92% Latrobe, 8% Wellington)**, **Yinnar (`3869`)**, and **Boolarra (`3870`)**—are listed with **no reticulated gas** in Table A (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />):

- **Off-gas hot water:** Replacing an inefficient electric storage water heater lists an indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" />, while bottled LPG water heaters fall under VEU Part 3 where **the accredited provider confirms** the certificate discount (<LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/lpg-homes/">see bottled LPG homes</LinkIfEnabled>).
- **Morwell Electri-fair-cation trial (completed 2026):** Between 2024 and 2026, Gippsland Climate Change Network (GCCN), AusNet's Innovation Advisory Committee, and Morwell Neighbourhood House delivered the **AusNet–GCCN Electri-fair-cation Project**, upgrading participating Morwell homes in street clusters with electric cooking, reverse-cycle heating, hot water, solar, insulation, and draught proofing (<SourceLink id="gccn-energise-gippsland" label="GCCN — Energise Gippsland Electri-fair-cation Project" />). With that cluster trial completed in 2026, Latrobe Valley households now plan upgrades through ongoing state discounts—map your order in the <a href="/victoria/get-off-gas-planner/">Get-Off-Gas Planner</a> or check <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3844">Traralgon 3844</a>, <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3840">Morwell 3840</a>, or <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3825">Moe 3825 in the Victoria Eligibility Checker</a>.
