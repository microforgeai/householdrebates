## Cold-climate ceiling insulation: Regional vs Metropolitan postcodes

Unlike inner Melbourne, **12 of Macedon Ranges Shire's 13 postcodes** carry the **Cold** climatic classification in VEU Specifications v25.0 Table A (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />). Because VEU Activity 48 multiplies the climate factor by a separate **Metropolitan (`0.98`)** or **Regional (`1.04`)** factor, two neighbouring Macedon Ranges towns can see slightly different indicative insulation discounts on an uninsulated or under-R2.0 150 m² roof (minimum <Money id="vic-veu-copay-insulation" /> customer co-payment; <SourceLink id="energyvic-insulation" label="Energy Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts" />):

- **Regional + Cold (`3435` Lancefield, `3437` Gisborne, `3440` Macedon, `3442` Woodend, `3444` Kyneton, `3446` Malmsbury):** Aligns directly with Energy Victoria's published regional cold-climate example of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-regional" />.
- **Metropolitan + Cold (`3431` Riddells Creek, `3432` Bolinda, `3433` Monegeetta, `3434` Romsey, `3438` New Gisborne, `3441` Mount Macedon):** Combines VEU Part 48's Cold factor (`0.00771`) with the Metropolitan regional factor (`0.98`), giving our scaled estimate of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-est-metro-cold" />—**the accredited provider confirms** the final quote after measuring ceiling area and R-values.
- **Metropolitan + Mild (`3430` Clarkefield):** The single Mild postcode in the shire (94% Macedon Ranges, 6% Hume; also covered on our <a href="/victoria/north-west-corridor/">North-West Corridor guide</a>) aligns with Energy Victoria's metro-mild example of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-metro" />.

Check pre-installation electrical safety rules and downlight clearances in our <a href="/victoria/ceiling-insulation-rebate/">ceiling insulation rebate guide</a>.

## Heat pump hot water zones (Zone 5 vs Zone 4) and winter heating

VEU Table A splits Macedon Ranges across two water-heating zones, and calculates certificate numbers using the postcode's assigned zone (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />):

- **Heat pump zone 5 (`3430` to `3438`):** Covers Clarkefield, Riddells Creek, Bolinda, Monegeetta, Romsey, Lancefield, Gisborne, and New Gisborne.
- **Heat pump zone 4 (`3440` to `3446`):** Covers Macedon, Mount Macedon, Woodend, Kyneton, and Malmsbury.

Across cool-temperate zone 7 winters, Energy Victoria recommends frost-protected heat pump models for cooler parts of Victoria (<SourceLink id="energyvic-existing-homes" label="Energy Victoria residential guidance" />). Review tank volume and cold-weather recovery before ordering via the <a href="/victoria/heat-pump-hot-water-size-guide/">Victoria heat pump sizing tool</a>.

For homes more than two years old on reticulated gas, Energy Victoria's indicative VEU replacement discounts include <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> for gas-to-heat-pump hot water (<Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> minimum contribution), <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> for replacing a room gas heater with a single split system, and <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-16kw" /> (16 kW) or <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" /> (22.4 kW) when replacing ducted gas heating with ducted reverse-cycle (with a <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" /> minimum co-payment from 30 September 2026; <SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />).

## Off-gas towns and bottled LPG properties

Four postcodes in the shire—**Clarkefield (`3430`)**, **Bolinda (`3432`)**, **Monegeetta (`3433`)**, and **Malmsbury (`3446`, 79% Macedon Ranges, 19% Hepburn, 2% Mount Alexander)**—have **no reticulated mains gas** in Table A, alongside rural acreages outside town gas mains (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files" />):

- **Electric storage hot water to heat pump:** Lists an indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" /> (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />).
- **Bottled LPG water heaters:** Eligible under VEU Part 3, though Energy Victoria does not publish a separate LPG dollar example—**the accredited provider confirms** the certificate discount (<LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/lpg-homes/">see our bottled LPG homes guide</LinkIfEnabled>).
- **Stepping off gas completely:** Compare appliance order and meter abolishment on our <a href="/victoria/get-off-gas-planner/">Get-Off-Gas Planner</a>, or test your postcode directly—for example <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3442">Woodend 3442</a>, <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3444">Kyneton 3444</a>, or <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3437">Gisborne 3437 in the Victoria Eligibility Checker</a>.

## Council resources and community bulk-buy status

Macedon Ranges Shire Council provides advisory and community electrification support rather than a council-funded cash rebate (<SourceLink id="mrsc-take-climate-action" label="Macedon Ranges Shire Council — Take climate action" />):

- **Library kits and webinars:** Residents can borrow **Sustainable Home Kits** from local libraries to check draughts and appliance energy draw, join **Empower Your Home** webinars, and follow local **Cool Changes** community climate action plans across Macedon and Mount Macedon, Kyneton, Gisborne, Riddells Creek, Romsey and Lancefield, Woodend, and Malmsbury (alongside home electrification sessions held during the April 2026 **ChangeMakers** festival and **Riddells Creek Enviro-Energy Expo**).
- **Community heat pump bulk buy (closed):** Transition to Zero's **Macedon Ranges Heat Pump Hot Water Bulk Buy** closed to new enquiries on **31 October 2026**, with installations for existing bookings finishing on **30 November 2026** (<SourceLink id="t2zero-macedon-bulk-buy" label="Transition to Zero — Macedon Ranges bulk buy" />). Households starting quotes now can use state-wide VEU and Solar Homes discounts via the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Eligibility Checker</a>.
