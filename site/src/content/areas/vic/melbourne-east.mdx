## How VEU settings divide across the six eastern councils

Plan Melbourne's Eastern metropolitan region groups **Manningham** (9 primary postcodes: `3105`–`3115`, from Bulleen, Templestowe, and Doncaster out to Warrandyte `3113` and Wonga Park `3115`), **Whitehorse** (9 primary postcodes: `3125`, `3127`–`3133`, and `3151`, from Burwood and Box Hill through Blackburn, Mitcham, Vermont, and Burwood East), **Monash** (9 primary postcodes: `3147`–`3150`, `3166`–`3170`, and university PO-box postcode `3800`, covering Ashwood, Mount Waverley, Glen Waverley, Wheelers Hill, Oakleigh, Clayton, and Mulgrave), **Maroondah** (3 primary postcodes: `3134` Ringwood, `3135` Heathmont and Ringwood East, and `3136` Croydon), **Knox** (9 primary postcodes: `3152` Wantirna, `3153` Bayswater, `3154` The Basin, `3155` Boronia, `3156` Ferntree Gully and Lysterfield, commercial PO-box postcode `3176` Scoresby BC, `3178` Rowville, `3179` Scoresby, and `3180` Knoxfield), and **Yarra Ranges** (26 primary postcodes) (<SourceLink id="plan-melbourne-regions-map" label="Plan Melbourne — Metropolitan regions map" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files" />).

Every one of those **65 eastern postcodes** is listed with **reticulated gas** in VEU Specifications v25.0 Table A (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />), yet eastern Melbourne contains the widest internal climate split of Greater Melbourne's five regions:

- **50 Metropolitan + Mild postcodes:** All 39 postcodes whose primary council is Manningham, Whitehorse, Monash, Maroondah, or Knox, plus 11 foothill and valley postcodes inside <a href="/victoria/yarra-ranges/">Yarra Ranges</a> (`3116` Chirnside Park, `3137` Kilsyth, `3138` Mooroolbark, `3139` Wandin North and Seville, `3140` Lilydale, `3158` Upwey, `3159` Menzies Creek and Selby, `3160` Belgrave and Tecoma, `3765` Montrose, `3791` Kallista, and `3797` Gilderoy, Gladysdale, Powelltown, Three Bridges, and Yarra Junction).
- **14 Metropolitan + Cold postcodes:** Kalorama (`3766`), Mount Dandenong (`3767`), Coldstream (`3770`), Yarra Glen (`3775`), Healesville (`3777`), Tremont (`3785`), Ferny Creek (`3786`), Sassafras (`3787`), Olinda (`3788`), Sherbrooke (`3789`), The Patch (`3792`), Monbulk (`3793`), Silvan (`3795`), and Mount Evelyn (`3796`).
- **1 Regional + Cold postcode:** Warburton (`3799`, including East Warburton, Millgrove, Reefton, and McMahons Creek) is the lone **Regional** entry in the eastern hub.
- **7 hot water heat pump zone 5 postcodes:** Wandin North and Seville (`3139`), Lilydale (`3140`), Upwey (`3158`), Belgrave and Tecoma (`3160`), Coldstream (`3770`), Yarra Glen (`3775`), and Healesville (`3777`). Notice that `3139`, `3140`, `3158`, and `3160` remain **Mild** for general VEU climate region while moving into **zone 5** for hot water heat pumps—you can inspect any boundary postcode in the <a href="/victoria/postcode/">VEU postcode lookup</a>.

Several eastern postcodes also straddle council borders in ABS dwelling counts: `3147` (Ashburton and Ashwood) splits 50% Monash and 50% Boroondara; `3127` (Mont Albert and Surrey Hills) splits 55% Whitehorse and 45% Boroondara; `3153` (Bayswater) splits 59% Knox and 41% Maroondah; `3167` (Oakleigh South) splits 60% Monash and 40% Kingston; `3113` (Warrandyte) splits 65% Manningham and 35% Nillumbik; and `3137` (Kilsyth) splits 71% Yarra Ranges and 29% Maroondah. Check your council rates notice before applying to a municipality-specific program.

## Ceiling insulation, ducted heating, and cold-climate hot water sizing

Those Table A differences directly alter <a href="/victoria/ceiling-insulation-rebate/">ceiling insulation discounts</a> on an uninsulated 150 m² ceiling (<SourceLink id="energyvic-insulation" label="Energy Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts" />; customer co-payment <Money id="vic-veu-copay-insulation" />):

- **50 Metropolitan + Mild suburbs:** Energy Victoria's metro-mild example of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-metro" />.
- **14 Metropolitan + Cold Dandenongs and valley postcodes:** <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-est-metro-cold" />, derived from VEU Part 48 Cold and Metropolitan multipliers (confirmed on site by the accredited provider).
- **Warburton (`3799`, Regional + Cold):** Energy Victoria's regional-cold example of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-regional" />.

Established homes older than two years across Doncaster, Blackburn, Glen Waverley, Ringwood, Wantirna, and Croydon commonly decommission ageing gas heaters: Energy Victoria lists <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> when replacing a room gas heater with one 3–9 kW split system, or <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" /> on a 22.4 kW ducted reverse-cycle replacement alongside the mandatory <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" /> ducted co-payment (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />; <SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 Part 6" />). When swapping mains gas hot water to a heat pump (<Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> indicative VEU discount; <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> minimum customer contribution; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />), households in the **7 zone 5 postcodes** and **NCC climate zone 7** hills towns should check winter ambient performance and frost-protection specs in our <a href="/victoria/heat-pump-hot-water-size-guide/">heat pump hot water size guide</a>.

## Council Home Energy Program and the SEC heat pump pilot in the east

Four eastern municipalities—**Knox**, **Maroondah**, **Monash**, and **Yarra Ranges**—participate in the **Council Home Energy Program** for independent upgrade advice and vetted supplier quotes (<SourceLink id="council-home-energy-program" label="Council Home Energy Program" />). Residents in **Maroondah** and **Yarra Ranges** (including Bayswater North, Ringwood, Croydon, Kilsyth, Lilydale, and the Dandenongs where their rates go to either council) can also use **SEC Victoria's heat pump hot water pilot** (<SourceLink id="premier-latrobe-pilot" label="Premier of Victoria — SEC heat pump pilot release" />). Households in **Manningham** and **Whitehorse** claim the same state VEU discounts and Solar Homes rebates directly—run your postcode and existing appliances through the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Eligibility Checker</a>.
