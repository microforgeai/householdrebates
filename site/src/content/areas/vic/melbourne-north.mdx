## What differs from inner Melbourne in the northern region

Plan Melbourne's Northern metropolitan region brings together **Merri-bek** (7 primary postcodes: `3044`, `3046`, `3055`–`3058`, `3060`, from Brunswick and Coburg to Pascoe Vale, Glenroy, and Fawkner), **Darebin** (6 primary postcodes: `3070`–`3073`, `3078`, and university PO-box postcode `3086`, covering Northcote, Thornbury, Preston, Reservoir, and Alphington), **Banyule** (8 primary postcodes: `3079`, `3081`, `3084`, `3085`, `3087`, `3088`, `3093`, `3094`, from Ivanhoe and Heidelberg through Macleod, Greensborough, Lower Plenty, and Montmorency), **Nillumbik** (10 primary postcodes: `3089`–`3091`, `3095`–`3097`, `3099`, `3759`–`3761`, from Diamond Creek, Plenty, and Eltham out to Hurstbridge, Panton Hill, Smiths Gully, and St Andrews), **Hume** (12 primary postcodes: `3043`, `3045`, `3047`–`3049`, `3059`, `3061`–`3064`, `3428`, `3429`), **Whittlesea** (11 primary postcodes: `3074`–`3076`, `3082`, `3083`, `3750`–`3752`, `3754`, `3755`, `3757`), and **Mitchell** (12 primary postcodes: `3521`, `3522`, `3658`–`3662`, `3753`, `3756`, `3758`, `3762`, `3764`) (<SourceLink id="plan-melbourne-regions-map" label="Plan Melbourne — Metropolitan regions map" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files" />).

In VEU Specifications v25.0 Table A (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />), **57 of those 66 northern postcodes** share the same row as inner <a href="/victoria/melbourne/">Melbourne</a> (**Metropolitan**, **reticulated gas**, **Mild**, **zone 4**—every northern postcode uses hot water zone 4), while three northern fringe groups diverge:

- **Eight Regional + Cold postcodes (all in Mitchell Shire):** `3521` (Pyalong), `3522` (Tooborac), `3658` (Broadford), `3659` (Tallarook), `3660` (Seymour), `3661` (Seymour PO boxes), `3662` (Puckapunyal), and `3764` (Kilmore). Read town-by-town guidance on our <a href="/victoria/mitchell/">Mitchell Shire rebate guide</a>.
- **One Metropolitan + Cold postcode:** `3762` (Bylands) stays **Metropolitan** while shifting into the **Cold** column, making **9 Cold postcodes** across the northern hub.
- **Six postcodes without reticulated mains gas:** `3521`, `3522`, `3661`, `3662`, `3758` (Wandong and Heathcote Junction), and `3762` (Bylands) are marked `No` for reticulated gas in Table A. By contrast, semi-rural Nillumbik postcodes such as Kangaroo Ground (`3097`), Arthurs Creek and Cottles Bridge (`3099`), and St Andrews (`3761`) remain marked `Yes` at the postcode level even where rural properties run on bottled LPG or firewood.

Several northern postcodes also cross council boundaries in ABS dwelling counts: `3083` (Bundoora and Kingsbury) splits three ways across Whittlesea (43%), Banyule (30%), and Darebin (27%); `3078` (Alphington and Fairfield) splits 74% Darebin and 26% Yarra; `3043` (Gladstone Park, Gowanbrae, and Tullamarine) splits 84% Hume and 16% Merri-bek; `3085` (Macleod and Yallambie) splits 83% Banyule and 17% Darebin; `3088` (Briar Hill, Greensborough, and St Helena) splits 83% Banyule and 17% Nillumbik; and `3757` (Whittlesea, Humevale, and Kinglake West) splits 79% Whittlesea and 21% Murrindindi. For growth estates in **Craigieburn** and **Mickleham** (`3064`), **Sunbury** (`3429`), **Bulla** (`3428`), **Wollert** (`3750`), **Mernda** (`3754`), **Beveridge** (`3753`), and **Wallan** (`3756`), remember that VEU heater and hot water replacements require a building **more than two years old** (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />); see Sunbury and Bulla detail on our <a href="/victoria/north-west-corridor/">North-West Corridor guide</a> or check any postcode in the <a href="/victoria/postcode/">VEU postcode tool</a>.

## Ceiling insulation, heating, and hot water figures across the north

Because ceiling insulation certificate calculations combine **VEU area** and **climatic region**, three 150 m² ceiling insulation figures apply across the northern hub (<SourceLink id="energyvic-insulation" label="Energy Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts" />; <SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 Part 48" />):

- **57 Metropolitan + Mild postcodes** (Merri-bek, Darebin, Banyule, Nillumbik, Hume, <a href="/victoria/whittlesea/">City of Whittlesea</a>, plus Beveridge `3753`, Wallan `3756`, and Wandong `3758`): Energy Victoria's metro-mild example of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-metro" />.
- **8 Regional + Cold postcodes in Mitchell** (`3521`, `3522`, `3658`–`3662`, `3764`): Energy Victoria's regional-cold example of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-regional" />.
- **1 Metropolitan + Cold postcode** (`3762` Bylands): <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-est-metro-cold" />, scaled from Part 48 Cold and Metropolitan factors (confirmed by your accredited provider; customer co-payment <Money id="vic-veu-copay-insulation" />).

In established northern homes over two years old, Energy Victoria lists indicative VEU discounts of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> when replacing a room gas heater with one 3–9 kW split system, <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> when replacing mains gas hot water with a heat pump, and <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" /> when replacing electric storage hot water (minimum hot water customer contribution <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />).

## Active and paused council programs in Melbourne's north

Council-backed upgrade schemes vary sharply across municipal boundaries in the north, especially following Yarra Energy Foundation's voluntary administration in November 2026:

- **Whittlesea and Merri-bek (active):** Both councils participate in the **Council Home Energy Program** (<SourceLink id="council-home-energy-program" label="Council Home Energy Program" />). On its Whittlesea page, the program offers resident-funded **"free home energy assessments"** until **June 2027** or until budget runs out (one per property, excluding homes assessed in the past five years; <SourceLink id="whittlesea-home-energy-program" label="Home Energy Program — City of Whittlesea" />). Merri-bek households also have access to the state-owned **SEC installer network** (<SourceLink id="premier-latrobe-pilot" label="Premier of Victoria — SEC pilot and installer network release" />).
- **Mitchell Shire (active):** Covered by **Goulburn Murray Solar Savers**, connecting residents with vetted installers for solar PV, batteries, heat pump hot water, split systems, insulation, and draught proofing (<SourceLink id="gmca-solar-savers" label="Goulburn Murray Climate Alliance — Solar Savers" />).
- **Hume and Darebin (paused upgrade schemes):** **Hume Home Energy Upgrades** paused new solar and electrification registrations after Yarra Energy Foundation entered voluntary administration; only insulation and draught-proofing registrations submitted before **19 October 2026** continue via Enviroflex (<SourceLink id="hume-energy-upgrades" label="Hume Home Energy Upgrades pause notice" />). Hume residents seeking bill-relief advice can still contact Hume City Council's **Household Energy Officer** (<SourceLink id="hume-energy-bill-support" label="Hume City Council — Help with energy bills" />). **Darebin Energy Upgrades** likewise paused registrations while Darebin City Council reviews delivery arrangements (<SourceLink id="darebin-energy-upgrades" label="Darebin Energy Upgrades special message" />).
- **Banyule and Nillumbik:** Neither council currently runs a separate municipal rebate on our verified source list; residents access state-wide VEU discounts and Solar Homes rebates directly, which you can check for your postcode in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Eligibility Checker</a>.
