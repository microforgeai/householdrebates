## South-east insulation and Table A exceptions

Plan Melbourne's Southern metropolitan boundary spans **Kingston**, **Greater Dandenong**, **City of Casey**, **Cardinia Shire**, **Frankston City**, and **Mornington Peninsula Shire** (<SourceLink id="plan-melbourne-regions-map" label="Plan Melbourne — Metropolitan regions map" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files" />). Cardinia Shire does not have a standalone council page—all **12 Cardinia-primary postcodes** (`3781` Cockatoo, `3782` Avonsleigh, Clematis, Emerald, and Macclesfield, `3783` Gembrook, `3807` Beaconsfield and Guys Hill, `3808` Beaconsfield Upper and Dewhurst, `3809` Officer and Officer South, `3810` Pakenham, Pakenham South, and Pakenham Upper, `3812` Maryknoll, Mount Burnett, and Nar Nar Goon, `3813` Tynong, `3814` Cora Lynn and Garfield, `3815` Bunyip, Iona, and Tonimbuk, and `3981` Koo Wee Rup) sit directly in the 67-row table above.

Every south-east postcode has **reticulated gas** and sits in **heat pump zone 4** under VEU Specifications v25.0 Table A (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />), with only two Western Port swamp-fringe postcodes altering ceiling insulation calculations (<SourceLink id="energyvic-insulation" label="Energy Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts" />; customer co-payment <Money id="vic-veu-copay-insulation" />):

- **65 Metropolitan + Mild postcodes** (from Clarinda `3169`, Springvale `3171`, and Dandenong `3175` across Berwick `3806`, Officer `3809`, Pakenham `3810`, Cranbourne `3977`, Frankston `3199`, Mornington `3931`, and Portsea `3944`): Energy Victoria's metro-mild 150 m² ceiling example of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-metro" />.
- **Tooradin (`3980`, Metropolitan + Cold, covering Blind Bight, Tooradin, and Warneet in Casey):** <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-est-metro-cold" />, scaled from Part 48 Cold and Metropolitan multipliers (the accredited provider confirms your exact quote).
- **Koo Wee Rup (`3981`, Regional + Cold, covering Bayles, Catani, Dalmore, Heath Hill, Koo Wee Rup, and Yannathan):** the south-east's lone **Regional** entry, matching Energy Victoria's regional-cold 150 m² ceiling example of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-regional" />.

## How the 67 south-east postcodes divide across six councils

Because municipal program rules stop at council borders while Australia Post boundaries often cross them, check the **Council share** column if you live near a shire boundary (<SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 dwelling allocations" />):

- **Kingston (8 primary postcodes: `3169`, `3189`, `3192`, `3194`–`3197`, `3202`) and Greater Dandenong (6 primary postcodes: `3164`, `3171`–`3175`):** `3172` (Dingley Village and Springvale South) splits almost evenly—51% Greater Dandenong and 49% Kingston—while `3192` (Cheltenham) sits 84% in Kingston and 16% in Bayside, and industrial PO-box postcode `3164` (Dandenong South) records zero census dwellings.
- **Casey (11 primary postcodes: `3177`, `3802`–`3806`, `3975`–`3978`, `3980`) and Cardinia (12 primary postcodes):** Cross-border postcodes include `3804` Narre Warren North (94% Casey, 6% Yarra Ranges), `3807` Beaconsfield (93% Cardinia, 7% Casey), `3782` Emerald (85% Cardinia, 15% Yarra Ranges), `3977` Cranbourne and Skye (88% Casey, 12% Frankston), and `3981` Koo Wee Rup (98% Cardinia, 2% Baw Baw).
- **Frankston (5 primary postcodes: `3198`–`3201`, `3910`) and Mornington Peninsula (25 primary postcodes: `3911`–`3944`):** `3911` Baxter splits 70% Mornington Peninsula and 30% Frankston, while `3912` Pearcedale and Somerville splits 78% Mornington Peninsula and 22% Casey.

In newer subdivisions around **Officer** (`3809`), **Pakenham** (`3810`), **Clyde** and **Clyde North** (`3978`), and **Botanic Ridge** (`3977`), verify your occupancy permit date first: VEU heater and hot water replacements (<Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> from mains gas hot water or <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" /> from electric storage hot water to a heat pump, with a <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> minimum customer contribution; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />) require a home built **more than two years ago**. You can also search any individual postcode in the <a href="/victoria/postcode/">VEU postcode lookup</a>.

## Council rebates, Kingston support, and local south-east guides

Municipal support varies across the six south-east councils:

- **City of Casey (<a href="/victoria/casey/">Casey rebate guide</a>):** Reimburses <Money id="casey-diy-draught-rebate" /> on eligible DIY weather-sealing materials (one claim per household with itemised product receipts; tradesperson installation excluded) and lends Home Audit Kits (<SourceLink id="casey-home-energy-rebates" label="City of Casey — Home energy rebates" />). Casey residents are also eligible to use **SEC Victoria's installer network** (<SourceLink id="premier-latrobe-pilot" label="Premier of Victoria — SEC installer network release" />).
- **Kingston:** Participates in the **Council Home Energy Program**, connecting Clarinda, Cheltenham, Mentone, Mordialloc, Parkdale, Chelsea, Patterson Lakes, and Heatherton households with council-vetted upgrade advice and suppliers (<SourceLink id="council-home-energy-program" label="Council Home Energy Program" />).
- **Frankston and Mornington Peninsula:** See bayside and peninsula upgrade details—including coastal outdoor-unit salt-air checks—on our <a href="/victoria/frankston/">Frankston rebate guide</a> and <a href="/victoria/mornington-peninsula/">Mornington Peninsula rebate guide</a>.
- **Greater Dandenong and Cardinia:** Neither municipality currently runs a separate council cash rebate on our verified source list; households claim state VEU discounts and Solar Homes rebates directly, which you can test for your address in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Eligibility Checker</a>.
