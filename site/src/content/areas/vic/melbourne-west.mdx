## Why western Melbourne's VEU numbers match inner Melbourne (except Little River)

Plan Melbourne's Western metropolitan region groups six councils across 34 postcodes (<SourceLink id="plan-melbourne-regions-map" label="Plan Melbourne — Metropolitan regions map" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files" />): **Maribyrnong** (`3011` Footscray and Seddon, `3012` Brooklyn, Kingsville, Maidstone, Tottenham, and West Footscray, `3013` Yarraville, and `3019` Braybrook), **Hobsons Bay** (`3015` Newport and Spotswood, `3016` Williamstown, `3018` Altona and Seaholme, `3025` Altona North, and `3028` Altona Meadows, Laverton, and Seabrook), **Moonee Valley** (`3032` Ascot Vale, Highpoint City, Maribyrnong, and Travancore, `3033` Keilor East, `3034` Avondale Heights, `3039` Moonee Ponds, `3040` Aberfeldie and Essendon, `3041` Essendon Fields and Strathmore, and `3042` Airport West, Keilor Park, and Niddrie), **Brimbank** (`3020` Albion and Sunshine, `3021` Albanvale, Kealba, Kings Park, and St Albans, `3022` Ardeer and Deer Park East, `3026` Derrimut and Laverton North, `3036` Keilor, and `3038` Keilor Downs and Taylors Lakes), **Melton** (`3023`, `3037`, `3335`–`3338`, and `3427`), and **Wyndham** (`3024`, `3027`, `3029`, `3030`, and `3211`).

In VEU Specifications v25.0 Table A (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />), **every single western postcode** carries **reticulated gas**, **Mild** climatic region, and **heat pump zone 4**:

- **33 Metropolitan + Mild postcodes** (`3011`–`3042`, `3335`–`3338`, and `3427`): identical VEU discount schedule to inner <a href="/victoria/melbourne/">Melbourne</a>, including Energy Victoria's <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-metro" /> ceiling insulation example for an uninsulated 150 m² home (customer co-payment <Money id="vic-veu-copay-insulation" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-insulation" label="Energy Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts" />).
- **Little River (`3211`, Regional + Mild):** split 58% Wyndham and 42% Greater Geelong by ABS dwelling counts, `3211` is the **only Regional postcode** in the western hub. Under VEU Part 48, its Regional location multiplier lifts the 150 m² ceiling insulation estimate slightly to <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-est-regional-mild" /> (confirmed on quote by your accredited provider).

Several western postcodes also straddle internal council boundaries: `3032` (Ascot Vale and Maribyrnong) splits 58% Moonee Valley and 42% Maribyrnong; `3023` (Caroline Springs, Burnside, Cairnlea, Deer Park, and Ravenhall) splits 55% Melton and 45% Brimbank; `3037` (Delahey, Hillside, and Sydenham) splits 58% Melton and 42% Brimbank; and `3427` (Diggers Rest) splits 93% Melton and 7% Hume. In established homes over two years old, Energy Victoria lists indicative VEU discounts such as <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> when replacing a room gas heater with one 3–9 kW split system, <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-16kw" /> when replacing ducted gas heating with a 16 kW ducted reverse-cycle unit, or <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> when replacing mains gas hot water with a heat pump (minimum hot water contribution <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />).

In newer western estates—Rockbank and Thornhill Park (`3335`), Fraser Rise and Aintree (`3336`), Cobblebank and Strathtulloh (`3338`), Diggers Rest (`3427`), Tarneit and Truganina (`3029`), Point Cook (`3030`), and Manor Lakes and Wyndham Vale (`3024`)—check your occupancy permit date first, because VEU heater and hot water replacements require a building **more than two years old**. Read estate-specific rules on our <a href="/victoria/wyndham/">Wyndham City page</a> and <a href="/victoria/north-west-corridor/">North-West Corridor guide</a>, or search any postcode in the <a href="/victoria/postcode/">VEU postcode lookup</a>.

## Council programs, meter loans, and webinars in community languages

Because VEU climate settings are uniform across Melbourne's west, the practical local differences come from municipal programs and bill-relief support:

- **Brimbank (`3020`–`3023`, `3033`, `3036`–`3038`, `3042`):** Participates in the **Council Home Energy Program**, giving Sunshine, St Albans, Deer Park, Taylors Lakes, and Sydenham households access to council-vetted upgrade advice and suppliers (<SourceLink id="council-home-energy-program" label="Council Home Energy Program" />).
- **Wyndham (`3024`, `3027`, `3029`, `3030`, `3211`):** Runs **Energy Smart Homes**, with recorded electrification workshops, a downloadable **Your Go Electric Plan** workbook, and **My Home Energy and Water Efficiency Kits** available to borrow through Wyndham libraries (<SourceLink id="wyndham-energy-smart-homes" label="Wyndham City — Energy Smart Homes" />).
- **Interpreted webinars across all six western councils:** Through the Western Alliance for Greenhouse Action, the **Home Energy in Your Language** webinar series runs for residents across **Wyndham, Melton, Brimbank, Hobsons Bay, Maribyrnong, and Moonee Valley** (<SourceLink id="wyndham-energy-smart-homes" label="Wyndham City — Home Energy in Your Language" />). You can also find translated state brochures and interpreter contacts on <a href="/victoria/help-in-your-language/">Help in your language</a>.
- **Melton (`3023`, `3037`, `3335`–`3338`, `3427`):** Lends **Power-Mate Lite** plug-in meters under its **Living Green** program so households can check which older appliances draw the most electricity before requesting quotes (<SourceLink id="melton-living-green" label="Melton City Council — Living Green" />).
- **Concession card holders:** Eligible Pensioner Concession Card, Health Care Card, and Veterans' Affairs Gold Card holders across western Melbourne can pair appliance upgrades with ongoing Victorian bill discounts (<SourceLink id="dffh-electricity-concession" label="DFFH — Annual Electricity Concession" />); review the rules in our <a href="/victoria/concession-card-holders/">concession card holders guide</a> and test your upgrade scenario in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Eligibility Checker</a>.
