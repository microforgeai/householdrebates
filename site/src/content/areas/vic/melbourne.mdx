## Inner Melbourne vs Greater Melbourne's other four regions

Greater Melbourne does not share a single VEU classification from end to end. This page covers **inner Melbourne**—combining Plan Melbourne's **Inner Metro** (**Melbourne**, **Port Phillip**, **Yarra**) and **Inner South East** (**Stonnington**, **Glen Eira**, **Boroondara**, **Bayside**) regions into one **52-postcode** hub (<SourceLink id="plan-melbourne-regions-map" label="Plan Melbourne — Metropolitan regions map" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files" />).

If your suburb sits outside those seven inner councils, check the matching regional hub or lookup tool:

- **<a href="/victoria/melbourne-north/">Melbourne's North</a>:** Hume, Whittlesea, Darebin, Banyule, Merri-bek (including Brunswick `3056`), Nillumbik, and Mitchell (66 postcodes).
- **<a href="/victoria/melbourne-west/">Melbourne's West</a>:** Wyndham, Melton, Brimbank, Hobsons Bay, Maribyrnong, and Moonee Valley (34 postcodes), plus our <a href="/victoria/north-west-corridor/">North-West Corridor guide</a> for Sunbury, Diggers Rest, Melton, and Gisborne.
- **<a href="/victoria/melbourne-east/">Melbourne's East and the Dandenongs</a>:** Manningham, Whitehorse, Monash, Maroondah, Knox, and Yarra Ranges (65 postcodes, including Cold hills and heat pump zone 5 postcodes).
- **<a href="/victoria/melbourne-south-east/">Melbourne's South-East</a>:** Kingston, Greater Dandenong, Casey, Cardinia, Frankston, and Mornington Peninsula (67 postcodes).
- **Statewide search:** Look up any of Victoria's 719 VEU postcodes in the <a href="/victoria/postcode/">VEU postcode lookup</a>.

## Heating, hot water, and ceiling insulation across the 52 inner postcodes

Because all 52 postcodes from Kensington (`3031`), Carlton (`3053`), Fitzroy (`3065`), and Kew (`3101`) across Hawthorn (`3122`), Camberwell (`3124`), South Yarra (`3141`), Caulfield (`3162`), St Kilda (`3182`), Brighton (`3186`), and Port Melbourne (`3207`) sit in **Metropolitan / reticulated gas / Mild / zone 4** (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />), Energy Victoria's metropolitan schedule applies uniformly to homes built more than two years ago:

- **Room gas heater to one 3–9 kW split system:** indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />).
- **Ducted gas heater to ducted reverse-cycle:** Energy Victoria lists <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" /> on a 22.4 kW system, alongside a mandatory <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" /> customer co-payment on any ducted unit (<a href="/victoria/air-conditioner-rebate/">compare all split and ducted capacities</a>).
- **Hot water heat pump upgrades:** indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" /> when replacing electric storage hot water or <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> when replacing mains gas hot water, with a <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> minimum customer co-contribution (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />; see <a href="/victoria/heat-pump-rebate/">heat pump hot water rebates</a> and our <a href="/victoria/gas-to-electric-hot-water-2027/">gas-to-electric hot water rule guide</a>).
- **Ceiling insulation (uninsulated 150 m² house):** Energy Victoria's published metro-mild example of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-metro" />, with a <Money id="vic-veu-copay-insulation" /> minimum co-payment (<SourceLink id="energyvic-insulation" label="Energy Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts" />).

In inner-city apartments, Victorian terraces, and heritage overlays across the City of Melbourne, Yarra, Port Phillip, and Stonnington, check owners-corporation approval rules for external condensers or heat pump tanks before booking work (<a href="/victoria/apartments-and-units/">see apartments and owners corporations</a>), and ensure your accredited provider captures pre-installation photos before disconnecting any old unit.

## Inner-Melbourne council programs

Alongside state-wide VEU discounts, three local council initiatives apply inside inner Melbourne:

- **Council Home Energy Program (Yarra, Boroondara, Glen Eira, and Stonnington):** Residents in those four municipalities can access council-supported electrification advice and vetted suppliers (<SourceLink id="council-home-energy-program" label="Council Home Energy Program" />).
- **City of Boroondara Solar Savers (`3101`–`3104`, `3122`–`3124`, `3126`, `3146`):** On the Australian Government's rebates portal, Boroondara's council-administered **Solar Savers Program** lists a **"free home energy assessment"** and a one-off discount of <Money id="local-boroondara-solar-savers-max" /> across eligible upgrades—including solar panels, batteries, heat pump hot water, split systems, insulation, draught proofing, and induction cooktops—available until **31 December 2026** or until program funds are exhausted (<SourceLink id="energygov-boroondara-solar-savers" label="energy.gov.au — Solar Savers Program (City of Boroondara)" />).
- **City of Melbourne, Port Phillip, and Bayside:** City of Melbourne publishes apartment and home electrification resources under its **Sustainable Energy** strategy (<SourceLink id="city-of-melbourne-climate" label="City of Melbourne — Sustainable energy" />), while residents across Melbourne, Port Phillip, and Bayside claim VEU discounts and Solar Homes rebates directly—test your address and appliances in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Eligibility Checker</a>.
