## Heating and cooling upgrades for Melbourne houses and apartments

Across inner and middle-ring Melbourne suburbs, the most common VEU heating upgrades are:

- **Single split system replacing a room gas wall furnace or space heater:** indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> (for a 3 to 9 kW split system).
- **Whole-home ducted reverse-cycle replacing ducted gas heating:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" /> for the largest 22.4 kW ducted systems (smaller capacities receive lower discounts; see the full table on our <a href="/victoria/air-conditioner-rebate/">Air Conditioner Rebate Victoria guide</a>).

For strata apartments and townhouses in the City of Melbourne and surrounding councils, remember that outdoor condenser placement and body corporate rules should be checked before installation, and your installer must record pre-installation photos of any decommissioned heater before work starts.

## Heat pump hot water discounts in Melbourne

When replacing an existing hot water unit in a Melbourne building at least two years old:

- **Inefficient electric water heater to heat pump:** indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" />.
- **Inefficient mains gas water heater to heat pump:** indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" />.

Every VEU hot water upgrade requires a minimum customer co-contribution of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" />. From **1 March 2027**, a mains gas water heater in an existing Melbourne home that needs replacement must generally be replaced with an efficient electric unit unless an exemption (such as a major switchboard alteration or certain apartment constraints) applies.
