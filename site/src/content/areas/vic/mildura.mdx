## Summer cooling and reverse-cycle upgrades in Mildura's Hot climate

In NCC climate zone 4—characterised by long, hot dry summers and crisp Mallee winters—high-efficiency reverse-cycle air conditioning handles peak summer cooling as well as winter heating (<SourceLink id="abcb-climate-zones-data" label="ABCB — Australian Climate Zone Map dataset" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />):

- **Replacing gas heating in homes over 2 years old:** Decommissioning a room gas heater for a 3–9 kW split system lists an indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> (minimum <Money id="vic-veu-copay-split-under-10kw" /> co-payment), while replacing ducted gas heating with a 16 kW ducted reverse-cycle system lists <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-16kw" /> (minimum <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" /> co-payment from 30 September 2026).
- **Upgrading an older split system or adding cooling without gas:** Replacing an existing non-ducted reverse-cycle unit lists an indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-split-replaced" />, and installing a room split system where no heater is decommissioned lists <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-no-decomm" />. Compare scenarios in our <a href="/victoria/air-conditioner-rebate/">Victoria air conditioner rebate guide</a>.
- **Pairing summer cooling and hot water with solar:** Abundant Sunraysia sunshine pairs well with Solar Victoria's <Money id="vic-solar-homes-pv-rebate" /> solar panel rebate for eligible households (<SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Solar panel rebate" />), daytime-timed heat pump water heaters, and <a href="/victoria/solar-hot-water-rebate/">electric-boosted solar hot water systems</a>.

## Why Mildura's Hot-region ceiling insulation discount is smaller

All **12 Mildura-council postcodes** (including PO-box postcode `3502`) sit in VEU Table A's **Regional** and **Hot** columns—12 of just 47 Hot postcodes statewide (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; neighbouring **Swan Hill (`3585`)** and **Robinvale (`3549`)** on our <a href="/victoria/solstice-gas-shutdown-towns/">Solstice gas shutdown towns guide</a> also sit in the Hot column).

Under VEU Specifications v25.0 Part 48, the **Hot** climatic region has a greenhouse gas savings factor of **`0.00546`**—lower than both **Mild (`0.00576`)** and **Cold (`0.00771`)**. Combining `0.00546` with the Regional multiplier (`1.04`) produces a smaller certificate discount than cooler regions for the same uninsulated or under-R2.0 150 m² roof: our scaled Part 48 estimate is <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-est-regional-hot" /> (with a <Money id="vic-veu-copay-insulation" /> minimum customer co-payment), and **the accredited provider confirms** the exact amount after inspecting your ceiling (<SourceLink id="energyvic-insulation" label="Energy Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts" />). Even with a smaller certificate count, ceiling insulation slows summer roof-space heat transfer—see rules on our <a href="/victoria/ceiling-insulation-rebate/">ceiling insulation rebate guide</a>.

## River towns with mains gas vs off-gas Mallee towns

VEU Table A splits the municipality cleanly between irrigated Murray River postcodes and dryland Mallee postcodes (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />):

- **Reticulated gas (7 postcodes):** **Mildura (`3500`)**, **Mildura PO boxes (`3502`)**, **Irymple (`3498`)**, **Red Cliffs (`3496`)**, **Merbein (`3505`)**, **Nichols Point (`3501`)**, and **Colignan (`3494`)**—all in **heat pump zone 4**. Replacing mains gas hot water with a heat pump lists an indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> (<Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> minimum contribution; see <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">gas hot water to heat pump</a>).
- **No reticulated gas (5 postcodes):** **Ouyen (`3490`)**, **Cowangie (`3506`)**, **Walpeup (`3507`)**, **Underbool (`3509`)**, and **Murrayville (`3512`)**. Replacing an older electric resistance water heater lists <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" /> (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />), while bottled LPG water heaters are assessed under VEU Part 3 where **the accredited provider confirms** the discount (<LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/lpg-homes/">see bottled LPG homes</LinkIfEnabled>).

## Mildura Rural City Council guidance and library toolkit

Mildura Rural City Council does not run a municipal cash rebate, instead offering practical **Electrify Your Home** guidance and a borrowable **Home Energy Efficiency Toolkit** through **Mildura Library** (for borrowers aged over 18 who sign a loan waiver; <SourceLink id="mildura-electrify-your-home" label="Mildura Rural City Council — Electrify your home" />).

The library kit includes a **thermal imaging camera** (useful for spotting missing ceiling batts and summer heat leaks around eaves and windows), a **PowerMate Lite** plug-in meter to measure standby and cooling power use, an energy-saving thermometer, and a water-flow cup. After checking your home, test <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3500">Mildura 3500</a>, <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3498">Irymple 3498</a>, or <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3490">Ouyen 3490 in the Victoria Eligibility Checker</a>.
