## The Hume corridor split: Metropolitan south vs Regional north

Plan Melbourne places Mitchell Shire inside the **Northern metropolitan region** (<SourceLink id="plan-melbourne-regions-map" label="Plan Melbourne — Metropolitan regions map (PDF)" />; see <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/melbourne-north/">Melbourne North</LinkIfEnabled>), yet VEU Specifications v25.0 Table A divides the shire's 12 postcodes across three distinct ceiling insulation settings (all in **heat pump zone 4**; <SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />):

- **Metropolitan + Mild (`3753` Beveridge [90% Mitchell, 10% Whittlesea], `3756` Wallan, and off-gas `3758` Wandong / Heathcote Junction):** Matches Energy Victoria's metro-mild 150 m² ceiling insulation example of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-metro" /> (minimum <Money id="vic-veu-copay-insulation" /> co-payment; <SourceLink id="energyvic-insulation" label="Energy Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts" />).

## New estates in Wallan and Beveridge: the 2-year building rule

Fast-growing estates in **Beveridge (`3753`)** and **Wallan (`3756`)** have a critical VEU eligibility catch:

- **Appliance replacements require a home older than 2 years:** Energy Victoria's household VEU replacement discounts—such as <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> for gas-to-heat-pump hot water (<Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> minimum contribution), <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> for room gas to split-system air conditioning, or <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-16kw" /> for replacing ducted gas with a 16 kW ducted reverse-cycle system (<Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" /> minimum co-payment)—only apply once the building is **more than two years old** (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />).
- **What newer homes under 2 years old can access:** Installing a room split system without decommissioning an existing heater carries Energy Victoria's smaller no-decommissioning discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-no-decomm" />, and eligible owner-occupiers can apply for Solar Victoria's <Money id="vic-solar-homes-pv-rebate" /> solar panel rebate (<SourceLink id="energyvic-new-buildings" label="Energy Victoria — New buildings" />; <SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Solar panel rebate" />). Check your occupancy timing in <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3756">Wallan 3756</a> or <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3753">Beveridge 3753 in the Victoria Eligibility Checker</a>.

## Off-gas towns across Mitchell Shire

Half of Mitchell Shire's 12 VEU postcodes—**Pyalong (`3521`)**, **Tooborac (`3522`)**, **Seymour PO boxes (`3661`)**, **Puckapunyal (`3662`)**, **Wandong and Heathcote Junction (`3758`)**, and **Bylands (`3762`)**—have **no reticulated mains gas** (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />).

Off-gas households replacing an older electric resistance storage tank have an indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" />, whereas bottled LPG hot water upgrades fall under VEU Part 3 where **the accredited provider confirms** the certificate discount (<LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/lpg-homes/">see bottled LPG homes</LinkIfEnabled>). Households in Kilmore, Broadford, Tallarook, Seymour, Wallan, or Beveridge planning to remove mains gas appliances can sequence each step on our <a href="/victoria/get-off-gas-planner/">Get-Off-Gas Planner</a>.

## Goulburn Murray Solar Savers in Mitchell Shire

Unlike outer-metro councils whose programs are paused, Mitchell Shire participates in **Goulburn Murray Solar Savers**, confirmed in a February 2026 Goulburn Murray Climate Alliance notice across seven member councils (<SourceLink id="gmca-solar-savers" label="Goulburn Murray Climate Alliance — Solar Savers" />).

Solar Savers is a council-backed vetted-provider pathway (not a separate council cash grant) covering rooftop solar, household batteries, hot water heat pumps, reverse-cycle split systems, induction cooktops, ceiling insulation, draught proofing, and home energy assessments, and stacks with eligible state VEU discounts and Solar Homes rebates. Start by checking your appliance mix in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Eligibility Checker</a>.
