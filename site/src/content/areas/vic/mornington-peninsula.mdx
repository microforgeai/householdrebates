## Postcodes, council boundaries, and shire guidance across the Peninsula

Under ABS 2021 Census dwelling allocations (<SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files" />), 23 of the 25 postcodes on this page (`3913`–`3944`, spanning Western Port towns from Tyabb, Hastings, Bittern, Crib Point, Balnarring, Somers, and Flinders across Port Phillip Bay from Mount Eliza, Mornington, and Mount Martha to Dromana, Rosebud, Rye, Blairgowrie, Sorrento, and Portsea) sit **100% within Mornington Peninsula Shire**. Two northern boundary postcodes cross council lines: `3911` (Baxter and Langwarrin South: 70% Mornington Peninsula, 30% <a href="/victoria/frankston/">Frankston</a>) and `3912` (Somerville and Pearcedale: 78% Mornington Peninsula, 22% <a href="/victoria/casey/">Casey</a>).

Mornington Peninsula Shire's <SourceLink id="mornington-peninsula-climate" label="Towards a Net Zero Peninsula initiatives" /> note that electricity and gas use is the largest local carbon emissions source and promote energy-smart building upgrades alongside state-wide VEU incentives.

## Hot water upgrades on the Mornington Peninsula: electric vs gas vs LPG

Across Mornington Peninsula Shire, hot water fuel types vary sharply between bayside townships and rural hinterland or southern coastal pockets:

- **Replacing an inefficient electric storage water heater with a heat pump** (common in Red Hill `3937`, Main Ridge `3928`, Flinders `3929`, and Cape Schanck `3939` where properties are off mains gas): indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" /> (see our <a href="/victoria/electric-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">electric hot water to heat pump guide</a>).
- **Replacing an inefficient mains gas water heater with a heat pump** (common in Mount Eliza `3930`, Mornington `3931`, Dromana `3936`, and Rosebud `3939`): indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" />.
- **Minimum customer co-contribution:** <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" />.

If your Peninsula property uses **bottled LPG**, note that Victoria's **1 March 2027** mains gas hot water replacement mandate does not apply to LPG, and your accredited provider will confirm which VEU hot water replacement category applies to your existing unit.
