## Hot water upgrades on the Mornington Peninsula: electric vs gas vs LPG

Across Mornington Peninsula Shire (including Mornington 3931, Mount Eliza 3930, Mount Martha 3934, Dromana 3936, Rosebud 3939, Rye 3941, and Sorrento 3943), hot water fuel types vary by locality:

- **Replacing an inefficient electric storage water heater with a heat pump** (common in hinterland and southern Peninsula homes without mains gas): indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" />.
- **Replacing an inefficient mains gas water heater with a heat pump** (common in Mornington, Mount Eliza, and Rosebud): indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" />.
- **Minimum customer co-contribution:** <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" />.

If your Peninsula property uses **bottled LPG**, note that Victoria's **1 March 2027** mains gas hot water replacement mandate does not apply to LPG, and your accredited provider will confirm which VEU hot water replacement category applies to your existing unit.
