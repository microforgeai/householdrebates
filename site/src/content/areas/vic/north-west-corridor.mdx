## Sunbury and Bulla (3429 and 3428)

In VEU Specifications v25.0 Table A, **Sunbury and Wildwood (`3429`)** and **Bulla (`3428`)** sit 100% inside Hume City Council and carry the standard outer-metro settings: **Metropolitan**, **reticulated gas**, **Mild** climatic region, and **heat pump zone 4** (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files" />). You can verify any street or surrounding <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/melbourne-north/">Melbourne North</LinkIfEnabled> suburb on our <a href="/victoria/postcode/">Victorian Postcode Lookup</a>.

- **What Table A changes here:** Ceiling insulation quotes follow Energy Victoria's Metropolitan + Mild calculation (<Money id="vic-veu-insulation-metro" /> for an average 150 m² home), and hot water heat pumps use zone 4 certificate ratings (<SourceLink id="energyvic-insulation" label="Energy Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts" />).
- **Council status vs state discounts:** Registrations for **Hume Home Energy Upgrades** are paused following Yarra Energy Foundation's voluntary administration, with only insulation and draught-proofing registrations lodged before **19 October 2026** continuing (<SourceLink id="hume-energy-upgrades" label="Hume Home Energy Upgrades — pause notice" />). State-wide VEU discounts and Solar Homes rebates are unaffected—<a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3429">check Sunbury postcode 3429 in the Victoria Eligibility Checker</a> (or <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3428">check Bulla 3428</a>) and contact Hume's Household Energy Officer for bill concession help (<SourceLink id="hume-energy-bill-support" label="Hume City Council — Energy bill support" />).

## Diggers Rest (3427): the Melton and Hume council split

**Diggers Rest (`3427`)** crosses two municipal boundaries: under ABS ASGS Edition 3 dwelling counts, **about 93% of dwellings sit in the City of Melton** and **about 7% sit in Hume City** (<SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files" />).

- **VEU and Solar Homes treat all of `3427` alike:** Every street in `3427` is classified in VEU Table A as **Metropolitan**, **reticulated gas**, **Mild** climatic region, and **heat pump zone 4** (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />). Whether your rates notice comes from Melton or Hume makes no difference to VEU point-of-sale discounts or Solar Homes hot water and solar PV eligibility.
- **Where your council boundary matters:** Melton ratepayers can borrow a **Power-Mate Lite** plug-in energy meter through Melton City Council's Living Green program (<SourceLink id="melton-living-green" label="Melton City Council — Living Green" />), whereas Hume ratepayers can contact Hume's **Household Energy Officer** for energy concession and Utility Relief Grant guidance (<SourceLink id="hume-energy-bill-support" label="Hume City Council — Energy bill support" />).
- **Run your Diggers Rest scenario:** <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3427">Open the Victoria Eligibility Checker pre-filled for 3427</a> to check heating, hot water, insulation, and the 2-year building-age rule.

## Melton and Caroline Springs (3335 to 3338 and 3023)

Across **Thornhill Park, Rockbank, Bonnie Brook, Grangefields, and Plumpton (`3335`)**, **Fraser Rise, Aintree, and Deanside (`3336`)**, **Melton, Melton West, Harkness, Kurunjang, and Toolern Vale (`3337`)**, **Melton South, Brookfield, Weir Views, Strathtulloh, Cobblebank, Eynesbury, and Exford (`3338`)**, and **Caroline Springs, Burnside, Burnside Heights, Ravenhall, Cairnlea, and Deer Park (`3023` — 55% Melton, 45% Brimbank)**, Table A classifies every postcode as **Metropolitan**, **reticulated gas**, **Mild**, and **heat pump zone 4** (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files" />; see also <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/melbourne-west/">Melbourne West</LinkIfEnabled>):

- **Established homes (more than 2 years old):** Older brick-veneer homes in Melton, Melton South, Melton West, Kurunjang, and Caroline Springs decommissioning gas appliances can compare Energy Victoria's indicative VEU discounts—<Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> when replacing a room gas heater with a 3–9 kW split system, <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-10kw" /> (10 kW), <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-16kw" /> (16 kW), or <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" /> (22.4 kW) when replacing ducted gas with ducted reverse-cycle (with a <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" /> minimum customer co-payment from 30 September 2026), and <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> when replacing mains gas hot water with a heat pump (<Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> minimum contribution) (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />; <SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 Part 6" />).
- **Council support:** Melton City Council does not run a separate cash appliance rebate; residents can borrow a **Power-Mate Lite** meter through Living Green to check appliance power draw before upgrading (<SourceLink id="melton-living-green" label="Melton City Council — Living Green" />).
- **Check your postcode:** <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3337">Check Melton 3337 in the Victoria Eligibility Checker</a> (or <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3023">check Caroline Springs 3023</a>).

## Gisborne, New Gisborne and Riddells Creek (3437, 3438, 3431 and 3430)

North of Sunbury, VEU Table A changes sharply across southern <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/macedon-ranges/">Macedon Ranges</LinkIfEnabled> postcodes (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files" />):

- **New Gisborne (`3438`) and Riddells Creek (`3431`):** Classified as **Metropolitan** with **reticulated gas**, **Cold** climatic region, and **heat pump zone 5** (100% Macedon Ranges Shire).
- **Gisborne, Gisborne South, and Bullengarook (`3437`):** Classified as **Regional** with **reticulated gas**, **Cold** climatic region, and **heat pump zone 5** (100% Macedon Ranges Shire).
- **Clarkefield (`3430`):** Classified as **Metropolitan**, **Mild** climatic region, and **heat pump zone 5**, with **no reticulated gas** (94% Macedon Ranges, 6% Hume). Off-gas properties replacing an inefficient electric storage water heater have an indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" />, while bottled LPG water heaters fall under VEU Part 3 with no published Energy Victoria LPG dollar figure—the accredited provider confirms the amount (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />; <SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 Part 3" />).
- **Why heat pump zone 5 matters:** Postcodes `3430`, `3431`, `3437`, and `3438` all sit in **heat pump climatic zone 5** (colder winter ambient conditions). VEU uses zone-5 performance tables when calculating hot water certificates, and cold-climate frost protection and tank sizing matter—check our <a href="/victoria/heat-pump-hot-water-size-guide/">heat pump hot water size guide</a> before choosing a model, then <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3437">check Gisborne 3437</a> or <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/?pc=3438">check New Gisborne 3438 in the Victoria Eligibility Checker</a>.

## New homes in fast-growing corridor estates

Across new estates in Thornhill Park, Cobblebank, Fraser Rise, Aintree, Deanside, Diggers Rest, and Sunbury, check your occupancy date before requesting VEU quotes:

- **More than 2 years old for replacements:** Energy Victoria's household heating/cooling and hot water replacement discounts require a home built **more than 2 years ago** (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />).
- **Homes under 2 years old:** Installing a reverse-cycle split system where no existing heater is decommissioned attracts Energy Victoria's smaller no-decommissioning VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-no-decomm" />, while eligible owner-occupiers can apply for Solar Victoria's <Money id="vic-solar-homes-pv-rebate" /> rooftop solar panel rebate (including during construction) (<SourceLink id="energyvic-new-buildings" label="Energy Victoria — New buildings" />; <SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Solar panel rebate" />).
- **All-electric new-build standard:** Under currently legislated Victorian rules from **1 January 2027**, all new homes must be built all-electric (<SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Solar panel rebate" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-new-buildings" label="Energy Victoria — New buildings" />). See building-permit timing and exemptions in <a href="/victoria/new-homes-all-electric-2027/">new all-electric homes in Victoria (2027)</a>, or test your home's age and appliances in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Eligibility Checker</a>.
