## Regional cold ceiling insulation discounts in Greater Shepparton

Across all 13 Greater Shepparton postcodes (<SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS 2021 postcode allocation" />), VEU Specifications v25.0 Table A assigns **Regional**, **Cold** climatic region, and **heat pump zone 4** (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; compare neighbouring <a href="/victoria/bendigo/">Greater Bendigo</a>):

- **Reticulated gas postcodes (6):** `3610` (Murchison), `3616` (Tatura), `3618` (Merrigum), `3629` (Mooroopna), `3630` (Shepparton), and `3631` (Kialla).
- **Non-reticulated postcodes (7):** `3614` (Toolamba), `3617` (Byrneside), `3633` (Congupna), `3634` (Tallygaroopna and Katandra West), `3646` (Dookie), `3647` (Dookie College), and PO-box postcode `3632`.

Because Greater Shepparton sits in VEU's Regional + Cold classification, eligible uninsulated or under-R2.0 ceilings align with Energy Victoria's regional cold example of <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-regional" /> (compared with <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-metro" /> in mild metropolitan postcodes; <SourceLink id="energyvic-insulation" label="Energy Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts" />). Your accredited provider confirms the exact discount in your quote—see electrical safety and eligibility rules on our <a href="/victoria/ceiling-insulation-rebate/">ceiling insulation rebate guide</a>.

## Hot Goulburn Valley summers, cooling swaps, and rental standards

Shepparton sits in National Construction Code **climate zone 4** (hot dry summer, cool winter), unlike Melbourne's temperate zone 6 (<SourceLink id="abcb-climate-zones-data" label="ABCB Climate Zone Map dataset" />). Replacing an older gas heater with a reverse-cycle split system provides both high-efficiency winter heating and summer cooling—compare indicative discounts and minimum co-payments on <a href="/victoria/gas-room-heater-to-split-system/">gas room heater to split system</a>.

For Greater Shepparton rental providers and renters, minimum ceiling insulation, draught proofing, hot water, heating, and main-living-area cooling obligations are also scheduled under <a href="/victoria/landlords-rental-standards-2027/">Victoria's 2027 rental energy-efficiency standards</a> (as currently legislated).

## Goulburn Murray Solar Savers and Hume VEU providers

Through the **Goulburn Murray Climate Alliance (GMCA)**, **Goulburn Murray Solar Savers** is open to households in Greater Shepparton and six other GMCA councils, connecting residents with vetted providers for solar panels, batteries, heat pump hot water, split systems, ceiling insulation, draught proofing, and home energy assessments (<SourceLink id="gmca-solar-savers" label="Goulburn Murray Climate Alliance (GMCA)" />).

On Energy Victoria's **Hume** regional directories, <Money id="vic-veu-providers-hume-hw" /> accredited providers were listed for hot water (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />) and <Money id="vic-veu-providers-hume-hc" /> for heating and cooling (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />). Check your postcode and current appliances in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
