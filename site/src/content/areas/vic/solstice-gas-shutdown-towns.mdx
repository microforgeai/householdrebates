## Which ten Victorian towns are affected?

Solstice Energy is closing its regional compressed natural gas (CNG) distribution networks by mid-to-late 2026 across **ten Victorian towns**: **Marong**, **Heathcote**, **Swan Hill**, **Maldon**, **Robinvale**, **Kerang**, **Nathalia**, **Terang**, **Lakes Entrance**, and **Orbost** (<SourceLink id="solarvic-solstice" label="Solar Victoria — Supporting Solstice Energy customers" />).

Note on the postcode table above: only **Marong (`3515`)** and **Heathcote (`3523`)** (within Greater <a href="/victoria/bendigo/">Bendigo</a>) are listed in our verified postcode extract on this page; residents in the other eight towns should confirm their exact VEU postcode classification with one of the **eight VEU accredited providers** Solar Victoria confirmed as servicing the Solstice towns (<SourceLink id="solarvic-solstice" label="Solar Victoria — Supporting Solstice Energy customers" />).

## Special Solar Victoria exemptions and top-up support

To help impacted CNG customers move straight to electric appliances, Solar Victoria applies dedicated rule exemptions on top of standard <a href="/victoria/veu-vs-solar-homes/">VEU and Solar Homes stacking</a> (<SourceLink id="solarvic-solstice" label="Solar Victoria — Supporting Solstice Energy customers" />):

- **No household income cap:** Solar Homes' usual combined taxable income cap (<Money id="vic-solar-homes-income-cap" />) does not apply to impacted Solstice CNG customers.
- **Hot water system of any age:** The standard three-year minimum age rule on existing hot water systems is waived for <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">gas hot water to heat pump</a> or solar hot water replacements.
- **Rental providers eligible:** Landlords of impacted Solstice properties can access support to electrify tenanted homes.
- **Top-up when VEU rules are not met:** Where a newer home (under two years old) or recently installed appliance does not qualify for a VEU discount, Solar Victoria provides a <Money id="vic-solstice-topup" /> top-up (<SourceLink id="solarvic-solstice" label="Solar Victoria — Supporting Solstice Energy customers" />).

## Going all-electric vs switching to bottled LPG

Impacted households generally choose between converting gas appliances to bottled LPG or replacing them with reverse-cycle air conditioning, a heat pump water heater, and induction cooking. City of Greater Bendigo's Marong study found that bottled LPG carries **at least 2.5 times the running cost** of running electric appliances (<SourceLink id="bendigo-solstice" label="City of Greater Bendigo — Support for Solstice Energy customers" />).
