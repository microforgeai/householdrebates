## Free home energy assessments and events in Whittlesea

The City of Whittlesea launched its **Home Energy Program** (council announcement 20 October 2026) to help local households cut bills and improve comfort toward the municipality's **net zero community emissions by 2036** goal (<SourceLink id="whittlesea-home-energy-news" label="City of Whittlesea — Home Energy Program news" />).

Under the program:

- **Free home energy assessments:** Available until **June 2027** or until allocated funds run out. Assessments are capped at **one per property** and are not available if the home has already had an assessment within the **past five years** (<SourceLink id="whittlesea-home-energy-program" label="Home Energy Program — City of Whittlesea" />).
- **Community sessions:** Council runs local workshops, one-on-one expert 'speed date' sessions, and a Home Energy Expo so residents can compare upgrade priorities before requesting quotes (<SourceLink id="whittlesea-home-energy-news" label="City of Whittlesea — Home Energy Program news" />).

## Easy Electric SEC installers and VEU accredited providers

When Whittlesea households are ready to replace gas appliances, electrification enquiries through the Home Energy Program are routed to **Easy Electric SEC** installers (<SourceLink id="sec-easy-electric" label="SEC Victoria — Easy Electric announcement" />). You are also free to compare quotes from any independent business on the Essential Services Commission register—follow our step-by-step checks in <a href="/victoria/find-an-accredited-provider/">how to find and verify an accredited VEU provider</a>.

## Planning gas-to-electric upgrades across Whittlesea postcodes

Across Epping (`3076`), Wollert (`3750`), South Morang (`3752`), Mernda and Doreen (`3754`), and Whittlesea township (`3757`), VEU Specifications v25.0 Table A classifies every listed postcode as **Metropolitan**, **reticulated gas**, and **Mild** climatic region (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; compare broader <a href="/victoria/melbourne/">Melbourne metro settings</a>):

- **Hot water:** Replacing an ageing gas storage or instantaneous unit is often the simplest first step—see <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">gas hot water to heat pump</a> for VEU and Solar Homes stacking rules.
- **Staging the rest of your gas exit:** Sequence heating, hot water, and cooking upgrades using the <a href="/victoria/get-off-gas-planner/">Get-Off-Gas Planner</a>, then confirm your household's eligibility in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
