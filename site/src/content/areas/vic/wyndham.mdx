## Wyndham City Energy Smart Homes, library kits, and multilingual help

Wyndham City supports local households planning energy upgrades through three practical community programs (<SourceLink id="wyndham-energy-smart-homes" label="Wyndham City — Energy Smart Homes" />):

- **Energy Smart Homes workshops and planner:** Recorded home electrification sessions plus a step-by-step **Your Go Electric Plan** template.
- **Borrowable library kits:** **My Home Energy and Water Efficiency Kits** can be borrowed from local Wyndham libraries to test standby power draw, draughts, and showerhead flow rates before spending money on upgrades.
- **Home Energy in Your Language (WAGA):** Delivered by the Western Alliance for Greenhouse Action across six western Melbourne councils including Wyndham, providing interpreted webinars in **Arabic, Mandarin, Cantonese, Vietnamese, Sgaw Karen, and Punjabi**.

Eligible cardholders in Wyndham should also verify their ongoing bill discounts in our guide for <a href="/victoria/concession-card-holders/">concession card holders in Victoria</a>.

## VEU eligibility in Wyndham's established suburbs vs new estates

Under VEU Specifications v25.0 Table A (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS 2021 postcode allocation" />), Wyndham postcodes `3024`, `3027`, `3029`, and `3030` follow the same **Metropolitan / Mild** classification as inner <a href="/victoria/melbourne/">Melbourne</a>, while **Little River (`3211`)** is classified as **Regional / Mild** (all five sit on reticulated gas in heat pump zone 4).

Before booking a quote in fast-growing estates across Tarneit, Truganina, Manor Lakes, or Point Cook, check your occupancy date:

- **Homes more than 2 years old:** Qualify for full VEU replacement discounts when swapping an old gas or electric unit (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />; see <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">gas hot water to heat pump</a>).
- **Homes under 2 years old or under construction:** Read why the 2-year rule applies and what construction solar rebates are available in <a href="/victoria/new-homes-all-electric-2027/">new all-electric homes in Victoria (2027)</a>, or test your address in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
