## Why Yarra Ranges VEU settings depend on your exact postcode

Unlike uniform inner-metro councils, Yarra Ranges stretches from Lilydale (`3140`) and Wandin North (`3139`) across Healesville (`3777`), the Dandenong Ranges (`3786`–`3788`, `3795`, `3796`), and east to Warburton (`3799`). No single VEU classification covers the entire shire (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />):

- **Metropolitan vs Regional:** Most listed Yarra Ranges postcodes sit in **Metropolitan**, while Warburton (`3799`) sits in **Regional**.
- **Mild vs Cold climatic region:** Foothill postcodes such as `3139` and `3140` sit in **Mild**, whereas hills and valley postcodes (`3770`, `3775`, `3777`, `3786`–`3788`, `3795`, `3796`) sit in **Cold**—which affects certificate calculations for <a href="/victoria/ceiling-insulation-rebate/">ceiling insulation upgrades</a> and space heating.
- **Heat pump zone 4 vs zone 5:** Postcodes `3139`, `3140`, `3770`, `3775`, and `3777` sit in **heat pump climatic zone 5**, while the remaining listed postcodes sit in **zone 4**. Use our <a href="/victoria/heat-pump-hot-water-size-guide/">heat pump hot water size guide</a> to check cold-climate frost protection and tank sizing.

Always check the postcode table above for your street's postcode before comparing quotes on <a href="/victoria/ducted-gas-to-ducted-reverse-cycle/">ducted gas to ducted reverse-cycle</a> or hot water upgrades.

## Council Home Energy Program and older hills housing

Yarra Ranges Council highlights that Victorian houses built before 1990 average a **1.5-star** energy rating (<SourceLink id="yarra-ranges-sustainable-homes" label="Yarra Ranges Council — Sustainable homes" />). Through Council's **Home Energy Program**, residents can get help with draught proofing, ceiling insulation, and home assessments, with appliance electrification enquiries connected to **Easy Electric SEC** (<SourceLink id="yarra-ranges-sustainable-homes" label="Yarra Ranges Council — Sustainable homes" />). You can also verify any VEU installer directly using our <a href="/victoria/find-an-accredited-provider/">accredited provider guide</a>.

## Local community energy groups

Alongside council programs, volunteer-led local groups including **Healesville CoRE** and **Repower the Dandenongs** run neighbourhood electrification information sessions and peer support across the shire (<SourceLink id="yarra-ranges-sustainable-homes" label="Yarra Ranges Council — Sustainable homes" />).
