## Why Yarra Ranges VEU settings depend on your exact postcode

Stretching from urban foothill suburbs at Chirnside Park (`3116`), Kilsyth (`3137`), Mooroolbark (`3138`), and Montrose (`3765`) across the Dandenong Ranges ridge, down to the Yarra Valley floor around Lilydale (`3140`), Coldstream (`3770`), Yarra Glen (`3775`), and Healesville (`3777`), and east along the Upper Yarra highway to Yarra Junction (`3797`) and Warburton (`3799`), Yarra Ranges spans 26 postcodes with four distinct VEU combinations (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS 2021 postcode allocation" />):

- **7 Metropolitan + Mild + zone 4 postcodes:** Chirnside Park (`3116`), Kilsyth (`3137`, split 71% Yarra Ranges and 29% Maroondah), Mooroolbark (`3138`), Menzies Creek and Selby (`3159`, split 93% Yarra Ranges and 7% Cardinia), Montrose (`3765`), Kallista (`3791`), and Yarra Junction, Gladysdale, Gilderoy, Powelltown, and Three Bridges (`3797`).
- **4 Metropolitan + Mild + zone 5 postcodes:** Wandin North, Seville, Seville East, and Gruyere (`3139`), Lilydale (`3140`), Upwey (`3158`), and Belgrave, Belgrave Heights, Belgrave South, and Tecoma (`3160`) stay **Mild** for space heating and ceiling insulation yet shift into **zone 5** for hot water heat pumps.
- **14 Metropolitan + Cold postcodes:** Coldstream (`3770`), Yarra Glen, Dixons Creek, Steels Creek, and Tarrawarra (`3775`, 91% Yarra Ranges and 9% Nillumbik), and Healesville, Badger Creek, Chum Creek, and Toolangi (`3777`, 95% Yarra Ranges and 5% Murrindindi) pair **Cold** climate ratings with **zone 5** hot water, while Kalorama (`3766`), Mount Dandenong (`3767`), Tremont (`3785`), Ferny Creek (`3786`), Sassafras (`3787`), Olinda (`3788`), Sherbrooke (`3789`), The Patch (`3792`), Monbulk (`3793`), Silvan (`3795`), and Mount Evelyn (`3796`) sit in **Cold** + **zone 4**.
- **1 Regional + Cold + zone 4 postcode:** Warburton, East Warburton, Millgrove, Reefton, McMahons Creek, and Big Pats Creek (`3799`) form the shire's lone **Regional** postcode. Even though all 26 shire postcodes are marked `Yes` for reticulated gas at the postcode level in Table A, individual bushland properties on unreticulated roads in Toolangi, Powelltown, Reefton, or Sherbrooke often run on bottled LPG or wood heaters rather than mains gas.

In homes built more than two years ago, replacing an older gas ducted unit with a 22.4 kW <a href="/victoria/ducted-gas-to-ducted-reverse-cycle/">ducted reverse-cycle system</a> carries an indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" /> (<Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" /> minimum customer co-payment), while replacing mains gas hot water with a heat pump carries <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> (<Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> minimum contribution; <SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />). ## Council Home Energy Program and pre-1990 hills housing

Yarra Ranges Council highlights that Victorian houses constructed before 1990 average just a **1.5-star** thermal efficiency rating—meaning uninsulated pitched roofs, subfloor air leaks, and unsealed wall vents drive winter heating bills across older hills weatherboards and split-level brick homes (<SourceLink id="yarra-ranges-sustainable-homes" label="Yarra Ranges Council — Sustainable homes" />).

On Yarra Ranges Council's Sustainable Homes page, municipal support splits by upgrade type (<SourceLink id="yarra-ranges-sustainable-homes" label="Yarra Ranges Council — Sustainable homes" />):

- **Thermal shell upgrades and assessments:** Draught proofing, ceiling insulation top-ups, and home energy assessments run through the council-backed **Home Energy Program**.
- **Appliance electrification:** Enquiries for reverse-cycle air conditioners, hot water heat pumps, solar PV, and induction cooking are directed to the state government's **Easy Electric SEC** service.

Whether you request quotes via council channels, SEC, or a local hills trade, confirm VEU provider accreditation before signing any contract using our <a href="/victoria/find-an-accredited-provider/">accredited provider guide</a>.

## Local community energy groups

Two volunteer-led community energy groups highlighted by Yarra Ranges Council—**Healesville CoRE** in the Yarra Valley and **Repower the Dandenongs** across hills townships—run local information nights, thermal-comfort workshops, and neighbour-to-neighbour electrification guidance (<SourceLink id="yarra-ranges-sustainable-homes" label="Yarra Ranges Council — Sustainable homes" />). Enter your street's postcode and existing appliances in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a> to see which Victorian rebates and co-payments apply.
