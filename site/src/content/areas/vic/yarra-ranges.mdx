## Why Yarra Ranges VEU settings depend on your exact postcode

Unlike uniform inner-metro councils, Yarra Ranges covers 26 council postcodes stretching from Chirnside Park (`3116`), Kilsyth (`3137`), Mooroolbark (`3138`), Lilydale (`3140`), and Upwey/Belgrave (`3158`–`3160`) across the Dandenong Ranges, Healesville (`3777`), and east to Warburton (`3799`). No single VEU classification covers the entire shire (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS 2021 postcode allocation" />):

- **Metropolitan vs Regional:** 25 of the 26 Yarra Ranges postcodes sit in **Metropolitan**, while Warburton (`3799`) is the only **Regional** postcode (all 26 have reticulated gas).
- **Mild vs Cold climatic region:** 11 foothill and valley postcodes (including `3116`, `3137`–`3140`, `3158`–`3160`, `3765`, `3791`, and `3797`) sit in **Mild**, whereas 15 hills and valley postcodes (`3766`, `3767`, `3770`, `3775`, `3777`, `3785`–`3789`, `3792`, `3793`, `3795`, `3796`, and `3799`) sit in **Cold**—which affects certificate calculations for <a href="/victoria/ceiling-insulation-rebate/">ceiling insulation upgrades</a> and space heating.
- **Heat pump zone 4 vs zone 5:** Seven postcodes (`3139`, `3140`, `3158`, `3160`, `3770`, `3775`, and `3777`) sit in **heat pump climatic zone 5**, while the remaining 19 postcodes sit in **zone 4**. Use our <a href="/victoria/heat-pump-hot-water-size-guide/">heat pump hot water size guide</a> to check cold-climate frost protection and tank sizing.

Always check the postcode table above for your street's postcode before comparing quotes on <a href="/victoria/ducted-gas-to-ducted-reverse-cycle/">ducted gas to ducted reverse-cycle</a> or hot water upgrades.

## Council Home Energy Program and older hills housing

Yarra Ranges Council highlights that Victorian houses built before 1990 average a **1.5-star** energy rating (<SourceLink id="yarra-ranges-sustainable-homes" label="Yarra Ranges Council — Sustainable homes" />). Through Council's **Home Energy Program**, residents can get help with draught proofing, ceiling insulation, and home assessments, with appliance electrification enquiries connected to **Easy Electric SEC** (<SourceLink id="yarra-ranges-sustainable-homes" label="Yarra Ranges Council — Sustainable homes" />). You can also verify any VEU installer directly using our <a href="/victoria/find-an-accredited-provider/">accredited provider guide</a>.

## Local community energy groups

Alongside council programs, volunteer-led local groups including **Healesville CoRE** and **Repower the Dandenongs** run neighbourhood electrification information sessions and peer support across the shire (<SourceLink id="yarra-ranges-sustainable-homes" label="Yarra Ranges Council — Sustainable homes" />). Check which Victorian rebates apply to your home in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
