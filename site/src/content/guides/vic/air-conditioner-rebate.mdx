## Why the headline figure only applies to the largest ducted systems

You will often see marketing advertisements quote <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" /> off air conditioning in Victoria. According to Energy Victoria's heating and cooling discount tables, <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" /> is the maximum indicative discount for replacing a whole-home **ducted gas heater** with a **22.4 kW ducted reverse-cycle system**. Smaller homes or single-room upgrades receive different discounts based on the energy savings calculated for that specific scenario:

- Replacing a **non-ducted (room) gas heater** with a 3 to 9 kW split system: <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> (see our <a href="/victoria/gas-room-heater-to-split-system/">gas room heater to split-system swap guide</a>).
- Replacing a **ducted gas heater** with a 10 kW, 16 kW, or 22.4 kW ducted reverse-cycle system: <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-10kw" /> (10 kW) or <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-16kw" /> (16 kW) (see our <a href="/victoria/ducted-gas-to-ducted-reverse-cycle/">ducted gas to ducted reverse-cycle swap guide</a>).
- Replacing a **ducted gas heater** with a multi-split system (<Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-multisplit" />) or four single split systems (<Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-4splits" />) (see our <a href="/victoria/ducted-gas-to-split-systems/">ducted gas to split systems guide</a>).
- Replacing a **hard-wired electric heater or central electric ducted heater** with reverse-cycle air conditioning (see our <a href="/victoria/electric-heater-to-reverse-cycle/">electric heater to reverse-cycle guide</a>).
- Installing a new split system **without decommissioning** an existing heater: <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-no-decomm" /> (or <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-no-decomm" /> for a ducted unit without decommissioning).

## Eligibility requirements for Victorian homes

To access a VEU heating and cooling discount, your installation must meet Energy Victoria's household rules:

1. **Approved product:** The unit must be a high-efficiency reverse-cycle air conditioner listed on the VEU Register of Products that both heats and cools.
2. **Minimum warranty:** For household installations, the product must carry a minimum **5-year warranty**.
3. **Property age:** If you are replacing an existing heating system, the property must be **more than 2 years old**.
4. **Accredited provider involvement before work starts:** You must work with a VEU accredited provider, or a licensed installer partnered with one, **before** any installation or decommissioning work starts.

<Callout type="caution" title="Do not decommission your old gas heater first">
  If you remove your old gas heater or install your new air conditioner before a VEU accredited provider has collected the required pre-installation evidence, you will generally lose access to the discount. Read our guide on <a href="/victoria/reverse-cycle-vs-gas-heater/">the VEU timing and decommissioning rules</a>.
</Callout>

## Rules for renters and landlords

Rental properties in Victoria are eligible for VEU heating and cooling discounts. If you are a renter, you must obtain your landlord's written permission before any heating or cooling work takes place.

From **1 March 2027**, Victorian rental minimum standards also require landlords to install an efficient reverse-cycle air conditioner in the main living area when an existing heater breaks down and cannot be repaired, and require efficient cooling in the main living area at the start of a new lease.
