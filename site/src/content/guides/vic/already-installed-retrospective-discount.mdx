## Why retrospective VEU claims are rare

Under the Victorian Energy Upgrades program, discounts are designed to be arranged **before** installation starts (<a href="/how-quotes-work/">how VEU quotes work</a>). Energy Victoria warns that retrospective discounts—claiming after an upgrade is already installed—are available **only in very limited cases** and are **not assured** (<SourceLink id="energyvic-retrospective" label="Energy Victoria — Retrospective discounts" />).

Accredited providers are under no obligation to process a retrospective claim, and most cannot create certificates if required pre-installation evidence was missed before the old heater or hot water unit left the property.

## Strict six-month calendar-year deadline

Even if you have every document and find a willing provider (<LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/find-an-accredited-provider/">how to find a VEU accredited provider</LinkIfEnabled>), VEU rules set a hard statutory cut-off (<SourceLink id="energyvic-retrospective" label="Energy Victoria — Retrospective discounts" />):

- Certificates for a completed upgrade must be processed **within 6 months of the end of the calendar year** in which the installation took place.
- For example, an upgrade installed in **December 2025** had to be processed by **30 June 2026**; an upgrade installed anywhere in **2026** must be processed by **30 June 2027**.

## Evidence you must supply to an accredited provider

To assess whether a retrospective claim is even possible, an accredited provider typically needs all of the following (<SourceLink id="energyvic-retrospective" label="Energy Victoria — Retrospective discounts" />):

1. **Itemised tax invoice** showing the exact brand and model number of the VEU-approved product installed.
2. **Electrical and/or plumbing compliance certificates** issued by the licensed tradespeople who performed the work.
3. **Decommissioning and disposal evidence** proving how the old gas or electric appliance was permanently removed and disposed of.
4. **Geo-tagged before-and-after photographs** of the old equipment in situ prior to removal and the new equipment installed.

## Upgrades without decommissioning (including induction cooktops)

Energy Victoria notes that retrospective claims are easier where **no existing appliance had to be decommissioned** (<SourceLink id="energyvic-retrospective" label="Energy Victoria — Retrospective discounts" />).

For <a href="/victoria/gas-cooktop-to-induction/">induction cooktops</a>, households can buy an approved unit from a retailer and claim the discount afterwards through a participating provider—note that induction cooktops purchased **before 30 June 2026** must appear on the older **Part 46** product list to qualify (<SourceLink id="energyvic-induction" label="Energy Victoria — Induction cooktop discounts" />). If you have not started your next upgrade yet, check your options first in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
