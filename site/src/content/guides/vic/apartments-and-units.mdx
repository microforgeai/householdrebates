## VEU eligibility for individual units and owners corporations

Energy Victoria confirms that both individual apartment owners and **owners corporations** can participate in the Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) program (<SourceLink id="energyvic-renters-landlords" label="Energy Victoria — Renters, landlords and body corporate" />):

- **Owners corporations:** Eligible under VEU residential activities (for **Class 1, Class 2, Class 3, or Class 4** buildings) or commercial activities depending on the building's classification and common-property plant.
- **Individual apartments and units:** Can claim VEU discounts on lot-specific upgrades—for example, Energy Victoria publishes apartment, townhouse, and house <a href="/victoria/draught-proofing-rebate/">weather sealing (draught-proofing) examples</a> at 2-year and 5-year minimum product warranties (<SourceLink id="energyvic-weather-sealing" label="Energy Victoria — Weather sealing discounts" />), provided the dwelling has no open-flued or flueless indoor gas appliance. Check your owners-corporation rules before installing any external split-system or heat pump unit on common property or balconies.

## Centralised gas hot water and 2027 rules

Apartment buildings face distinct technical rules under Victoria's 2027 standards:

- **1 March 2027 gas hot water rule:** Regulation 45E(3) explicitly exempts a **Class 2 building** (such as an apartment block) served by a **centralised gas hot water system** (<SourceLink id="austlii-reg-45e" label="Building and Plumbing Amendment Regulations 2025 — reg 45E text" />; see <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-ban-exemptions/">all regulation 45E exemptions</a>).
- **2027 rental minimum standards:** Consumer Affairs Victoria includes exemptions where a rented apartment is served by centralised heating, cooling, or hot water, lacks space for an outdoor compressor, or is restricted by owners-corporation rules (<SourceLink id="cav-min-energy-standards" label="Consumer Affairs Victoria — Minimum energy efficiency standards" />; see <a href="/victoria/landlords-rental-standards-2027/">Victorian landlord 2027 standards</a>).

## Solar for Apartments program (Solar Victoria)

Separate from the standalone-home Solar Homes solar PV rebate (<a href="/victoria/veu-vs-solar-homes/">compare VEU vs Solar Homes</a>), Solar Victoria runs **Solar for Apartments** to help owners corporations install shared rooftop solar PV across multi-unit residential buildings (<SourceLink id="solarvic-apartments" label="Solar Victoria — Solar for Apartments" />):

- **Solar for Apartments funding:** **Solar for Apartments** provides <Money id="vic-solar-apartments-per-apartment" /> per participating apartment and <Money id="vic-solar-apartments-per-property" /> per apartment property, open until **30 June 2027** or until funding is exhausted.
- **Program expansion:** Solar Victoria is expanding the program—including removing the median property value limit (<SourceLink id="solarvic-apartments-news" label="Solar Victoria — Expanding solar savings to more Victorians" />). Check the current eligibility rules on its page before your owners corporation applies, and test individual lot upgrades in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
