## A FEDERAL Australian Government discount — not VEU

If you are comparing battery quotes in [Victoria](/victoria/), the upfront discount on your quote comes from the **FEDERAL** Australian Government **Cheaper Home Batteries Program**, administered nationwide under the Small-scale Renewable Energy Scheme (<SourceLink id="dcceew-cheaper-home-batteries" label="DCCEEW — Cheaper Home Batteries Program" />).

- **Program:** Commonwealth Cheaper Home Batteries Program (**FEDERAL**), regulated by the Clean Energy Regulator (<SourceLink id="cer-battery-changes-may-2026" label="CER — Battery rebates are changing 1 May 2026" />).
- **Not VEU or Solar Victoria:** Batteries are not discounted through Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEECs), and Victoria does not currently offer a separate state household battery grant or state battery loan. See [how VEU and Solar Homes differ](/victoria/veu-vs-solar-homes/) if you are combining a battery with state-supported solar panels or hot water.
- **How you receive it:** Eligible households, small businesses, and community organisations assign the system's Small-scale Technology Certificates (STCs) to their accredited retailer or installer, taking an indicative upfront discount of <Money id="fed-battery-discount-pct" /> off the installed price rather than waiting for a post-installation cash reimbursement.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Get quotes from an SAA-accredited installer before buying hardware">
  Both the battery system and inverter must be on the Clean Energy Council (CEC) approved product list, and the installation must be completed or supervised by an installer accredited with **Solar Accreditation Australia (SAA)** (<SourceLink id="dcceew-cheaper-home-batteries" label="DCCEEW" />). Never buy unapproved battery hardware online expecting to claim STCs later.
</Callout>

## Who can claim and system requirements

Under DCCEEW's published program rules (<SourceLink id="dcceew-cheaper-home-batteries" label="DCCEEW — Cheaper Home Batteries Program" />), an installation in Victoria must meet four core conditions:

1. **Eligible premises:** Households, small businesses, and community organisations across Australia.
2. **Battery capacity (<Money id="fed-battery-size-kwh" /> kWh nominal):** Small-scale battery systems with a nominal capacity from <Money id="fed-battery-size-kwh" /> kWh.
3. **Connected to new or existing solar PV:** The battery must be paired with an eligible new or existing rooftop solar photovoltaic (PV) system. If your home does not yet have solar panels, check our [Victorian solar panel rebate guide](/victoria/solar-panel-rebate/) first.
4. **Approved product and accredited installer:** Installed by a Solar Accreditation Australia accredited installer using battery equipment listed on the Clean Energy Council approved product list, meeting all state electrical safety requirements.

## How battery size changes the discount (14, 28, and 50 kWh taper)

Since **1 May 2026**, the Clean Energy Regulator applies a tiered capacity taper so that standard household battery sizes receive the full certificate rate per kWh while oversized systems receive a stepped-down rate above 14 kWh (<SourceLink id="cer-battery-changes-may-2026" label="CER — Battery rebates are changing 1 May 2026" />):

| Usable battery capacity slice | Share of STC factor applied | How it works in practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Up to 14 kWh** | <Money id="fed-battery-taper-0-14kwh" /> | Every usable kWh up to 14 kWh earns the full certificate factor for the installation period. |
| **Over 14 kWh to 28 kWh** | <Money id="fed-battery-taper-14-28kwh" /> | Only the slice between 14 kWh and 28 kWh is weighted at <Money id="fed-battery-taper-14-28kwh" /> of the base STC factor. |
| **Over 28 kWh to 50 kWh** | <Money id="fed-battery-taper-28-50kwh" /> | Only the slice between 28 kWh and 50 kWh is weighted at <Money id="fed-battery-taper-28-50kwh" /> of the base STC factor. |
| **Above 50 kWh (up to 100 kWh nominal)** | No extra STCs above 50 kWh usable | Systems up to <Money id="fed-battery-size-kwh" /> kWh nominal remain eligible, with STCs capped at the first 50 kWh of usable capacity. |

Because STCs trade on an open certificate market (up to the federal clearing house ceiling), the exact dollar deduction on your quote depends on your battery's **usable** capacity across those bands and the daily STC price your retailer locks in.

## What changes on 1 January 2027

Under DCCEEW's legislated schedule, the **FEDERAL** battery STC factor steps down on **1 January 2027** and then steps lower every six months through to 2030 (<SourceLink id="dcceew-cheaper-home-batteries" label="DCCEEW — Cheaper Home Batteries Program" />):

- **May to 31 December 2026:** <Money id="fed-battery-stc-factor-2026-h2" /> STCs per kWh of usable capacity (before applying the 14 / 28 / 50 kWh taper above).
- **1 January to 30 June 2027:** <Money id="fed-battery-stc-factor-2027-h1" /> STCs per kWh of usable capacity, then lower every six months until 2030.
- **Installation date sets the rate:** Clean Energy Regulator rules tie your STC entitlement to the **installation date** (when the system is installed and certified compliant), **not** the date you sign a quote or pay a deposit (<SourceLink id="cer-battery-changes-may-2026" label="CER — Battery rebates are changing 1 May 2026" />). If installer queues push a December 2026 booking into January 2027, the lower <Money id="fed-battery-stc-factor-2027-h1" /> factor applies.

## Small businesses and community organisations

Small businesses, farms, sporting clubs, and community organisations in Victoria can also claim the **FEDERAL** Cheaper Home Batteries discount on eligible <Money id="fed-battery-size-kwh" /> kWh solar-connected battery systems (<SourceLink id="dcceew-cheaper-home-batteries" label="DCCEEW" />). While VEU itself does not offer a commercial battery discount, businesses pairing a battery with 30 to 200 kW commercial solar panels, heat pumps, cold-room upgrades, or air conditioning can stack federal battery STCs alongside VEU commercial certificates — see our [Victorian business energy upgrades hub](/victoria/business-energy-upgrades/) or check household VEU upgrades in the [Victoria Eligibility Checker](/victoria/eligibility-checker/).
