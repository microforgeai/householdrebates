## Who can apply: cafes, grocers, farms, and room rules

Walk-in cold rooms are a **business-only** VEU product (<SourceLink id="energyvic-veu-products" label="Energy Victoria — VEU products" />), commonly used by cafes, restaurants, butchers, bottle shops, independent grocers, florists, and agricultural packhouses. Check these threshold rules before ordering equipment (<SourceLink id="energyvic-cold-rooms" label="Energy Victoria — Cold room discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-businesses" label="Energy Victoria — VEU for businesses" />):

- **Scheduled Activity Premises exclusion:** Sites on the Essential Services Commission Register of Scheduled Activity Premises cannot use VEU unless they have opted in—see the register note on our <a href="/victoria/business-energy-upgrades/#scheduled-premises">Victorian business energy upgrades hub</a>.
- **Walk-in size and temperature threshold:** To qualify for the cold room activity, the room must be **walk-in**, have **at least 4 m² of floor area**, and store goods **below 7 °C** or operate as a **freezer**.
- **Engage an accredited provider before work begins:** Contact a <a href="/victoria/find-an-accredited-provider/">VEU accredited provider</a> before installing controllers, expansion valves, or new refrigeration hardware.

## Plug-in fridges, display cabinets (Part 32), and fan motors (Part 33)

Businesses running smaller refrigeration cabinets or component upgrades have three additional VEU pathways:

- **High-efficiency household-type fridges and freezers (250 to 700 L):** Open to businesses as well as households, offering an indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-appliance-fridge" /> per eligible unit rated at least **5.5 stars** for fridges or **4 stars** for freezers (<SourceLink id="energyvic-fridge-freezer" label="Energy Victoria — Refrigerator and freezer discounts" />).
- **Refrigerated display cabinets (Spec Part 32):** Commercial supermarket, bottle-shop, and hospitality display cabinets are an active VEU activity under Specifications Part 32, with **no published consumer dollar figure**—the accredited provider confirms product approval and any upfront discount (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />).
- **Refrigeration fan motors (Spec Part 33):** Replacing inefficient evaporator or condenser fan motors sits under Specifications Part 33, also with **no published consumer dollar figure**—the accredited provider confirms (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />).
