## Who can apply: building age and scheduled premises

Before comparing indicative dollar amounts, check three core VEU rules that apply to commercial, retail, hospitality, industrial, and rural premises in Victoria:

- **Engage an accredited provider before work begins:** You must sign up with a <a href="/victoria/find-an-accredited-provider/">VEU accredited provider</a> (or a licensed contractor partnered with one) before removing old equipment or starting installation, and VEU telemarketing and doorknocking bans protect businesses from unsolicited approaches—see <a href="/victoria/veu-scams-cold-calls/">how to spot VEU cold-call scams</a>.
- **More than 2 years old for replacement upgrades:** Replacement discounts for commercial heating, cooling, and hot water require an existing building more than 2 years old (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />).

<h3 id="scheduled-premises">Large energy users: ESC Register of Scheduled Activity Premises</h3>

Sites listed on the Essential Services Commission **Register of Scheduled Activity Premises** (typically large industrial greenhouse-gas emitters and scheduled facilities) are excluded from VEU discounts **unless the site operator has formally opted in** (<SourceLink id="energyvic-businesses" label="Energy Victoria — VEU for businesses" />; <SourceLink id="esc-scheduled-premises" label="ESC — Register of Scheduled Activity Premises" />). Check the ESC register before requesting commercial quotes.

## What Victorian Energy Upgrades covers for businesses

Energy Victoria lists commercial and industrial lighting, commercial solar PV, walk-in cold rooms, pre-rinse spray valves, and high-efficiency motors as **business-only** VEU products, alongside shared heating, cooling, hot water, and glazing activities (<SourceLink id="energyvic-veu-products" label="Energy Victoria — VEU products" />). Explore our four dedicated commercial guides:

- **<a href="/victoria/business-heating-cooling/">Business heating and cooling</a>:** Indicative VEU discounts from <Money id="vic-veu-biz-rcac-room-gas" /> (room gas heater to a 3 to 9 kW split system) to <Money id="vic-veu-biz-rcac-ducted-gas-56kw" /> on a 56 kW ducted gas replacement—always check the full 16 to 56 kW size table and mandatory <a href="/victoria/veu-minimum-co-payment/">VEU minimum co-payments</a>.
- **<a href="/victoria/business-led-lighting/">Business LED lighting</a>:** Commercial fluorescent tube replacements, warehouse and car park metal halide high-bay upgrades, and council street lighting.
- **<a href="/victoria/business-cold-rooms-refrigeration/">Walk-in cold rooms and commercial refrigeration</a>:** Indicative discounts of <Money id="vic-veu-biz-coldroom-small" />, <Money id="vic-veu-biz-coldroom-medium" />, and <Money id="vic-veu-biz-coldroom-large" /> across small, medium, and large walk-in cool rooms and freezers (with a mandatory customer payment of <Money id="vic-veu-biz-coldroom-copay" />), plus commercial display cabinets and fan motors.
- **<a href="/victoria/business-hot-water-motors/">Business hot water, motors and spray valves</a>:** Up to five standard hot water systems, larger commercial heat pumps (carrying a mandatory payment of <Money id="vic-veu-copay-business-large" />), electric motors (<Money id="vic-veu-biz-motor-185kw-premium" /> on a 185 kW premium unit, with a mandatory payment of <Money id="vic-veu-biz-motor-copay" />), and commercial kitchen pre-rinse spray valves (<Money id="vic-veu-biz-prv" /> each).

<h2 id="business-window-glazing">Commercial window glazing</h2>

Energy Victoria notes that **most businesses** can access VEU window glazing discounts when replacing inefficient single-glazed glass (<SourceLink id="energyvic-window-glazing" label="Energy Victoria — Window glazing discounts" />). Published examples list indicative discounts of <Money id="vic-veu-glazing-double" /> per 10 m² for double glazing and <Money id="vic-veu-glazing-secondary" /> per 10 m² for secondary glazing, with the accredited provider confirming product ratings and final amounts.

<h2 id="business-batteries">Solar batteries for small businesses (federal program)</h2>

Victorian Energy Upgrades does not offer a standalone commercial battery discount. However, the **FEDERAL** **Cheaper Home Batteries Program** covers eligible households, **small businesses**, and community organisations installing approved solar-connected battery systems with a nominal capacity of <Money id="fed-battery-size-kwh" /> kWh, providing an indicative upfront discount of <Money id="fed-battery-discount-pct" /> off installation costs (<SourceLink id="dcceew-cheaper-home-batteries" label="DCCEEW — Cheaper Home Batteries Program" />; read more on our <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/battery-rebate/">battery rebate guide</LinkIfEnabled>).

<h2 id="custom-upgrades">Custom (M&amp;V) upgrades for complex commercial sites</h2>

For larger commercial, manufacturing, food-processing, and agricultural facilities whose projects go beyond standard product tables, VEU offers **custom measurement and verification (M&amp;V) upgrades** (<SourceLink id="energyvic-custom-upgrades" label="Energy Victoria — Custom upgrades for businesses" />). Custom projects cover:

- Process heat and steam systems
- Large commercial HVAC and chillers
- Industrial refrigeration plant
- Large fans and electric motors over 185 kW
- Site-specific renewable energy integrations

Because certificates are created from measured before-and-after energy savings rather than a fixed deemed schedule, Energy Victoria publishes no standard dollar figure—an accredited M&amp;V provider confirms project suitability and estimated certificate value.

<h2 id="household-only-products">Household-only VEU products vs business appliances</h2>

Under the Essential Services Commission's activity premises rules (<SourceLink id="esc-veu-activities" label="ESC — Overview of VEU activities" />):

- **Household-only products:** **Ceiling insulation, induction cooktops, weather sealing, and in-home energy displays** apply to residential premises only and are not open to commercial buildings.
- **Heat pump clothes dryers (Part 25):** Unlike those residential-only activities, ESC lists Part 25 clothes dryers for both residential and non-residential premises (<SourceLink id="esc-veu-activities" label="ESC — Overview of VEU activities" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-veu-products" label="Energy Victoria — VEU products" />). A business purchasing an approved heat pump clothes dryer is **possibly** eligible for an indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-appliance-dryer" /> per unit—the accredited provider confirms.

<h3 id="apartment-common-areas">Apartment and multi-residential common areas</h3>

For Class 2 apartment buildings, owners corporations upgrading shared basement, corridor, or central plant equipment may fall under commercial VEU specifications—such as Part 34 building-based lighting or Part 44 commercial heat pump water heaters (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />). Eligibility for multi-residential common areas is assessed case by case, and the accredited provider confirms.

## Key eligibility rules that catch businesses out

<h3 id="indoor-lighting-fluorescent-only">Indoor lighting: existing fluorescent lights only</h3>

Indoor commercial LED upgrades under VEU now apply **only when the existing indoor lights are fluorescent** (<SourceLink id="energyvic-lighting" label="Energy Victoria — Lighting discounts" />). Car park, outdoor, and public lighting follow separate rules—read our <a href="/victoria/business-led-lighting/">business LED lighting guide</a>.

<h3 id="cold-room-rules">Walk-in cold rooms: minimum size and temperature</h3>

VEU cold room discounts require a **walk-in** room with **at least 4 m² of floor area** that stores goods **below 7 °C** or operates as a freezer (<SourceLink id="energyvic-cold-rooms" label="Energy Victoria — Cold room discounts" />)—compare small, medium, and large tiers in our <a href="/victoria/business-cold-rooms-refrigeration/">cold rooms and commercial refrigeration guide</a>.

<h3 id="pre-rinse-spray-valves">Pre-rinse spray valves: existing valve or fitting required</h3>

Commercial kitchens can receive the indicative <Money id="vic-veu-biz-prv" /> discount per valve only where an existing pre-rinse spray valve or dedicated fitting is already installed (<SourceLink id="energyvic-pre-rinse-spray-valve" label="Energy Victoria — Pre-rinse spray valve discounts" />)—see our <a href="/victoria/business-hot-water-motors/">business hot water, motors and spray valves guide</a>.

## Farms and rural businesses

Standard business VEU activities apply to Victorian farms, packhouses, dairies, and rural sheds—particularly walk-in cool rooms (<a href="/victoria/business-cold-rooms-refrigeration/">cold rooms and refrigeration</a>) and three-phase irrigation, dairy, or ventilation motors up to 185 kW (<a href="/victoria/business-hot-water-motors/">business hot water and motors</a>).
