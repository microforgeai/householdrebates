## Who can apply: building age, scheduled sites, and provider rules

Energy Victoria states that Victorian businesses across offices, retail shops, hospitality venues, warehouses, and community premises can apply for VEU heating and cooling discounts, subject to three threshold rules (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-businesses" label="Energy Victoria — VEU for businesses" />):

- **Building more than 2 years old for replacements:** Upgrading an existing gas, hard-wired electric, or older reverse-cycle unit requires a building more than 2 years old. Newer premises can only consider the smaller non-decommissioning scenarios in the table below, which the accredited provider confirms.
- **Scheduled Activity Premises exclusion:** Large industrial sites listed on the Essential Services Commission Register of Scheduled Activity Premises are excluded unless they have formally opted in—see the register overview on our <a href="/victoria/business-energy-upgrades/#scheduled-premises">Victorian business energy upgrades hub</a>.
- **Accredited provider before work starts:** Engage a <a href="/victoria/find-an-accredited-provider/">VEU accredited provider</a> before buying or removing any HVAC equipment, and watch out for banned unsolicited sales approaches (<a href="/victoria/veu-scams-cold-calls/">VEU cold-call and doorknocking rules</a>).

## Indicative commercial heating and cooling discounts by size

Commercial VEU discounts scale strongly with system capacity (`16 kW`, `25 kW`, `32 kW`, and `56 kW` for ducted reverse-cycle systems) and with the inefficiency of the heater being decommissioned (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />). ## Mandatory minimum customer co-payments (Part 6)

Under VEU Specifications v25.0 Part 6 (effective from 30 September 2026), businesses pay the same mandatory minimum customer co-payments per installed reverse-cycle air conditioner as Victorian households (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; read full tier rules on <a href="/victoria/veu-minimum-co-payment/">what the VEU minimum co-payment means</a>):

- **Any ducted reverse-cycle air conditioner (any kW capacity):** <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" /> per installed system.
- **Multi-split system with total rated cooling capacity of 10 kW or more:** <Money id="vic-veu-copay-multisplit-10kw-plus" /> per installed system.
- **Multi-split system with total rated cooling capacity below 10 kW:** <Money id="vic-veu-copay-multisplit-under-10kw" /> per installed system.
- **Single non-ducted reverse-cycle unit of 10 kW or more:** <Money id="vic-veu-copay-nonducted-10kw-plus" /> per installed unit.
- **Single non-ducted split system below 10 kW:** <Money id="vic-veu-copay-split-under-10kw" /> per installed unit.
