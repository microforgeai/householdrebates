## Who can apply: building age, scheduled sites, and provider rules

Before ordering commercial water heaters, three-phase motors, or kitchen tapware, confirm the core VEU rules (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-businesses" label="Energy Victoria — VEU for businesses" />):

- **More than 2 years old for hot water replacements:** Standard commercial hot water replacement discounts require an existing building more than 2 years old.
- **Scheduled Activity Premises exclusion:** Sites on the Essential Services Commission Register of Scheduled Activity Premises cannot use VEU unless they have opted in—see our <a href="/victoria/business-energy-upgrades/#scheduled-premises">Victorian business energy upgrades hub</a>.
- **Accredited provider before work starts:** Engage a <a href="/victoria/find-an-accredited-provider/">VEU accredited provider</a> before disconnecting old hot water plant, motors, or kitchen valves.

## Commercial hot water: up to 5 standard systems vs large C&amp;I heat pumps

Energy Victoria splits business hot water upgrades into two pathways (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />; <SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />):

- **Up to 5 standard hot water systems:** Small and medium businesses can upgrade up to **5 standard systems** using the same indicative per-system VEU figures as households (compare changeover details in our <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">gas hot water to heat pump guide</a>), with a <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> minimum customer contribution per system:
  - Inefficient electric water heater to heat pump: <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" />
  - Inefficient electric water heater to solar-boosted water heater: <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-solar" />
  - Inefficient gas water heater to heat pump: <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" />
  - Inefficient gas water heater to solar-boosted water heater: <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-solar" />
- **Larger commercial and industrial heat pumps (Spec Part 44):** Larger commercial heat pump installations under VEU Specifications Part 44 have **no limit on the number of systems**, **no published consumer dollar figure**, and a mandatory customer contribution of <Money id="vic-veu-copay-business-large" />. The accredited provider calculates certificates from your site's thermal output and confirms the quote.

## Commercial kitchen pre-rinse spray valves

Cafes, restaurants, pubs, aged-care kitchens, and food-processing wash bays can access an indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-biz-prv" /> for each water-efficient pre-rinse spray valve installed **where a spray valve or dedicated plumbing fitting is already installed** (<SourceLink id="energyvic-pre-rinse-spray-valve" label="Energy Victoria — Pre-rinse spray valve discounts" />). Brand-new sinks without an existing spray valve or fitting do not qualify, and the accredited provider confirms valve approval before installation.
