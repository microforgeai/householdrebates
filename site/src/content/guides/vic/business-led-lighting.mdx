## Who can apply: commercial premises and the indoor fluorescent rule

Commercial and industrial lighting is a **business-only** VEU product category (<SourceLink id="energyvic-veu-products" label="Energy Victoria — VEU products" />). Before planning an upgrade, check the core eligibility rules (<SourceLink id="energyvic-lighting" label="Energy Victoria — Lighting discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-businesses" label="Energy Victoria — VEU for businesses" />):

- **Scheduled Activity Premises exclusion:** Large energy-using sites on the Essential Services Commission Register of Scheduled Activity Premises cannot claim VEU lighting discounts unless the operator has opted in—check the rules on our <a href="/victoria/business-energy-upgrades/#scheduled-premises">Victorian business energy upgrades hub</a>.
- **Indoor lighting is fluorescent-only:** Energy Victoria specifies that **indoor commercial lighting upgrades now only qualify when the existing lights are fluorescent**. Premises whose indoor spaces already run halogen, incandescent, or older LED fittings cannot claim indoor VEU lighting certificates.
- **Engage an accredited provider first:** You must work with a <a href="/victoria/find-an-accredited-provider/">VEU accredited provider</a> before removing old fluorescent tubes or ballasts so pre-upgrade evidence is recorded properly.

## Car parks, public street lighting (Part 27), and apartment common areas (Part 34)

Different VEU specification parts govern outdoor, public, and multi-residential common-area lighting (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />):

- **Car parks, yards, and outdoor high bays:** Unlike indoor office or retail spaces, outdoor and car park upgrades replacing inefficient high-intensity discharge lamps (such as metal halide car park lights switched to LED high bays, shown at <Money id="vic-veu-biz-lighting-20-carpark" /> for 20 fittings) remain eligible outside the indoor fluorescent-only rule.
- **Public and street lighting (Part 27):** The indicative <Money id="vic-veu-biz-lighting-100-street" /> example for 100 street lights applies under VEU Specifications **Part 27**, which covers public roadway and street lighting upgrades for **Victorian councils and road authorities**.
- **Apartment building common areas (Part 34):** VEU Specifications **Part 34** includes **Class 2** multi-residential common areas (such as shared foyers, corridors, stairwells, and basement parking managed by an owners corporation). Common-area LED upgrades are **possibly** eligible where existing fittings meet Part 34 rules, and the accredited provider confirms—see our <a href="/victoria/apartments-and-units/">apartments and owners corporations guide</a>.
