## Repeat limits by VEU upgrade activity

Claiming a VEU discount on one appliance does **not** lock your home out of the rest of the Victorian Energy Upgrades program—for example, a household that upgraded hot water can still look into heating and cooling, ceiling insulation, or an induction cooktop. Within each activity, official Energy Victoria guidance sets out the following rules:

- **Hot water systems:** Victorian households can upgrade **up to 2 hot water systems** under VEU (while businesses can upgrade up to 5) (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />). See <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">gas hot water to heat pump discounts</a> for household scenarios.
- **Induction cooktops:** To be eligible, you **must not have previously claimed** a VEU induction cooktop discount (<SourceLink id="energyvic-induction" label="Energy Victoria — Induction cooktop discounts" />). Read more in our <a href="/victoria/gas-cooktop-to-induction/">gas cooktop to induction guide</a>.
- **Heating and cooling:** Energy Victoria's consumer heating and cooling page does not state a single household unit cap across every scenario—**ask the accredited provider** to check previous VEU certificate creations at your address and confirm how many systems qualify before any work begins.

## Stacking VEU, Solar Homes, and STCs on hot water

On an eligible heat pump or solar hot water upgrade, three incentives can work together on the **same** installation (<SourceLink id="solarvic-hot-water-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Hot water rebate" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />):

1. **Federal STCs (Small-scale Technology Certificates):** Applied as an upfront point-of-sale discount by your installer.
2. **Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) discount:** Applied upfront by your VEU accredited provider.
3. **Solar Homes hot water rebate:** Calculated **after** STCs and VEECs have been deducted, provided you are an eligible owner-occupier, obtain Solar Victoria pre-approval before installation, and your property address has **not** previously received a Solar Homes hot water rebate or battery rebate.

Energy Victoria states that combining the VEU hot water discount with the Solar Homes hot water rebate saves households <Money id="vic-hw-combined-solar-homes" />, or <Money id="vic-hw-combined-solar-homes-local" /> when choosing an eligible locally made product. Make sure an accredited provider is engaged before installation starts—see <a href="/victoria/already-installed-retrospective-discount/">rules on already-installed or retrospective VEU claims</a>—and compare full eligibility rules in <a href="/victoria/veu-vs-solar-homes/">VEU vs Solar Homes</a>, our <a href="/victoria/heat-pump-rebate/">Heat Pump Hot Water Rebate Victoria guide</a>, or the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
