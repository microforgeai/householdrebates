## Mandatory electrical and ceiling safety checks come first

Before any ceiling insulation can be installed under the Victorian Energy Upgrades program, Energy Victoria requires two separate safety assessments (<SourceLink id="energyvic-insulation" label="Energy Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts" />; <SourceLink id="solarvic-insulation-news" label="Solar Victoria — Ceiling insulation discounts news" />):

1. **Pre-installation electrical safety assessment by a licensed electrician:** Checks roof-space wiring, downlights, exhaust fans, and clearances.
2. **Installer safety assessment by an EEC-certified installer:** Installers must be certified by the **Energy Efficiency Council (EEC)** and complete an on-site hazard check before work starts.

<Callout type="warning" title="Halogen downlights and unsafe wiring block eligibility until fixed">
  Energy Victoria states that homes with recessed halogen downlights or unsafe electrical wiring are **ineligible** for VEU ceiling insulation until those hazards are rectified by a licensed electrician. Never lay insulation over unrated downlights or untested wiring.
</Callout>

## Is your ceiling eligible (uninsulated or below R2.0)?

To qualify under Energy Victoria's household rules, your ceiling must be either:

- **Completely uninsulated**, or
- **Under-insulated below R2.0**—which Energy Victoria describes as existing batts or loose-fill measuring roughly **90 mm thick or sitting lower than a ceiling joist**.

Combining ceiling insulation with <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/draught-proofing-rebate/">weather sealing (draught proofing)</LinkIfEnabled> helps stop heated air escaping around doors, vents, and wall gaps.

## Rental properties and the R5.0 standard from 1 March 2027

Under Victoria's minimum energy efficiency standards for rental properties (<SourceLink id="energyvic-rental-efficiency" label="Energy Victoria — Energy efficiency for rental properties" />), rental homes with **no ceiling insulation** must have at least **R5.0** ceiling insulation installed at the start of a new lease from **1 March 2027**, unless an exemption applies (<LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/landlords-rental-standards-2027/">read our Victorian landlord 2027 guide</LinkIfEnabled>).
