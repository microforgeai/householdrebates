## Ongoing electricity and winter gas bill concessions

Households holding a valid **Pensioner Concession Card**, **Health Care Card**, or **DVA Gold Card** can register their card with their energy retailer for two ongoing Victorian concessions:

- **Annual Electricity Concession:** <Money id="vic-concession-electricity-rate" /> off household electricity usage and service charges year-round, calculated after the first <Money id="vic-concession-electricity-excluded" /> of annual electricity bills (<SourceLink id="dffh-electricity-concession" label="DFFH — Annual electricity concession" />).
- **Winter Gas Concession:** <Money id="vic-concession-gas-rate" /> off mains gas usage and service charges between **1 May and 31 October**, calculated after the first <Money id="vic-concession-gas-excluded" /> of winter gas bills (<SourceLink id="dffh-winter-gas-concession" label="DFFH — Winter gas concession" />).

If you switch heating, hot water, and cooking off mains gas completely (<LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/get-off-gas-planner/">see our Get-Off-Gas Planner</LinkIfEnabled>), your gas bills and Winter Gas Concession stop once your gas account closes, while the Annual Electricity Concession continues across your electric load. Both homeowners and <a href="/victoria/renters/">Victorian renters</a> whose names are on the energy account can claim these concessions.

## Appliance upgrade discounts: VEU vs Solar Homes

Concession card holders can access appliance upgrades through two separate Victorian programs (<a href="/victoria/veu-vs-solar-homes/">compare VEU vs Solar Homes</a>):

- **Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU):** Energy Victoria's heating, cooling, and hot water pages apply **no household income test** (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />)—including upgrades such as <a href="/victoria/electric-heater-to-reverse-cycle/">replacing a fixed electric resistance heater with a reverse-cycle split system</a>. Watch out for uninvited callers claiming "free pensioner upgrades"—every VEU upgrade still carries a mandatory co-payment, and cold calling is banned (<a href="/victoria/veu-scams-cold-calls/">read our VEU scams guide</a>).
- **Solar Homes Program:** Eligible owner-occupiers with combined household income under <Money id="vic-solar-homes-income-cap" /> (<SourceLink id="solarvic-income-cap-news" label="Solar Victoria — Targeted assistance update" />) can apply for hot water or rooftop solar PV rebates.

## Free bill and concession help: Energy Assistance Program

The **Energy Assistance Program** provides free Victorian Government help for concession holders and households experiencing bill stress—checking that your retailer has applied your concessions, comparing energy plans, and arranging payment-difficulty support (<SourceLink id="energyvic-energy-assistance" label="Energy Victoria — Energy Assistance Program" />). To check appliance discounts for your home, open the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
