## Mandatory safety rule: carbon monoxide and indoor gas heaters

<Callout type="warning" title="Homes with open-flued or flueless indoor gas appliances do not qualify">
  Energy Victoria excludes any home that has an <strong>open-flued gas space heater</strong>, an <strong>open-flued ducted heater in a cupboard</strong>, an <strong>indoor open-flued gas water heater</strong>, or a <strong>flueless space heater</strong> from VEU weather sealing (<SourceLink id="energyvic-weather-sealing" label="Energy Victoria — Weather sealing discounts" />). Sealing doors, windows, wall vents, and chimneys reduces indoor airflow and can cause dangerous, odourless <strong>carbon monoxide</strong> to spill into living spaces—so the accredited provider checks your appliances first before any sealing work takes place.
</Callout>

If you are unsure what "open-flued" or "flueless" means:

- **Open-flued gas appliance:** Draws air for combustion from the room inside your home and vents exhaust gases up a flue or chimney (common in older wall furnaces, gas space heaters, and decorative gas log fires). If exhaust fans run and air gaps are sealed tight, negative pressure can suck exhaust fumes back into the room.
- **Flueless gas heater:** Burns gas inside the room with no flue pipe to the outside at all, releasing combustion gases directly indoors.

Until an unsafe indoor gas appliance is permanently decommissioned or replaced with an all-electric system, do **not** seal fixed wall vents or draught-proof the home. In rented properties with gas appliances, Consumer Affairs Victoria also requires a gas safety check before draught proofing (<a href="/victoria/renters/">see rules for Victorian renters</a>).

## What VEU weather sealing covers

In an eligible home, an accredited provider's installer can fit approved weather-sealing products across (<SourceLink id="energyvic-weather-sealing" label="Energy Victoria — Weather sealing discounts" />):

- External doors and windows
- Self-sealing exhaust fans
- Unused wall vents and chimneys
- Evaporative cooler ceiling vents

Pairing weather sealing with <a href="/victoria/ceiling-insulation-rebate/">VEU ceiling insulation</a> helps keep conditioned air inside, and from **1 July 2027** draught proofing becomes part of <a href="/victoria/landlords-rental-standards-2027/">Victoria's minimum rental energy efficiency standards</a> at a new lease.
