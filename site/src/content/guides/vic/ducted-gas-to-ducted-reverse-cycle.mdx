## What is the minimum you must pay?

Under Victorian Energy Upgrades Specifications version 25.0, Part 6 (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-veu-specifications-page" label="Energy Victoria — Industry specifications" />), from **30 September 2026** the mandatory minimum customer co-payment for **every household ducted reverse-cycle air conditioner of any capacity** is <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" /> per installed product.

Prior to 30 September 2026, the household ducted minimum co-payment was <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted-until-sep-2026" />. Even where a large ducted replacement attracts a substantial certificate discount, the accredited provider cannot reduce your out-of-pocket payment below the current co-payment floor. Read more in our guide to <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/veu-minimum-co-payment/">the VEU minimum co-payment</LinkIfEnabled>.

## Annual running-cost savings and eligibility rules

Energy Victoria's heating and cooling product page estimates that replacing a ducted gas heater with an efficient ducted reverse-cycle system saves households <Money id="vic-running-ducted-gas-to-rcac" /> a year on energy bills.

To meet VEU household eligibility rules:

- **Building age:** Your home must be **more than 2 years old** when replacing an existing heating system.
- **Approved product and warranty:** The ducted reverse-cycle unit must be listed on the VEU Register of Products and carry at least a **5-year household warranty**.
- **Provider before work starts:** You must use a VEU accredited provider, or a licensed installer working with one, **before** disconnecting your old gas unit—see <a href="/victoria/reverse-cycle-vs-gas-heater/">the VEU timing and decommissioning rules</a>.

## Can renters and landlords upgrade ducted gas heating?

Private rental homes are eligible for VEU heating and cooling discounts when the tenant and rental provider work together and the landlord gives written permission before work starts. From **1 March 2027**, when a fixed heater in a rental home's main living area breaks down and cannot be repaired, the rental provider must replace it with an efficient reverse-cycle air conditioner. Check your property in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
