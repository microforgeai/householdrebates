## Four single splits vs one multi-split: indicative VEU discounts

## Multi-split product rules and 30 September 2026 calculation updates

Under VEU Specifications version 25.0 (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-veu-specifications-page" label="Energy Victoria — Industry specifications" />):

- **Revised multi-split calculations:** Version 25.0 revised multi-split incentive calculations for upgrades from **30 September 2026**. Because certificate counts depend on the exact indoor and outdoor model ratings and postcode climate zone, your accredited provider confirms the final discount amount in your quote.
- **Same manufacturer requirement:** All indoor units connected to a multi-split outdoor unit must be from the **same manufacturer** as the outdoor unit.
- **Standard household rules:** The property must be more than 2 years old, products must carry at least a 5-year household warranty, and an accredited provider must be engaged before decommissioning the ducted gas heater.
