## Hard-wired panel or slab heater vs central electric ducted heating

Homes in regional towns, apartments, and older Melbourne suburbs without mains gas often run fixed electric resistance heaters (such as hard-wired wall panel heaters, in-slab heating, or central electric ducted furnaces). Energy Victoria publishes two household electric-replacement scenarios (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />):

- **Hard-wired electric resistance room heater to a 3–9 kW split system:** <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-hardwired-electric" />.
- **Central electric resistance ducted heater to a 10–22.4 kW ducted reverse-cycle system:** <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-central-electric-ducted" />.

Compare these against gas-replacement figures on our <a href="/victoria/air-conditioner-rebate/">Air Conditioner Rebate Victoria guide</a> and our <a href="/victoria/ducted-gas-to-ducted-reverse-cycle/">ducted gas to ducted reverse-cycle guide</a>.

## Do portable plug-in heaters count?

No. Portable plug-in fan, oil-column, or radiant heaters are **not** listed as an eligible current heating system on Energy Victoria's heating and cooling table. Only fixed, hard-wired electric resistance room heaters or central electric resistance ducted heaters qualify for the electric-replacement scenarios above; if no fixed heater is decommissioned, the smaller no-decommission split or ducted scenario applies instead.

## Minimum co-payments and eligibility checks

Under VEU Specifications version 25.0 (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />), mandatory minimum customer co-payments apply per installed product from **30 September 2026**:

- **Single non-ducted split system below 10 kW:** <Money id="vic-veu-copay-split-under-10kw" />.
- **Single non-ducted system of 10 kW or more:** <Money id="vic-veu-copay-nonducted-10kw-plus" />.
- **Every ducted reverse-cycle system of any capacity:** <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" />.

Your home must be **more than 2 years old** when replacing an existing heater, the new reverse-cycle unit must carry at least a **5-year household warranty**, and you must engage a VEU accredited provider before decommissioning the existing electric heater.
