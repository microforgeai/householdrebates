On Energy Victoria's hot water discounts page (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />), replacing an inefficient electric resistance water heater in a building at least two years old carries two indicative household VEU figures:

## Separate Solar Homes hot water rebate and Federal STCs

Alongside the VEU point-of-sale discount, two separate programs may apply depending on your household and product:

- **Solar Homes hot water rebate:** <Money id="vic-solar-homes-hw-rebate" /> (or <Money id="vic-solar-homes-hw-rebate-local" /> for an eligible locally made product) from Solar Victoria (<SourceLink id="solarvic-hot-water-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Hot water rebate" />). You must be an owner-occupier within Solar Victoria's income and property value caps, replace a system at least 3 years old, and get pre-approval before installation. Read <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/veu-vs-solar-homes/">how VEU and Solar Homes compare</LinkIfEnabled>.
- **Federal STCs:** Eligible air-source heat pumps with a tank capacity under **425 litres** on the Clean Energy Regulator register also create Small-scale Technology Certificates (<SourceLink id="cer-heat-pumps" label="CER — Solar water heaters and air source heat pumps" />).

We show the VEU and Solar Homes amounts separately because Solar Victoria calculates its rebate on the remaining price after STCs and VEECs are deducted, and your accredited provider confirms your exact out-of-pocket quote.

## How much less electricity does a heat pump use, and can it run on solar PV?

According to Energy Victoria, heat pump water heaters use <Money id="vic-hp-electricity-reduction" /> less electricity than a standard electric storage system.

Energy Victoria's consumer factsheet (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hw-factsheet" label="VEU Water Heating Consumer Factsheet PDF" />) also highlights that many heat pumps include **built-in timers** so the unit runs during the middle of the day using electricity from your rooftop solar panels. If you plan to heat water only once a day during solar hours, ask your installer whether you should step up a tank size (<LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/heat-pump-hot-water-size-guide/">check our heat pump hot water size guide</LinkIfEnabled>), or test your setup in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
