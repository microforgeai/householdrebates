## Accredited providers vs local licensed installers

Under the Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) program, **only accredited providers** can apply the upfront VEU discount and register certificates (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />).

- Some large heating, cooling, and hot water companies are both the licensed installer and the VEU accredited provider.
- Many independent plumbers and electricians are **not** accredited providers themselves—if you hire your own tradesperson, they must already partner with an accredited provider **before** work starts (<a href="/how-quotes-work/">see how VEU quotes and consent rules work</a>).
- Remember that unsolicited cold calling and doorknocking are banned under VEU (<a href="/victoria/veu-scams-cold-calls/">how to spot VEU scams and cold calls</a>).

## Energy Victoria's regional provider lists

Energy Victoria publishes downloadable lists of participating accredited providers on each VEU product page, grouped into six Victorian regions (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Find an accredited provider by region" />):

- **Greater Melbourne**
- **Barwon South West**
- **Gippsland**
- **Grampians**
- **Hume**
- **Loddon Mallee**

Energy Victoria notes that **not every accredited provider offers every VEU upgrade activity at present**—check the list on the specific product page (hot water, heating and cooling, ceiling insulation, induction cooktops, or weather sealing) that matches your job.

## Easy Electric SEC (free state-owned quote tool)

Alongside Energy Victoria's directories, the Victorian Government's state-owned **State Electricity Commission (SEC)** operates **Easy Electric SEC**—a **free** online tool that helps Victorian households plan electric upgrades and connects you with **SEC-endorsed installers** (<SourceLink id="sec-easy-electric" label="SEC Victoria — Going electric at home with Easy Electric SEC" />; <SourceLink id="sec-easy-electric-faq" label="SEC Victoria — Easy Electric SEC FAQs" />).

According to the SEC's published FAQs, once you choose to request quotes through Easy Electric SEC, an SEC-endorsed installer contacts you **within one business day**, at no cost for using the SEC matching tool.

## Council-backed home energy programs

Several Victorian councils also connect residents with vetted installers or home energy advice—for example, the council-backed program serving the <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/whittlesea/">City of Whittlesea</LinkIfEnabled> (<SourceLink id="whittlesea-home-energy-program" label="Home Energy Program — City of Whittlesea" />) and sustainable homes initiatives in <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/yarra-ranges/">Yarra Ranges</LinkIfEnabled> (<SourceLink id="yarra-ranges-sustainable-homes" label="Yarra Ranges Council — Sustainable homes" />).
