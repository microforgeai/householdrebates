## How much is the VEU induction cooktop discount?

According to Energy Victoria (<SourceLink id="energyvic-induction" label="Energy Victoria — Induction cooktop discounts" />), the indicative household VEU discount for replacing a gas cooktop with an eligible induction cooktop is <Money id="vic-veu-induction" /> per product, calculated on a certificate price basis of <Money id="vic-veu-induction-cert-price-basis" /> (<LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/what-is-a-veec/">see how VEEC certificate prices work</LinkIfEnabled>).

Under VEU Specifications version 25.0 (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />), each induction cooktop product also carries a mandatory minimum customer co-payment of <Money id="vic-veu-copay-induction" />.

## How the buy-first claim process differs from other VEU upgrades

For air conditioners and hot water systems, you must engage an accredited provider before purchasing anything. Induction cooktops follow a different workflow on Energy Victoria's product page:

1. **Check eligibility first:** Confirm your home, existing gas cooktop, and chosen model meet all VEU criteria, and review a participating accredited provider's claim requirements.
2. **Buy the cooktop:** Purchase the eligible induction cooktop from a retailer.
3. **Send the invoice to an accredited provider:** Submit your tax invoice and required evidence to the accredited provider to receive the discount.
4. **Arrange installation separately:** Have a licensed electrician wire the dedicated induction circuit and a licensed gasfitter cap the old gas cooktop connection.

## Which cooktops and homes qualify?

Every one of Energy Victoria's criteria must be met:

- **Replacing gas:** Must replace a current gas cooktop in a Victorian home built **at least 2 years ago**.
- **One claim limit:** You must **not have previously claimed** an induction cooktop discount (<LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/can-you-claim-veu-twice/">see rules on claiming VEU more than once</LinkIfEnabled>).
- **Built-in or freestanding only:** Portable plug-in benchtop induction units are **not** eligible.
- **Minimum size, zones, and warranty:** At least **550 × 380 mm**, **3 or more cooking zones**, and a **minimum 2-year warranty**.

## Disconnecting mains gas completely

Existing Victorian homes have **no legal requirement** to replace gas cooktops (<SourceLink id="energyvic-existing-homes" label="Energy Victoria — Existing residential buildings" />). However, for households that have already switched heating and hot water (for example via our <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">gas hot water to heat pump guide</a>), replacing a gas cooktop is often the final step to abolishing the gas meter—which Energy Victoria notes could save <Money id="vic-gas-supply-saving" /> per year in avoided gas supply charges (<LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/get-off-gas-planner/">see our Get-Off-Gas Planner</LinkIfEnabled>). Check your cooktop and other appliances in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
