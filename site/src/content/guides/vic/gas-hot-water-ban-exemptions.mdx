## Plain-English summary (not legal advice)

From **1 March 2027**, regulation 45E restricts licensed plumbers from installing a mains gas water heater to replace an existing water heater in an established Victorian home unless a listed exemption applies. The authoritative legislation is published on Victorian Legislation (<SourceLink id="legvic-bpar-2025" label="Building and Plumbing Amendment Regulations 2025 (SR 140/2025) on legislation.vic.gov.au" />), with the section text reproduced on AustLII (<SourceLink id="austlii-reg-45e" label="SR 140/2025 reg 6 — regulation 45E text (AustLII)" />) and summarised in the Victoria Government Gazette (<SourceLink id="vic-gazette-s654-2025" label="Victoria Government Gazette S654 (2025)" />).

<Callout type="info" title="Important notice: not legal advice">
  This guide is a plain-English consumer summary and is <strong>not legal advice</strong>. Under regulation 45E, compliance rests on the licensed plumber (facing a maximum penalty of <strong>20 penalty units</strong> for an unlawful installation), and your plumber determines whether an exemption applies to your property.
</Callout>

## Technical and property exemptions — regulation 45E(3)

Under **regulation 45E(3)** (<SourceLink id="austlii-reg-45e" label="Regulation 45E(3) text" />), a mains gas replacement is exempt where installing a non-mains-gas water heater is prevented by one of the following:

- **Insufficient indoor or outdoor space:** There is not enough space inside the building and no suitable outdoor space to install a compliant non-mains-gas alternative.
- **Centralised Class 2 building or pool system:** The water heater serves a **Class 2 building** (such as an apartment block—see our <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/apartments-and-units/">apartments and units guide</LinkIfEnabled>) or a swimming pool connected to a centralised gas hot water system.
- **Unlawful under another law:** Installing the electric or solar alternative would breach another law or planning requirement.
- **Switchboard or distribution board alteration:** Installing the non-mains-gas system would require altering the property's **main switchboard** or **distribution board** (<SourceLink id="vic-gazette-s654-2025" label="Victoria Government Gazette S654" />).
- **Network augmentation:** Installing the system would require electricity supply network augmentation beyond a basic or standard connection.

## Operational and transitional exemptions — regulation 45E(4) and 45E(5)

**Regulation 45E(4)** exempts three operational situations (<SourceLink id="austlii-reg-45e" label="Regulation 45E(4) text" />; <SourceLink id="vic-gazette-s654-2025" label="Victoria Government Gazette S654" />):

- **Temporary disconnection and reconnection** of an existing gas water heater (for example, during renovations).
- **No-electricity like-for-like gas replacement** where the gas water heater requires **no electricity connection** to operate.
- **Warranty replacement** during the manufacturer, supplier, or installer warranty period.

**Regulation 45E(5)** exempts three transitional situations (<SourceLink id="austlii-reg-45e" label="Regulation 45E(5) text" />):

- **Pre-March 2027 contracts:** Work completed **within 3 months after 1 March 2027** under a contract signed before 1 March 2027—see <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-broken-before-or-after-march-2027/">what happens if your gas hot water breaks before or after March 2027</a>.
- **Prior building work:** Eligible building work already underway under section 10(1) or 10(2) before 1 March 2027.
- **Rectification orders:** Work carried out under a rectification order issued before 1 March 2027.
