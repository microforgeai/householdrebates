## Before 1 March 2027 vs from 1 March 2027

Under Victoria's currently legislated building and plumbing standards for existing residential buildings (<SourceLink id="energyvic-existing-homes" label="Energy Victoria — Existing residential buildings" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />):

- **Before 1 March 2027:** Under currently legislated rules, there is no ban on repairing a mains gas water heater or replacing a failed unit with another gas heater, although switching to an approved electric heat pump or solar hot water system lets you access point-of-sale discounts—see our <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">gas hot water to heat pump guide</a>.
- **From 1 March 2027:** Under the currently legislated rule, when a mains gas water heater (including a gas pool heater) in an existing home breaks down and needs replacement, it **cannot** be replaced with another mains gas water heater unless an exemption applies. Repairing a serviceable existing gas unit remains allowed.

Read the broader policy timeline in <a href="/victoria/gas-to-electric-hot-water-2027/">Victoria's 2027 gas-to-electric hot water guide</a>. This page is a plain-English summary, not legal advice; your licensed plumber confirms how the regulations apply to your job.

## Transitional contracts and warranty replacements

Regulation 45E sets out narrow cases where a mains gas water heater replacement can still proceed on or after **1 March 2027** (<SourceLink id="austlii-reg-45e" label="Building and Plumbing Amendment Regulations 2025 — reg 45E text (AustLII)" />):

- **Contract signed before 1 March 2027:** Work carried out **within 3 months after 1 March 2027** under a contract signed with the owner before 1 March 2027 is exempt.
- **Warranty replacement:** Replacing a defective gas water heater during the manufacturer, supplier, or installer warranty period is exempt.
- **Technical and building exemptions:** Limited space, centralised Class 2 apartment systems, switchboard alterations, or network augmentation exemptions are covered in detail on <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-ban-exemptions/">Victoria's 2027 gas hot water exemptions</a>.

## What about LPG, gas heating, cooking, and rental homes?

- **Bottled LPG:** Homes running on bottled LPG rather than reticulated mains gas are **not** affected by the currently legislated 1 March 2027 mains gas replacement rule (<SourceLink id="vicplanning-gas-roadmap" label="Planning Victoria — Victoria's Gas Substitution Roadmap" />).
- **Gas space heating and cooktops:** Under currently legislated 2027 rules, existing gas heaters and gas cooktops in owner-occupied homes do not have to be removed when your hot water unit fails (<SourceLink id="energyvic-existing-homes" label="Energy Victoria — Existing residential buildings" />).
- **Rental properties:** Under currently legislated rules from **1 March 2027**, when a hot water heater in a Victorian rental property fails beyond repair, the rental provider must replace it with an efficient electric heat pump or electric-boosted solar water heater (<SourceLink id="energyvic-rental-efficiency" label="Energy Victoria — Energy efficiency for rental properties" />).
