## How VEU, Solar Homes, and STCs stack on a gas-to-heat-pump swap

When replacing an inefficient mains gas hot water system with an air-source heat pump, three separate incentives can reduce your upfront cost (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />; <SourceLink id="solarvic-hot-water-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Hot water rebate" />; <SourceLink id="cer-heat-pumps" label="Clean Energy Regulator — Air source heat pumps" />):

1. **Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) gas-to-heat-pump discount:** indicative <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> in a building at least 2 years old, with a minimum 5-year warranty and a minimum customer contribution of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" />.
2. **Federal STCs:** heat pumps with a volumetric capacity under **425 litres** listed on the Clean Energy Regulator register create Small-scale Technology Certificates, usually deducted upfront on your quote.
3. **Solar Homes hot water rebate:** <Money id="vic-solar-homes-hw-rebate" /> (<Money id="vic-solar-homes-hw-rebate-local" /> for an eligible locally made product), covering <Money id="vic-solar-homes-hw-share" /> of the purchase price calculated **after** STCs and VEECs are deducted. Available to owner-occupiers with combined household income below <Money id="vic-solar-homes-income-cap" />, property value below <Money id="vic-solar-homes-property-cap" />, and an existing water heater at least **3 years old**.

Energy Victoria lists the combined VEU plus Solar Homes reduction as <Money id="vic-hw-combined-solar-homes" /> (<Money id="vic-hw-combined-solar-homes-local" /> with an eligible locally made product), while Solar Victoria's own hot water rebate page puts the combined upfront reduction at <Money id="vic-hw-combined-solarvic-other" /> on another eligible system or <Money id="vic-hw-combined-solarvic-local" /> on a locally made system (<SourceLink id="solarvic-hot-water-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Hot water rebate" />). The two agencies' standard-product examples differ because VEEC certificate values and system retail prices vary; your accredited provider confirms the exact combined figure in your quote. Energy Victoria also estimates annual bill savings of <Money id="vic-running-gas-to-electric-hw" /> a year, and from **1 October 2026** households who opt in through their electricity retailer to the **Victorian Midday Power Saver** (**"three hours of free electricity"** from **11am to 2pm daily** — <SourceLink id="energyvic-midday-power-saver" label="Energy Victoria — Victorian Midday Power Saver" />) can set a heat pump timer to run in that midday window. Compare all hot water scenarios on our <a href="/victoria/heat-pump-rebate/">Heat Pump Rebate Victoria hub</a> and read <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/veu-vs-solar-homes/">how VEU and Solar Homes differ</LinkIfEnabled>.

## Does bottled LPG count?

VEU Specifications Part 3 (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />) covers gas or LPG water heaters, but Energy Victoria publishes only a mains gas figure on its consumer table, so the accredited provider confirms the discount for an LPG replacement.

## Practical swap checklist: placement, noise, frost, and tank sizing

Switching from an instantaneous gas wall unit or gas storage tank to a heat pump involves a few practical site checks:

- **Outdoor airflow and clearance:** Air-source heat pumps draw warmth from surrounding air and discharge cool air, so they need clear outdoor ventilation rather than a sealed cupboard.
- **Noise and neighbours:** Because a heat pump runs a fan and compressor while heating, ask your installer to place it away from bedroom windows and neighbour boundaries.
- **Winter and frost performance:** Ask for a model rated for your Victorian climate zone so cold winter mornings do not reduce recovery speed.
- **Tank size and 2027 planning:** Match your storage volume to household size and shower habits (<LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/heat-pump-hot-water-size-guide/">see our heat pump hot water size guide</LinkIfEnabled>). Remember that from **1 March 2027**, failed mains gas water heaters in existing homes generally must be replaced with an electric unit—see our <a href="/victoria/gas-to-electric-hot-water-2027/">1 March 2027 gas hot water rule guide</a>, and check your home in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
