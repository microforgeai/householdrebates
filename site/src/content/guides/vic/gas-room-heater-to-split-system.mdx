## Why the discount is a range and why decommissioning matters

Energy Victoria shows <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> as a range because certificate creation scales with the heating and cooling efficiency stars of the specific 3 to 9 kW model you pick, your VEU climate zone, and the accredited provider's certificate pricing.

To qualify for the gas-replacement range rather than the much smaller <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-no-decomm" /> no-decommission discount:

1. Your home must be **more than 2 years old**.
2. A VEU accredited provider (or an installer working with one) must record pre-installation photos and details **before** the old gas wall furnace or space heater is disconnected—see <a href="/victoria/reverse-cycle-vs-gas-heater/">our guide to the VEU timing and decommissioning rules</a>.
3. The old room gas heater must be safely decommissioned by a licensed gasfitter, and any wall or chimney opening sealed properly (see <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/draught-proofing-rebate/">draught proofing discounts in Victoria</LinkIfEnabled>).

## Sizing your split system properly

Energy Victoria's buyers guide (<SourceLink id="energyvic-rcac-buyers-guide" label="Energy Victoria — Reverse-cycle air conditioner buyers guide" />) recommends asking for an in-home visit before choosing a unit:

- **Avoid oversizing:** A split system that is too large for the room cycles inefficiently and uses more electricity than needed.
- **Compare heating capacity, cooling capacity, and noise:** Check both winter heating output and outdoor condenser noise levels, especially near boundary fences or bedroom windows, and ensure at least a **5-year household warranty**.
