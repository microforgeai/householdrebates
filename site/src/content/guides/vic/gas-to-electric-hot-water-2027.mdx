## What changes on 1 March 2027 for existing Victorian homes

Under Victoria's currently legislated electrification standards for buildings, from **1 March 2027**, an existing mains gas water heater (including a gas pool heater) that reaches the end of its service life and needs replacing cannot be replaced with another mains gas water heater, and households must instead install an electric water heater—such as a heat pump or solar-boosted water heater—unless a regulatory exemption applies.

Key boundaries confirmed by Energy Victoria and the Department of Transport and Planning:

- **Working gas units do not need early removal:** You only need to switch when your existing gas water heater breaks down and needs replacement.
- **Repairs are still allowed:** If your existing gas water heater can be safely repaired, you can repair it.
- **Owner-occupier gas heating and cooking are not affected:** Under the currently legislated 2027 rules, owner-occupiers are not required to remove or replace gas space heaters or gas cooktops.
- **Bottled LPG is not affected:** The Victoria Gas Substitution Roadmap confirms that homes using bottled LPG (rather than reticulated mains gas) are not affected by this rule.

## Exemptions in the regulation (reg 45E)

The exemptions to the currently legislated 1 March 2027 mains gas hot water replacement requirement are set out in the Plumbing Regulations, as amended by the Building and Plumbing Amendment Regulations 2025 (<SourceLink id="legvic-bpar-2025" label="Statutory Rule 140/2025 on legislation.vic.gov.au" />, full regulation text at <SourceLink id="austlii-reg-45e" label="AustLII reg 6 inserting reg 45E" />, alongside Victoria Government Gazette <SourceLink id="vic-gazette-s654-2025" label="GG2025S654" />).

Grouped as regulation 45E sets them out:

- **Practical limits (45E(3)):** not enough space inside, or no suitable outdoor space, for an electric water heater; a Class 2 building (apartments) or a pool served by a centralised gas system; an electric option would be unlawful under another law; installing an electric option would need the main switchboard or a distribution board altered; or it would need the electricity network augmented beyond a basic or standard connection.
- **Kinds of work (45E(4)):** temporary disconnection and reconnection; like-for-like replacement where the gas water heater does not need an electricity connection to run; or replacement within the warranty period.
- **Timing (45E(5)):** work under a contract signed before 1 March 2027, if done within 3 months after that date; building work under s10(1) or (2) of the Building Act carried out before 1 March 2027; or rectification orders issued before 1 March 2027.

The rule applies to plumbers, and a plumber who breaches it faces a penalty of 20 penalty units. This is a plain-English summary, not legal advice. Your licensed plumber decides whether an exemption applies. ## Rules for rental properties from 1 March 2027

Under currently legislated rules, rental properties face broader minimum efficiency standards from **1 March 2027**:

- **Broken, unrepairable water heater:** Under the currently legislated standard from 1 March 2027, it must be replaced with an energy-efficient electric water heater (heat pump or electric-boosted solar).
- **Broken, unrepairable main living area heater:** Under the currently legislated standard from 1 March 2027, it must be replaced with an energy-efficient reverse-cycle air conditioner.
- **Start of a new lease:** Under the currently legislated standard, efficient cooling must be installed in the main living area when a new lease starts from 1 March 2027.
