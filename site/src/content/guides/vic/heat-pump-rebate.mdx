## Electric vs gas replacement: why the two figures differ

A common misconception on rebate websites is quoting <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" /> for every heat pump hot water upgrade. On Energy Victoria's hot water discounts page, the indicative VEU discount depends on the fuel source of the system you are replacing:

- **Replacing an inefficient electric water heater with a heat pump:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" />.
- **Replacing an inefficient gas water heater with a heat pump:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" />.
- **Replacing an inefficient electric water heater with a solar-boosted system:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-solar" />.
- **Replacing an inefficient gas water heater with a solar-boosted system:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-solar" />.

According to Energy Victoria, switching from a gas water heater to an efficient electric hot water system also saves households <Money id="vic-running-gas-to-electric-hw" /> per year on energy bills.

## Combining VEU with the Solar Homes hot water rebate

Eligible Victorian homeowners can combine the point-of-sale VEU discount with the Solar Victoria **Solar Homes hot water rebate**. ## Household eligibility rules and the minimum contribution

To be eligible for a VEU hot water discount in Victoria:

1. **Replacing an existing unit:** You must be replacing an existing hot water system in a building that is **at least 2 years old**. New builds and properties without an existing water heater are not eligible.
2. **Maximum of two systems per household:** Households can upgrade **up to 2 hot water systems** at the same address under the VEU program.
3. **Approved product and warranty:** The new heat pump or solar-boosted unit must be on the VEU Register of Products and include a **minimum 5-year warranty**.
4. **Minimum customer contribution:** Under VEU program rules, customers must pay a minimum co-contribution of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> toward the upgrade.
