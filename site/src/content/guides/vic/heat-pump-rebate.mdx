## Electric vs gas replacement: why the two figures differ

A common misconception on rebate websites is quoting <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" /> for every heat pump hot water upgrade. On Energy Victoria's hot water discounts page, the indicative VEU discount depends on the fuel source of the system you are replacing:

- **Replacing an inefficient electric water heater with a heat pump:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" /> (see our <a href="/victoria/electric-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">electric hot water to heat pump guide</a>).
- **Replacing an inefficient gas water heater with a heat pump:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> (see our <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">gas hot water to heat pump swap guide</a>).
- **Replacing an inefficient electric water heater with a solar-boosted system:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-solar" /> (see our <a href="/victoria/solar-hot-water-rebate/">solar hot water rebate guide</a>).
- **Replacing an inefficient gas water heater with a solar-boosted system:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-solar" />.

According to Energy Victoria, switching from a gas water heater to an efficient electric hot water system also saves households <Money id="vic-running-gas-to-electric-hw" /> per year on energy bills.

## Combining VEU with the Solar Homes hot water rebate

Eligible Victorian homeowners can combine the point-of-sale VEU discount with the Solar Victoria **Solar Homes hot water rebate**. Energy Victoria notes that combining both programs can reduce the upfront cost by <Money id="vic-hw-combined-solar-homes" />, or <Money id="vic-hw-combined-solar-homes-local" /> when you choose an eligible locally made hot water product.

## Solar Homes hot water rebate

Under Solar Victoria's hot water program (<SourceLink id="solarvic-hot-water-rebate" label="Solar Victoria hot water rebate" />), the standalone rebate is <Money id="vic-solar-homes-hw-rebate" /> (<Money id="vic-solar-homes-hw-rebate-local" /> for an eligible locally made product), covering <Money id="vic-solar-homes-hw-share" /> of the purchase price calculated after Federal STCs and Victorian VEECs are deducted.

To be eligible for the Solar Homes hot water rebate:

- You must be an **owner-occupier** of the home.
- Combined household taxable income must be under <Money id="vic-solar-homes-income-cap" /> (from 1 July 2026; it was <Money id="vic-solar-homes-income-cap-until-jun-2026" /> — see <SourceLink id="solarvic-income-cap-news" label="Solar Victoria's May 2026 announcement" />).
- The property must be valued under <Money id="vic-solar-homes-property-cap" />.
- The existing hot water system being replaced must be **at least 3 years old** from the date of purchase.
- You apply for **pre-approval first**, then have your system installed within **120 days**.

## Household eligibility rules and the minimum contribution

To be eligible for a VEU hot water discount in Victoria:

1. **Replacing an existing unit:** You must be replacing an existing hot water system in a building that is **at least 2 years old**. New builds and properties without an existing water heater are not eligible.
2. **Maximum of two systems per household:** Households can upgrade **up to 2 hot water systems** at the same address under the VEU program.
3. **Approved product and warranty:** The new heat pump or solar-boosted unit must be on the VEU Register of Products and include a **minimum 5-year warranty**.
4. **Minimum customer contribution:** Under VEU program rules, customers must pay a minimum co-contribution of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> toward the upgrade.
