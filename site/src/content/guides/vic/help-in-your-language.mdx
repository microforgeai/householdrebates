## Call an interpreter (TIS National 131 450)

If you want to ask about [Victorian](/victoria/) energy rebates in your language or prefer speaking over reading written documents, Solar Victoria states that you can call the **"free National Translating and Interpreting Service"** on <a href="tel:131450"><strong>131 450</strong></a> (<SourceLink id="solarvic-community-languages" label="Solar Victoria — Community languages" />): tell the operator the language you speak, and the interpreter will connect your call to Solar Victoria, Energy Victoria, or the Essential Services Commission.

Households in Melbourne's west can also check interpreted six-language **Home Energy in Your Language** webinars listed on our [Wyndham energy rebates guide](/victoria/wyndham/), and Pensioner or Health Care Card holders can review ongoing bill discounts on [energy rebates for concession card holders](/victoria/concession-card-holders/).

## Avoid scams and high-pressure sales

Multicultural households and older residents are frequently targeted by uninvited callers or door-to-door marketers pretending an energy upgrade is compulsory. Under Essential Services Commission rules (<SourceLink id="esc-cold-call-bans" label="ESC — Cold-call and doorknocking bans" />; <SourceLink id="esc-consumer-rights" label="ESC — Your consumer rights" />):

- **Cold calling and unsolicited doorknocking are banned:** No business may call or knock on your door about VEU upgrades unless you gave express prior consent.
- **No installer works for the government:** Every VEU upgrade is voluntary, and legitimate upgrades carry mandatory customer co-payments.
- **Share the official rules with family:** Download Energy Victoria's <SourceLink id="energyvic-translations" label="translated VEU consumer-rights factsheet (PDF)" /> in your family's language, read our full guide on [VEU scams, cold calls, and doorknocking bans](/victoria/veu-scams-cold-calls/), see [how quotes and consent work](/how-quotes-work/), or check genuine rules first in the [Victoria Eligibility Checker](/victoria/eligibility-checker/).
