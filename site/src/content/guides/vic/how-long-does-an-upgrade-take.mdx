## Pre-approval and quoting milestones before installation

Energy Victoria does not publish an official on-site installation duration because every switchboard, pipe run, and roof space differs—your installer will confirm their on-site schedule when quoting (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />; see <a href="/how-quotes-work/">how VEU quotes work</a>).

Two official program rules govern the lead-up to installation day:

- **VEU provider engagement first:** The accredited provider confirms eligible models on the VEU Register of Products, quotes with the point-of-sale discount included, and handles the certificate paperwork (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />). Compare household setups in <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">our gas hot water to heat pump guide</a>.
- **Solar Homes 120-day window:** When stacking the Solar Homes hot water rebate, you must secure Solar Victoria pre-approval **before** installation begins (except under Solar Victoria's emergency replacement pathway) and finish the installation **within 120 days** of pre-approval (<SourceLink id="solarvic-hot-water-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Hot water rebate" />). If your tank has already failed, see <a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-broken-before-or-after-march-2027/">what to do when a gas hot water unit breaks</a>.

## Plumbing compliance certificates: the 5-day rule

Under Victorian plumbing regulations, a licensed plumber must issue a **plumbing compliance certificate** **within 5 days of completion** whenever plumbing work is valued at <Money id="vic-plumbing-cert-threshold" /> or involves **any Type A gas appliance work** (<SourceLink id="bpc-plumbing-compliance" label="Victorian Building Authority — Plumbing compliance certificates" />).

Keep that compliance certificate alongside your tax invoice and electrical certificate of electrical safety—both your warranty and your provider's VEU certificate creation depend on them.

## Gas meter removal and abolishment timeframes

If your hot water or heating upgrade removes your **last** mains gas appliance and you decide to close your gas account (<LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/get-off-gas-planner/">see our Get-Off-Gas Planner</LinkIfEnabled>), you request disconnection or permanent abolishment through your gas retailer.

On **AusNet's** gas network, AusNet publishes indicative timeframes of **2 business days** for a gas meter removal and **up to 15 business days** for a permanent service cut-and-cap abolishment (<SourceLink id="ausnet-gas-abolishment" label="AusNet — Requesting a gas disconnection, reconnection or abolishment" />). **Note:** those timeframes are AusNet's; Victoria's other gas distributors (Australian Gas Networks and Multinet Gas Networks) may differ. Check your appliance path first in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
