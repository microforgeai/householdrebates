## Timeline of Victorian rental minimum standards (2027–2030)

This page is a plain-English summary of the currently legislated 2027–2030 rental standards for rental providers and property managers, **not legal advice**—refer to Consumer Affairs Victoria for full regulatory obligations (<SourceLink id="cav-min-energy-standards" label="Consumer Affairs Victoria — New minimum energy efficiency standards" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-rental-efficiency" label="Energy Victoria — Energy efficiency for rental properties" />).

| Rental standard | Compliance trigger and start date | Minimum technical specification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ceiling insulation** | New lease from **1 March 2027** (where no ceiling insulation is present) | At least **R5.0** ceiling insulation (<a href="/victoria/ceiling-insulation-rebate/">ceiling insulation guide</a>) |
| **Showerheads** | New lease from **1 March 2027** | **4-star WELS** water-efficiency rating |
| **Hot water** | From **1 March 2027**, when an existing water heater fails beyond repair | Energy-efficient electric: heat pump or electric-boosted solar (<a href="/victoria/gas-hot-water-to-heat-pump/">gas-to-heat-pump guide</a>) |
| **Main living area heating** | From **1 March 2027**, when the existing fixed heater fails beyond repair | Reverse-cycle air conditioner: **2-star+ GEMS** heating rating (non-ducted) or **HSPF ≥ 3.2** (ducted) for the climate area |
| **Main living area cooling** | New lease from **1 March 2027**; **all rentals from 1 July 2030** | Fixed cooling: **3-star+ GEMS** cooling rating (non-ducted) or **TCSPF ≥ 3.8** (ducted) |
| **Draught proofing** | New lease from **1 July 2027** | Weather sealing external doors, windows, and unused chimneys/vents (<a href="/victoria/draught-proofing-rebate/">weather sealing discounts by home type</a>) |

## VEU and Solar Homes discounts rental providers can claim

Consumer Affairs Victoria confirms that upgrades installed to meet these minimum rental standards are eligible for **Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU)** discounts (<SourceLink id="cav-min-energy-standards" label="Consumer Affairs Victoria — Minimum energy efficiency standards" />). Energy Victoria's rental properties guide highlights the following indicative incentives (<SourceLink id="energyvic-rental-efficiency" label="Energy Victoria — Energy efficiency for rental properties" />):

- **Room gas heater to split-system reverse-cycle air conditioner:** <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" />.
- **Ducted gas heater to a large 22.4 kW ducted reverse-cycle system:** <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" /> (note that this upper figure applies specifically to a **22.4 kW** ducted replacement; smaller 10 kW and 16 kW ducted systems receive smaller discounts—see <a href="/victoria/ducted-gas-to-ducted-reverse-cycle/">all ducted sizes</a>).
- **Inefficient gas water heater to a heat pump:** <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" />.
- **Solar Homes rooftop solar PV for rentals:** <Money id="vic-solar-homes-pv-rebate" /> on **up to 2 rental properties per financial year** (subject to Solar Victoria's rental provider eligibility rules).

See how tenants and landlords coordinate upgrades on our <a href="/victoria/renters/">Victorian renters guide</a>.

## Summary of exemptions (check CAV for details)

Consumer Affairs Victoria outlines exemptions where installing a standard upgrade is impractical or unlawful (<SourceLink id="cav-min-energy-standards" label="Consumer Affairs Victoria — Minimum energy efficiency standards" />), including:

- Properties served by **centralised** heating, cooling, or hot water systems (such as many multi-storey apartment buildings).
- Buildings with **no suitable outdoor space** to mount a heat pump or air-conditioner compressor.
- **Heritage overlays** or **owners-corporation rules** that prohibit the external installation.

Always check Consumer Affairs Victoria's official guidance for the documentation required to rely on an exemption, and run your rental property's existing appliances through the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
