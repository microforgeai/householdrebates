## Which VEU upgrades cover bottled LPG homes?

Households on 45 kg LPG cylinders in regional townships, outer-fringe acreage, and off-mains pockets are covered under the same Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) activity schedules as natural-gas homes for water heating, room space heating, and cooking. You do not need a reticulated street gas meter to participate — your installer records the existing LPG appliance or LPG connection before decommissioning or installing the replacement product.

Run the [Victorian VEU eligibility checker](/victoria/eligibility-checker/) first to match your postcode, home age, and existing LPG appliances against current VEU and Solar Homes rules.

## VEU hot water discounts for LPG homes

Essential Services Commission (ESC) VEU **Part 3** covers installing an electric-boosted solar or heat pump water heater and decommissioning an existing **gas or LPG water heater** ([ESC overview of VEU activities](https://www.esc.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades-program/about-victorian-energy-upgrades-program/overview-veu-activities)). That means replacing a bottled-LPG storage or instantaneous water heater with an approved heat pump or electric-boosted solar system creates Victorian Energy Efficiency Certificates (VEECs) under VEU Specifications v25.0.

However, Energy Victoria's household [hot water system discounts guide](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades/products/hot-water-system-discounts) publishes an indicative figure only for replacing a mains-gas water heater with a heat pump (<Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" />). Energy Victoria does not publish a separate household dollar figure for decommissioning an LPG water heater — **the accredited provider confirms** your indicative VEEC discount on the written quote based on the tank model, your postcode's VEU heat pump zone, and the live certificate price. Every VEU Part 3 upgrade also carries a mandatory <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> customer co-contribution.

Compare tank sizing, cold-climate Frost-protection checks, and Solar Victoria stacking rules in [replacing gas hot water with a heat pump](/victoria/gas-hot-water-to-heat-pump/) and [solar hot water rebates in Victoria](/victoria/solar-hot-water-rebate/).

## Replacing an LPG room heater or LPG cooktop

Two household VEU activities publish clear indicative figures that apply to LPG homes:

- **LPG room heaters to reverse-cycle split systems:** Energy Victoria's [heating and cooling discounts guide](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades/products/heating-and-cooling-discounts) defines a non-ducted gas heater to include **natural gas or LPG**. Decommissioning a fixed flued or unflued LPG room heater and installing a single 3 to 9 kW split system attracts an indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> in a home more than two years old, with a minimum customer co-payment of <Money id="vic-veu-copay-split-under-10kw" /> (GST inclusive) for single non-ducted systems below 10 kW. See [replacing a gas room heater with a split system](/victoria/gas-room-heater-to-split-system/) for decommissioning steps.
- **Induction cooktops at LPG homes (Part 46):** Under VEU Specifications v25.0 Part 46 and Energy Victoria's [induction cooktop discounts guide](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades/products/induction-cooktop-discounts), premises with an existing **gas or LPG connection** can claim an indicative <Money id="vic-veu-induction" /> upfront discount per eligible induction cooktop, subject to a <Money id="vic-veu-copay-induction" /> minimum customer co-payment. Read [switching from a gas cooktop to induction](/victoria/gas-cooktop-to-induction/) for switchboard and circuit checks before ordering.

Off-mains households shifting hot water, heating, and cooking onto electricity often pair these upgrades with rooftop solar and storage; compare current eligibility in our [Victorian solar panel rebate guide](/victoria/solar-panel-rebate/) and [federal home battery rebate guide](/victoria/battery-rebate/).

## The 1 March 2027 gas hot water rule (reg 45E) and LPG

Under [Building and Plumbing Amendment Regulations 2025](https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/as-made/statutory-rules/building-and-plumbing-amendment-regulations-2025) regulation 45E ([AustLII section 6 text](https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/num_reg/bapar2025n140o2025463/s6.html)) as currently legislated, from 1 March 2027 the end-of-life replacement restriction applies only to a gas water heater **connected to reticulated (mains) gas** in an existing Class 1 or Class 2 home.

- **Bottled LPG (45 kg cylinders):** A bottled-LPG water heater is not connected to reticulated gas. Under the regulation as currently legislated, bottled-LPG homes sit outside the 1 March 2027 restriction and are not required to switch to electric hot water when a cylinder-fed unit fails.
- **Piped-LPG estates:** Some regional subdivisions and lifestyle estates run an LPG network piped to the street rather than individual cylinders at the wall. If your estate has piped LPG, your plumber confirms whether the rule applies to your water heater.

For situations where a reticulated-gas home cannot fit an electric unit cleanly, read [gas hot water ban exemptions in Victoria](/victoria/gas-hot-water-ban-exemptions/).

## Which Victorian postcodes have no reticulated mains gas?

Across Victoria, <Money id="vic-veu-table-a-no-gas-postcodes" /> of the <Money id="vic-veu-table-a-total-postcodes" /> postcodes in [VEU Specifications v25.0](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0041/795488/Victorian-Energy-Upgrades-Specifications-2018-Version-25.pdf) Table A are recorded with no reticulated mains gas (`reticulated_gas = No`). Those postcodes span semi-rural ranges such as parts of [Macedon Ranges](/victoria/macedon-ranges/), northern corridor townships, Gippsland rural localities, and Mallee river towns.
