## Victoria's 1 January 2027 all-electric standard for new builds

Under Victoria's building electrification rules (<SourceLink id="energyvic-new-buildings" label="Energy Victoria — New buildings (residential and most commercial)" />):

- **New homes and knock-down rebuilds:** Must be **all-electric** where the **building permit is issued on or after 1 January 2027** (also covering most new commercial buildings), subject to a limited transitional exception where substantial design progress has been certified.
- **Existing buildings without mains gas:** Cannot connect to the reticulated gas network from **1 January 2027**. (For homes that already have mains gas, see how the separate March 2027 hot water rule works in <a href="/victoria/gas-to-electric-hot-water-2027/">our 2027 gas-to-electric hot water guide</a>.)
- **Estimated annual bill savings:** Energy Victoria estimates that a new all-electric home saves <Money id="vic-new-home-electric-saving" /> a year on energy bills compared with a dual-fuel home, rising to <Money id="vic-new-home-electric-solar-saving" /> a year when paired with rooftop solar (<SourceLink id="energyvic-new-buildings" label="Energy Victoria — New buildings" />).

## Why homes under 2 years old miss most VEU replacement discounts

Homeowners in fast-growing outer-suburban estates (such as <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/wyndham/">Wyndham</LinkIfEnabled>, Casey, and Whittlesea) often wonder why a 1-year-old house cannot claim the large heating or hot water VEU discounts:

- **The 2-year building-age rule:** VEU household replacement discounts for heating/cooling and hot water require your home to be **more than 2 years old** (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />).
- **Small VEU discount with no heater decommissioned:** For a new build installing a reverse-cycle air conditioner where no old heater is removed, Energy Victoria lists a typical VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-no-decomm" /> (<SourceLink id="energyvic-new-buildings" label="Energy Victoria — New buildings" />; see <a href="/victoria/air-conditioner-rebate/">Air Conditioner Rebate Victoria</a>).

## Solar Homes rebate for homes under construction

Eligible owner-occupiers building an all-electric home can apply for the **Solar Homes solar panel (PV) rebate** of <Money id="vic-solar-homes-pv-rebate" /> (plus an optional matching interest-free loan) **while the home is under construction**, with **270 days** from approval to complete the solar installation (<SourceLink id="energyvic-new-buildings" label="Energy Victoria — New buildings" />; <SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Solar panel rebate" />).
