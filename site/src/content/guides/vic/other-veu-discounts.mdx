## Smaller VEU discounts beyond heating, hot water, and insulation

Most Victorian households claim the largest [VEEC certificate](/victoria/what-is-a-veec/) discounts on reverse-cycle air conditioning, heat pump hot water, and [ceiling insulation](/victoria/ceiling-insulation-rebate/). However, Energy Victoria and the Essential Services Commission (ESC) also list several smaller thermal-shell and appliance activities under the Victorian Energy Upgrades program (<SourceLink id="energyvic-veu-products" label="Energy Victoria — VEU products" />; <SourceLink id="esc-veu-activities" label="ESC — Overview of VEU activities" />).

Because each item creates only a small number of certificates (often a single VEEC on appliances), fewer accredited providers actively market them, and appliance discounts are typically offered at point of sale through participating retail chains rather than by booking a home tradesperson.

## Window glazing discounts (double and secondary glazing)

Under VEU Parts 13 and 14, replacing inefficient single-glazed windows with approved thermal window products or fitting compliant secondary glazing earns a modest discount per square metre of glass (<SourceLink id="energyvic-window-glazing" label="Energy Victoria — Window glazing discounts" />):

### Extra eligibility rules on refrigerators and freezers

Under Energy Victoria's refrigerator and freezer guidance (<SourceLink id="energyvic-fridge-freezer" label="Energy Victoria — Refrigerator and freezer discounts" />), an eligible unit must sit on the ESC VEU product register **and** meet both size and star-rating floors:

- **Total volume:** **250 L to 700 L**.
- **Minimum energy rating:** **5.5 stars or higher** for a refrigerator, or **4 stars or higher** for a standalone freezer.
- **Kitchen electrification:** If you are upgrading kitchen appliances more broadly, see our [gas cooktop to induction discount guide](/victoria/gas-cooktop-to-induction/) as well.

<Callout tone="info" title="Confirm point-of-sale participation before buying">
  VEU appliance discounts must be arranged through a participating retailer or accredited provider at purchase time (<SourceLink id="energyvic-veu-products" label="Energy Victoria" />). Buying a high-star fridge, dryer, or TV from a non-participating store cannot be claimed back afterwards.
</Callout>

## Which of these VEU activities also apply to businesses?

Under the Essential Services Commission's activity schedule (<SourceLink id="esc-veu-activities" label="ESC — Overview of VEU activities" />), almost all of these smaller VEU activities are open to **both residential and non-residential** premises, with one residential-only exception (while larger commercial plant is covered separately under <a href="/victoria/business-led-lighting/">business LED lighting</a> and <a href="/victoria/business-cold-rooms-refrigeration/">walk-in cold rooms and commercial refrigeration</a>):

| VEU activity (ESC part) | Households (residential) | Businesses (non-residential) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Window glazing — double and secondary glazing** (Parts 13 & 14) | Yes (<Money id="vic-veu-glazing-double" /> / <Money id="vic-veu-glazing-secondary" /> per 10 m²) | Yes — open to non-residential buildings |
| **Water-efficient shower rose / shower head** (Part 17) | Yes (<Money id="vic-veu-appliance-showerhead" />) | Yes — open to gyms, hotels, and workplaces |
| **High-efficiency refrigerators and freezers** (Part 22) | Yes (<Money id="vic-veu-appliance-fridge" />) | Yes — domestic-sized 250–700 L register models |
| **High-efficiency televisions** (Part 24) | Yes (<Money id="vic-veu-appliance-tv" />) | Yes — open to non-residential premises |
| **Clothes dryers** (Part 25) | Yes (<Money id="vic-veu-appliance-dryer" />) | Yes — ESC lists Part 25 for both sectors |
| **High-efficiency pool pumps** (Part 26) | Yes (no published dollar example; provider confirms) | Yes — ESC lists Part 26 for both sectors |
| **In-home energy display units** (Part 30) | Yes (<Money id="vic-veu-appliance-ihd" />) | **No** — residential premises only |

## No longer offered: Residential Efficiency Scorecard

The VEU **Residential Efficiency Scorecard** home energy assessment activity (Part 45) **closed on 23 June 2026** and is no longer available for VEU discounts or certificates (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0, Part 45" />; <SourceLink id="esc-veu-activities" label="ESC — Overview of VEU activities" />). While accredited Scorecard assessors still exist outside the VEU certificate scheme, VEU no longer subsidises home Scorecard assessments — you can instead check which VEU product upgrades suit your home using our [Victoria Eligibility Checker](/victoria/eligibility-checker/).
