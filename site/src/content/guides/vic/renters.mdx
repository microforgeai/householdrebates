## How VEU works for renters and private rentals

Private rental properties across Victoria can access Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) discounts on heating and cooling, hot water, ceiling insulation, and weather sealing when tenants and rental providers work together (<SourceLink id="energyvic-renters-landlords" label="Energy Victoria — Renters, landlords and body corporate" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />).

- **Check with your rental provider first:** Because replacing a fixed heater (such as <a href="/victoria/gas-room-heater-to-split-system/">swapping a gas room heater for a split system</a>), changing a hot water unit, or adding ceiling insulation alters the property, written permission from your landlord is needed before any VEU accredited provider starts work.
- **Note on Solar Homes hot water:** Unlike VEU, Solar Victoria's **Solar Homes hot water rebate** is generally restricted to owner-occupiers (<SourceLink id="solarvic-hot-water-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Hot water rebate" />), with a specific exception for affected properties in <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/solstice-gas-shutdown-towns/">Solstice Energy gas shutdown towns</LinkIfEnabled> (<SourceLink id="solarvic-solstice" label="Solar Victoria — Supporting Solstice customers" />).
- **Bill concessions still apply directly to renters:** If you hold an eligible concession card and your name is on the electricity or gas account, see our guide for <a href="/victoria/concession-card-holders/">concession card holders in Victoria</a>.

## Changes renters can make without or with reasonable-change rules

Consumer Affairs Victoria sets out clear rules on energy-saving changes in rented homes (<SourceLink id="cav-renter-changes" label="Consumer Affairs Victoria — Renters making changes to the property" />):

- **No permission required:** Installing LED light bulbs that do not need new light fittings, fitting a low-flow showerhead (provided you store the original showerhead and refit it when moving out), and applying removable non-permanent window film.
- **Draught proofing ("reasonable change"):** In a rental property **without** gas appliances, draught proofing (<a href="/victoria/draught-proofing-rebate/">see VEU weather sealing</a>) is classed as a reasonable change that a rental provider **cannot refuse without a good reason**. In a property **with** gas appliances, a gas safety check is required first to prevent carbon monoxide hazards.

## What your landlord must upgrade from 2027 (currently legislated)

Under currently legislated rules from **1 March 2027**, Victoria's minimum rental energy efficiency standards require staged upgrades across ceiling insulation, 4-star WELS showerheads, main living area cooling, and end-of-life replacements of failed hot water and main living area heaters, followed by draught proofing from **1 July 2027** (<SourceLink id="cav-min-energy-standards" label="Consumer Affairs Victoria — New minimum energy efficiency standards" />).
