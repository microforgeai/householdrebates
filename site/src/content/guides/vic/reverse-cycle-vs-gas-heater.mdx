## Why "provider before work starts" is the golden rule of VEU

Energy Victoria states clearly on its product and program pages that households should contact an accredited provider **before purchasing or installing** any product, and that **retrospective discounts are typically not possible**. If you hire your own electrician or plumber, that tradesperson must already work with a VEU accredited provider **before any work starts**.

Why does timing matter so much? Because the accredited provider must collect specific geo-tagged photographs, serial numbers, and compliance declarations **before** the existing gas or electric heater is disconnected and removed. Once an old heater is on the nature strip or taken away without pre-installation evidence, the upgrade cannot be claimed as a heater replacement.

## Decommissioning vs installing without decommissioning

The difference in VEU certificate value between decommissioning an existing gas heater and installing a new air conditioner without decommissioning an existing heater is substantial:

- **Room split system (3–9 kW) replacing and decommissioning a room gas heater:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> (see our <a href="/victoria/gas-room-heater-to-split-system/">gas room heater to split-system swap guide</a>).
- **Room split system installed without decommissioning an existing heater:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-no-decomm" />.
- **22.4 kW ducted reverse-cycle replacing and decommissioning a ducted gas heater:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" />.
- **Ducted reverse-cycle installed without decommissioning an existing heater:** indicative discount of <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-no-decomm" />.

Keep in mind that from 30 September 2026 under VEU Specifications v25.0, any ducted reverse-cycle air conditioner also requires a minimum customer co-payment of <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" /> per installed product. See the full breakdown of co-payment tiers in our <a href="/victoria/air-conditioner-rebate/#the-minimum-you-pay-from-30-september-2026">air conditioner rebate co-payment guide</a>.

In addition, when replacing an existing heater, the building must be **more than 2 years old**.

<Callout type="warning" title="Already bought a unit in a retail sale? Stop before installing">
  If you have already purchased an approved reverse-cycle unit from a retailer but have not yet installed it, contact a VEU accredited provider before booking installation. Once installation work starts without a provider involved, <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/already-installed-retrospective-discount/">a retrospective VEU discount</LinkIfEnabled> is generally not possible.
</Callout>

## Rental minimum standards from 1 March 2027

For Victorian landlords and tenants:

- Renters can access VEU heating and cooling discounts today, provided they have the **landlord's approval** before work starts.
- From **1 March 2027**, when a fixed heater in a rental home's main living area breaks down and cannot be repaired, the landlord must replace it with an energy-efficient reverse-cycle air conditioner.
- From **1 March 2027**, rental properties must also have efficient cooling in the main living area at the start of a new lease.
