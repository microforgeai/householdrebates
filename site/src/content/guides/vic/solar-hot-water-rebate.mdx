## Replacing a broken solar hot water system

If your existing rooftop solar water heater has failed, Energy Victoria explicitly lists **broken, out-of-warranty solar hot water systems** (whether electric-boosted or gas-boosted) among the systems eligible to be replaced with an approved heat pump or new solar-boosted system. Energy Victoria also notes that some older gas-boosted and electric-boosted solar heaters count as inefficient systems under the VEU schedule—your accredited provider checks your existing model's eligibility before quoting.

## Electric-boosted vs gas-boosted solar and locally made products

When choosing between electric-boosted and gas-boosted solar hot water:

- **2027 rental and building standards:** Under Victoria's rental energy efficiency standards from **1 March 2027** (<SourceLink id="energyvic-rental-efficiency" label="Energy Victoria — Energy efficiency for rental properties" />), when an existing rental water heater breaks down and cannot be repaired, the rental provider must install an energy-efficient electric system—specifically a **heat pump or an electric-boosted solar water heater** (see <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/landlords-rental-standards-2027/">Victorian landlord 2027 standards</LinkIfEnabled>).
- **Solar Victoria locally made list:** On Solar Victoria's hot water rebate page (<SourceLink id="solarvic-hot-water-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Hot water rebate" />), the list of products eligible for the locally made incentive includes selected Dux electric-boosted solar models (listed by Solar Victoria alongside locally made heat pumps; check Solar Victoria's current product list rather than treating this as an endorsement).
