## Current Solar Homes solar panel rebate and interest-free loan

For residential homes in [Victoria](/victoria/), rooftop solar panels are currently supported through **Solar Victoria's Solar Homes Program**, separate from Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) (<SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Solar panel (PV) rebate" />):

- **Upfront solar panel (PV) rebate:** <Money id="vic-solar-homes-pv-rebate" />, deducted from your invoice once Solar Victoria pre-approves your application.
- **Optional interest-free loan:** Eligible applicants can also apply for an interest-free loan matching their rebate amount (<Money id="vic-solar-homes-pv-rebate" />), repaid over four years, to reduce upfront out-of-pocket costs (<SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Solar panel (PV) rebate" />).
- **Federal STCs still apply:** Solar Victoria's rebate sits on top of the Australian Government Small-scale Technology Certificate (STC) discount that solar retailers already include on standard residential PV quotes, and can be paired with the federal [home battery discount](/victoria/battery-rebate/) when adding an eligible battery.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Get Solar Victoria pre-approval before any installation starts">
  Your Solar Victoria-authorised solar retailer must upload a written quote to the Solar Homes portal, and you must verify your identity, income, and property eligibility **before** panels are installed (<SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-rebate" label="Solar Victoria" />). Retrospective claims after installation are not accepted.
</Callout>

## Who is eligible in 2026–27

Solar Victoria checks five main household and property rules on owner-occupier applications (<SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Solar panel (PV) rebate" />; <SourceLink id="solarvic-income-cap-news" label="Solar Victoria — Solar Homes targeted assistance" />):

1. **Owner-occupier (existing home or home under construction):** You own and live in the Victorian property, or you are the owner-occupier of a home currently under construction.
2. **Combined household taxable income under <Money id="vic-solar-homes-income-cap" />:** Reduced from <Money id="vic-solar-homes-income-cap-until-jun-2026" /> on **1 July 2026** so Solar Homes targets low- and middle-income households (<SourceLink id="solarvic-income-cap-news" label="Solar Victoria" />). Affected Solstice Energy compressed natural gas (CNG) customers remain exempt from this income cap (<SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-rebate" label="Solar Victoria" />).
3. **Property valued under <Money id="vic-solar-homes-property-cap" />:** For homes under construction, this is assessed on the combined land and build contract value (<SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-rebate" label="Solar Victoria" />; <SourceLink id="solarvic-hot-water-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Hot water rebate" />).
4. **No prior Solar Homes PV or battery rebate at the address:** Your household has not already claimed a Solar Homes solar panel rebate (or an earlier Solar Victoria battery rebate) at this property.
5. **10-year existing-system rule:** The property has not had a solar PV system installed in the last **10 years** (households with a system older than 10 years can apply to replace or expand it).
6. **Approved product and installer:** Panels and inverters must be on Solar Victoria's approved product lists and installed by a Solar Accreditation Australia accredited installer working with an authorised retailer.

Compare how these caps differ from uncapped VEU heating and hot water discounts in [VEU vs Solar Homes](/victoria/veu-vs-solar-homes/).

### Rental properties and apartments

- **Rental homes:** Solar Victoria also offers solar panel rebates for eligible Victorian rental properties, and the <Money id="vic-solar-homes-income-cap" /> combined household taxable income cap applies to rental applications from 1 July 2026 (<SourceLink id="solarvic-income-cap-news" label="Solar Victoria" />). Check <SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-rebate" label="Solar Victoria's solar panel rebate page" /> directly for current landlord-renter application steps.
- **Apartments and multi-unit buildings:** Strata buildings and units that cannot fit individual rooftop systems can apply through **Solar for Apartments** instead — see our [apartments and units rebate guide](/victoria/apartments-and-units/) for per-unit and per-building caps.

## What changes on 1 July 2027 (moving into VEU)

Under Solar Victoria's published transition announcement, residential rooftop solar support is scheduled to move from Solar Homes into **Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU)** from **1 July 2027** (<SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-into-veu" label="Solar Victoria — Solar support to continue through VEU" />):

- **Until 30 June 2027:** Current Solar Homes solar panel rebates (<Money id="vic-solar-homes-pv-rebate" />) and optional interest-free loans continue under existing Solar Victoria rules.
- **From 1 July 2027:** Under currently announced Victorian Government policy, residential solar PV incentives shift into VEU alongside heat pumps, air conditioners, and insulation (<SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-into-veu" label="Solar Victoria" />). Detailed residential VEU solar certificate calculations and co-payment rules from July 2027 have not yet been published.

## Building a new home and Residential Electrification Grants

- **Homes under construction:** Owner-occupiers building a new home can apply for the Solar Homes PV rebate before handover if they meet the income and <Money id="vic-solar-homes-property-cap" /> land-plus-build value limits (<SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-rebate" label="Solar Victoria" />). See [Victoria's 2027 all-electric new-home rules](/victoria/new-homes-all-electric-2027/) for how solar pairs with electric heating and hot water on new builds.
- **Residential Electrification Grants (approved providers only):** Separate from individual Solar Homes applications, Solar Victoria funds **Residential Electrification Grants** delivered in bulk through approved builders, networks, and project providers — offering <Money id="vic-electrification-grants-pv" /> towards solar PV and <Money id="vic-electrification-grants-hw" /> towards energy-efficient hot water for participating metro and regional households, including vulnerable and low-income households, retirement-home residents, and first home buyers (<SourceLink id="solarvic-electrification-grants" label="Solar Victoria — Residential Electrification Grants" />). Households apply through a participating provider on Solar Victoria's list rather than lodging a standalone Solar Homes claim.

## Commercial solar (30 to 200 kW) for businesses

Small, medium, and larger businesses cannot claim residential Solar Homes rebates, but VEU already offers commercial solar PV discounts for **30 to 200 kW** rooftop systems on non-residential premises (<SourceLink id="energyvic-solar-pv" label="Energy Victoria — Solar PV system discounts for businesses" />). See our [Victorian business energy upgrades hub](/victoria/business-energy-upgrades/) for commercial solar examples, or open the [Victoria Eligibility Checker](/victoria/eligibility-checker/) to check VEU upgrades for your home or business.
