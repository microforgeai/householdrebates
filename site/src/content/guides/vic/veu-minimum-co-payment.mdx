Victorian Energy Upgrades Specifications 2018 Version 25.0 sets mandatory minimum customer co-payments (all including GST) across household heating and cooling, ceiling insulation, and induction cooktops (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />):

Before **30 September 2026**, the minimum co-payment for a household ducted reverse-cycle air conditioner was <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted-until-sep-2026" />. From 30 September 2026 under Part 6 of the specifications, the exact GST-inclusive bands per installed product are:

- **Every ducted system of any capacity:** <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" />.
- **Multi-splits of 10 kW or more:** <Money id="vic-veu-copay-multisplit-10kw-plus" />.

- **Single non-ducted systems of 10 kW or more:** <Money id="vic-veu-copay-nonducted-10kw-plus" />.
- **Single non-ducted split systems under 10 kW:** <Money id="vic-veu-copay-split-under-10kw" />.

## Hot water and commercial minimum contributions

For household hot water upgrades (VEU activities 1 and 3—electric-to-heat-pump and gas-to-heat-pump or solar water heaters), the minimum customer payment is <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> per product including GST (<SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" />; <SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />).

Energy Victoria also notes that larger commercial and industrial upgrades carry a <Money id="vic-veu-copay-business-large" /> minimum co-payment (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />)—that threshold applies only to large business projects, not residential homes.

## Why "zero-cost ducted" offers are a red flag

Because <a href="/victoria/what-is-a-veec/">VEEC discounts</a> are deducted from the supplied-and-installed price **subject to** the mandatory co-payment floor:

- **No genuine zero-cost ducted system:** A legitimate VEU provider cannot legally supply and install a household ducted reverse-cycle air conditioner without collecting <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" /> from the customer.
- **No cashback or rebates of the co-payment:** Accredited providers and installers are prohibited from reimbursing, rebating, or crediting back the mandatory minimum co-payment after installation.
- **Report suspicious marketing:** If a marketer contacts you uninvited or promises an upgrade with nothing to pay, read our guide to <a href="/victoria/veu-scams-cold-calls/">VEU scams, cold-call bans, and how to report non-compliance</a>, and check genuine rules first with our <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
