## Cold calls and doorknocking are banned under VEU

Under Victorian Energy Upgrades consumer protection laws enforced by the Essential Services Commission (<SourceLink id="esc-cold-call-bans" label="ESC — Cold-call telemarketing and doorknocking bans" />):

- **Telemarketing cold calls** have been banned since **1 May 2024**.
- **Unsolicited doorknocking** has been banned since **1 August 2024**.

An accredited provider, installer, or marketer may only call or visit your home if you gave **express prior consent** first—see <a href="/how-quotes-work/">how VEU quotes and consent rules work</a>. For clarity, **Household Rebates is an independent information guide (<a href="/about/">about us</a>), not a government website, and we never cold-call or doorknock**.

## Red flags and what VEU marketers must tell you

Under the VEU code of conduct (<SourceLink id="esc-consumer-rights" label="ESC — Your consumer rights in the VEU program" />), anyone marketing or quoting a VEU upgrade:

- **Must not** claim to work for the Victorian Energy Upgrades program, the Essential Services Commission, or the Victorian Government.
- **Must not** tell you that a VEU upgrade is compulsory or urgent government work—they must tell you that participation is **voluntary**.
- **Must not** advertise a zero-dollar household hot water, air-conditioning, insulation, or induction upgrade that ignores <a href="/victoria/veu-minimum-co-payment/">the mandatory VEU minimum co-payment</a>.

Before agreeing to any quote, you can verify accredited businesses yourself using our guide on <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/find-an-accredited-provider/">how to find and check a VEU accredited provider</LinkIfEnabled> or test your home's setup in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.

## How complaints work and where to report breaches

Accredited providers must acknowledge consumer complaints within **5 business days** and aim to resolve them within **20 business days** (<SourceLink id="esc-consumer-rights" label="ESC — Your consumer rights in the VEU program" />).

If you receive an illegal cold call or doorknock, or cannot resolve an issue with a provider, contact the regulators and interpreter service listed on official pages (<SourceLink id="energyvic-your-rights" label="Energy Victoria — Your rights" />; <SourceLink id="esc-report-non-compliance" label="ESC — Report non-compliance in the VEU program" />; <SourceLink id="solarvic-community-languages" label="Solar Victoria — Community languages" />):

- **Essential Services Commission (VEU compliance and cold-call/doorknock reports):** Phone `(03) 9032 1310` or email `veu@esc.vic.gov.au`
- **National Translating and Interpreting Service (TIS National — phone interpreter):** Phone `131 450` (see <a href="/victoria/help-in-your-language/">Help in your language</a> for translated VEU and Solar Homes guides)
- **Australian Do Not Call Register:** Phone `1300 792 958`
