## Side-by-side comparison: VEU vs Solar Homes

| Feature | Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) | Solar Homes Program |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **How it is funded** | Market-based <a href="/victoria/what-is-a-veec/">VEEC certificates</a> sold to energy retailers | Victorian Government rebate administered by Solar Victoria |
| **How you claim** | Upfront point-of-sale discount via a VEU accredited provider | Apply for Solar Victoria pre-approval **before** installation |
| **Household income cap** | No income test on VEU product pages (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling" />) | Under <Money id="vic-solar-homes-income-cap" /> from 1 July 2026 (was <Money id="vic-solar-homes-income-cap-until-jun-2026" /> until 30 June 2026) |
| **Property value cap** | No property value cap on VEU product pages | Property valued under <Money id="vic-solar-homes-property-cap" /> |
| **Renters and rentals** | Eligible when tenant and landlord agree | Hot water rebate is for owner-occupiers; separate rental solar PV settings exist |
| **Main household upgrades** | Reverse-cycle air con, heat pump & solar hot water, insulation, induction cooktops, draught proofing, glazing | Hot water systems and rooftop solar panels (PV) |

## Solar Homes 2026–27 caps and hot water stacking

From **1 July 2026**, the combined household taxable income cap for Solar Homes fell to <Money id="vic-solar-homes-income-cap" /> (down from <Money id="vic-solar-homes-income-cap-until-jun-2026" />), alongside a property value limit under <Money id="vic-solar-homes-property-cap" />, while the Victorian Government funded an extra <Money id="vic-solar-homes-hw-extra-2026-27" /> hot water rebates for 2026–27 (<SourceLink id="solarvic-income-cap-news" label="Solar Victoria — Targeted assistance update" />; <SourceLink id="solarvic-hot-water-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Hot water rebate" />).

Eligible owner-occupiers upgrading hot water can combine both programs on the same system—see <a href="/victoria/can-you-claim-veu-twice/">how VEU and Solar Homes stack together</a> and our <a href="/victoria/heat-pump-rebate/">Heat Pump Hot Water Rebate Victoria guide</a>. Apartment owners and owners corporations can also compare shared options in our <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/apartments-and-units/">apartments and units guide</LinkIfEnabled>.

## What happens to rooftop solar PV rebates in July 2027?

Under current Solar Homes settings, eligible households can apply for a solar panel (PV) rebate of <Money id="vic-solar-homes-pv-rebate" /> plus an optional matching interest-free loan (<SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-rebate" label="Solar Victoria — Solar panel (PV) rebate" />).

Solar Victoria has announced that rooftop solar PV support will move into the **Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU)** program from **1 July 2027**, while existing Solar Homes solar PV rebate and loan arrangements stay in place until **30 June 2027** (<SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-into-veu" label="Solar Victoria — Solar support to continue through VEU" />).
