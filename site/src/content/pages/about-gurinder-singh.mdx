## Editorial responsibility

**Household Rebates** (`householdrebates.com.au`) is owned and operated by **Agenor Services Pty Ltd** (ABN `29 644 447 263`) in Melbourne, Victoria. All guides, calculator rules, and source checks across our [Victorian home energy rebates hub](/victoria/) are edited by **Gurinder Singh** (Editor, Household Rebates; director, Agenor Services Pty Ltd).

We are an independent private publication. We do not work for or represent the Victorian Government, Energy Victoria, Solar Victoria, the Essential Services Commission (ESC), or any energy retailer (<SourceLink id="energyvic-about-veu" label="Energy Victoria — About VEU" />).

## How figures and rules are checked

Many consumer rebate sites mix up gas-to-heat-pump discounts with electric-to-heat-pump discounts, quote stale figures without dates, or state future regulations without noting legislative status. Our publishing pipeline enforces five verification rules before any build succeeds:

1. **Single structured figure registry:** Every dollar amount, percentage, and program count renders from one central registry rather than hand-typed prose, with a direct link to the primary government source and a visible checked-on date.
2. **45-day freshness cap:** Automated build checks fail if any figure, FAQ entry, or tool assumption is more than 45 days past its last check date (`MAX_FIGURE_AGE_DAYS=45`).
3. **Primary specifications beat summary pages:** Where an overview page compresses details, we verify exact activity rules against the published VEU Specifications, Building and Plumbing Amendment Regulations, or regulator guidelines listed on our [Official Sources and Method](/sources/) page.
4. **Election and rule-change tracking:** Rules taking effect in 2027 carry explicit `currently legislated` wording and an election-watch notice (<SourceLink id="energyvic-caretaker-banner" label="Energy Victoria caretaker notice" />) scheduled for re-verification after the 28 November 2026 Victorian state election.
5. **Indicative framing:** Every VEU amount is labelled indicative because the accredited provider confirms eligibility and the final point-of-sale discount in writing before work begins (<SourceLink id="esc-veu-code" label="ESC — VEU Code of Conduct" />).

## Technical review policy

A technical reviewer line appears under a page's byline (and in structured `WebPage` metadata) **only** when a licensed practitioner — such as a licensed plumber, licensed electrician, or accredited energy assessor — has signed a written review agreement covering that page. When a technical review is published, the page displays the reviewer's full name, verifiable licence type and number, review date, and an explicit disclosure of whether they are also a participating paid installer. Until a reviewer signs for a page, no unnamed expert-review claim is shown.

## How the site is paid for and conflict disclosures

Using our guides and the [Victoria Eligibility Checker](/victoria/eligibility-checker/) requires no contact details and carries no charge to households or businesses. As disclosed in the notice at the top of every page and on [About Us and How We Are Paid](/about/):

- **Named installer enquiries (when open):** When Agenor Services Pty Ltd holds a signed agreement with one to three vetted installers who partner with a named VEU accredited provider (<SourceLink id="esc-leadgen-factsheet" label="ESC — Lead generation fact sheet" />), visitors may choose to request a quote. Participating installers may pay us a flat fee per consented enquiry (<SourceLink id="oaic-trading" label="OAIC — Trading in personal information" />).
- **Quote form currently closed:** While no installer agreement is active for a postcode or upgrade activity, the quote form stays off and pages point directly to official government directories and [how VEU quotes and consumer rights work](/how-quotes-work/).
- **Editorial separation:** Installer partnerships never alter which government figures, minimum co-payments, or eligibility exclusions we publish.
