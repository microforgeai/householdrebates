## Who publishes Household Rebates

**Household Rebates** (`householdrebates.com.au`) is owned and operated by **Agenor Services Pty Ltd** (ABN `29 644 447 263`), based in Melbourne, Victoria. All guides and fact-checks are edited in-house by <AuthorName strong={true} fallback="the Agenor editorial team" currentPath="/about/" />.

We are an independent private publisher. We do not work for, represent, or receive endorsement from the Victorian Government, Energy Victoria, Solar Victoria, the Department of Energy, Environment and Climate Action (DEECA), the Essential Services Commission (ESC), the Australian Government, the Clean Energy Regulator or any council.

<h2 id="how-we-are-paid">How we are paid</h2>

Writing and maintaining accurate, source-checked guides takes time. Our business model is straightforward:

1. **No cost to households:** Reading our guides and using the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Eligibility Checker</a> is free and does not require entering your contact details.
2. **Named installer enquiries (when open):** When we have a signed written agreement with one to three signed installers covering your postcode, you can choose to submit a quote request form.
3. **Lead fee:** Participating installers pay Agenor Services Pty Ltd a flat fee for each valid, consented quote enquiry we pass to them.
4. **Strict consent boundaries:** We never sell your details to unnamed lead brokers, never pre-tick consent boxes, and always name the exact installer and their VEU accredited provider on the form before you decide whether to submit. While we have no signed installer for an activity or postcode, our form remains closed and points you directly to Energy Victoria's official directory.

## Editorial and fact-checking method

Many rebate websites confuse electric-to-heat-pump figures with gas-to-heat-pump figures, or advertise the maximum ducted gas replacement discount as if every split system qualifies for it. To prevent that:

- Every dollar figure on Household Rebates comes from a single structured data file. Most are copied directly from an official government publication. A few are our own clearly labelled estimates, calculated from official formulas, with the method shown next to them.
- Every figure displays the date we last checked it against the official page and a direct link to that source.
- An automated build check fails if any figure is more than 45 days old or if an unverified dollar figure appears anywhere in our templates or articles.

See our full list of primary documents on the <a href="/sources/">Sources and Method page</a>.
