## How this checker works

Our cascade applies the household and business rules published by Energy Victoria, Solar Victoria, the Clean Energy Regulator, DCCEEW, and VEU Specifications v25.0:

- **Step 1 — Residential or Business:** Routes your answers down either the household path (homes, units, apartments, and rentals) or the business path (commercial, industrial, agricultural, and apartment common areas).
- **Step 2 — Postcode lookup ("Your area" card):** Matches your four-digit Victorian postcode against the 719 postcodes in VEU Specifications v25.0 Table A to show your localities, council share, Metropolitan or Regional classification, reticulated mains gas status, climate region, and heat pump zone.
- **Residential VEU discounts:** Checks replacement and building-age rules across heating and cooling (such as <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" /> for a room gas heater to split system, or <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" /> for a 22.4 kW ducted swap alongside the <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" /> ducted minimum co-payment), hot water (<Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> replacing mains gas or <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" /> replacing electric storage, with a <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> minimum customer co-payment), ceiling insulation (<Money id="vic-veu-insulation-metro" /> in metropolitan mild zones, <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-regional" /> in regional cold zones, or our clearly labelled Part 48 estimate in mixed zones), induction cooktops (<Money id="vic-veu-induction" /> per eligible product), weather sealing (such as <Money id="vic-veu-sealing-house-5yr" /> for a house with 5-year warranty products), window glazing, clothes dryers, showerheads, and retail appliances.
- **Business VEU discounts:** Checks ESC Scheduled Activity Premises exclusions and maps selected commercial activities—heating and cooling, commercial or household-size hot water, LED lighting, cold rooms and refrigeration, commercial solar PV, electric motors, pre-rinse spray valves, and commercial appliances—to Energy Victoria's published business rules and minimum co-payments.
- **State Solar Homes and federal battery support:** Checks Victorian Solar Homes hot water (<Money id="vic-solar-homes-hw-rebate" /> standard rebate) and solar panel (<Money id="vic-solar-homes-pv-rebate" />) thresholds, Solar for Apartments rules, and the federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program (<Money id="fed-battery-discount-pct" /> off upfront cost for eligible solar-connected batteries).

## What this checker does not do

- **Does not store or send your answers:** Every step runs in your browser. If you are a renter and click "Share this result with your landlord", your answers are encoded into the `#a=` part of the page link on your device—nothing is sent to or stored on our servers.
- **Does not replace an on-site assessment or written quote:** VEU discounts vary with certificate prices, product sizing, switchboard or flue work, and provider fees. Only an accredited provider (or a licensed installer working with one) confirms your actual point-of-sale discount in writing before installation starts—see [how VEU quotes work](/how-quotes-work/).
- **Does not lodge pre-approval or collect quote enquiries:** Solar Victoria rebates require pre-approval through an authorised retailer before installation starts, and our quote enquiry form is closed.
