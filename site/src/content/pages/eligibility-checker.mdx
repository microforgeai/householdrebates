## How this checker works

Our checker applies the household eligibility rules published by Energy Victoria and the VEU Specifications v25.0:

- **Heating and cooling:** Checks whether your building is more than 2 years old when replacing an existing heater, maps your existing heater and preferred upgrade to Energy Victoria's room split (<Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" />) or 22.4 kW ducted (<Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" />) scenarios, and shows the minimum customer co-payment rules from 30 September 2026 (including <Money id="vic-veu-copay-ducted" /> for any ducted system).
- **Hot water:** Checks that you are replacing an existing system in a building at least 2 years old and that fewer than 2 hot water systems have been upgraded under VEU at your address. Shows Energy Victoria's electric-to-heat-pump (<Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" />) and mains-gas-to-heat-pump (<Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" />) figures alongside the <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> minimum customer co-payment, and flags bottled LPG for provider confirmation.
- **Ceiling insulation:** Uses the VEU Specifications v25.0 postcode table locally in your browser to compare Energy Victoria's metropolitan mild (<Money id="vic-veu-insulation-metro" />) and regional cold (<Money id="vic-veu-insulation-regional" />) examples, checks ceiling depth and halogen downlight safety exclusions, and notes mandatory pre-installation electrical and installer safety checks.
- **Induction cooktops:** Checks that you are replacing a gas cooktop in a home more than 2 years old with an eligible built-in or freestanding 3-zone unit (<Money id="vic-veu-induction" /> per product) and explains Energy Victoria's buy-first invoice process.
- **Draught proofing (weather sealing):** Checks for indoor open-flued or flueless gas heaters and open-flued gas water heaters (which are excluded due to carbon monoxide risk) and shows Energy Victoria's warranty-based examples (such as <Money id="vic-veu-sealing-house-5yr" /> for a house with 5-year warranty products).
