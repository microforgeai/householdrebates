## How this checker works

Our checker applies the household eligibility rules published by Energy Victoria:

- **Heating and cooling:** Checks whether your building is more than 2 years old (required when replacing an existing heater), whether work has already started, and maps your existing heater and preferred upgrade to Energy Victoria's room split (<Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" />) or ducted (<Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" />) tables.
- **Hot water:** Checks that you are replacing an existing system in a building at least 2 years old, that fewer than 2 hot water systems have been upgraded under VEU at your address, and distinguishes between electric-to-heat-pump (<Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" />) and gas-to-heat-pump (<Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" />) replacements, noting the <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" /> minimum customer co-contribution.
