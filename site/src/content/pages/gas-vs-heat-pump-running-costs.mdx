## Solar daytime timers, Midday Power Saver, and concession holders

If your home has rooftop solar PV (especially in high-sunshine areas such as <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/mildura/">Mildura</LinkIfEnabled>), running a heat pump water heater on a daytime timer lets you heat water with self-generated solar electricity instead of grid imports. Households without rooftop solar can also check the **Victorian Midday Power Saver** (from **1 October 2026**), an opt-in offer through electricity retailers providing **"three hours of free electricity"** from **11am to 2pm daily** (<SourceLink id="energyvic-midday-power-saver" label="Energy Victoria — Victorian Midday Power Saver" />) — setting a heat pump's built-in timer to heat its tank inside that 11am–2pm window shifts water-heating consumption into the midday period. Eligible cardholders should also check how the year-round Annual Electricity Concession compares with the six-month Winter Gas Concession on <a href="/victoria/concession-card-holders/">concession card holders in Victoria</a>, and check upfront VEU discounts in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
