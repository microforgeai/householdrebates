## No single mandatory sequence—plan around appliance age and 2027 hot water rules

Victorian building standards do **not** require existing owner-occupied homes to remove working gas space heaters or gas cooktops (<SourceLink id="energyvic-existing-homes" label="Energy Victoria — Existing residential buildings" />). No official Victorian source prescribes a single order for going all-electric; instead, households usually weigh two practical triggers:

- **The 1 March 2027 hot water trigger:** Under the currently legislated rule, when an existing mains gas water heater fails and needs replacing from 1 March 2027, it generally cannot be replaced with another mains gas heater (unless an exemption applies).
- **Upfront VEU discounts per step:** Replacing an inefficient mains gas water heater with a heat pump carries an indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" /> (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hot-water" label="Energy Victoria — Hot water system discounts" />); replacing a gas room heater with a split system lists <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-room-gas" />, or replacing ducted gas with a large 22.4 kW ducted reverse-cycle system lists <Money id="vic-veu-rcac-ducted-gas-22kw" /> (<SourceLink id="energyvic-heating-cooling" label="Energy Victoria — Heating and cooling discounts" />); and replacing a gas cooktop with an eligible <a href="/victoria/gas-cooktop-to-induction/">induction cooktop</a> lists <Money id="vic-veu-induction" /> per product (<SourceLink id="energyvic-induction" label="Energy Victoria — Induction cooktop discounts" />).

Compare annual energy bill reductions across scenarios in our <a href="/victoria/gas-vs-heat-pump-running-costs/">gas vs heat pump running costs picker</a>. (Households in affected regional networks should also check extra assistance in <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/solstice-gas-shutdown-towns/">Solstice gas shutdown towns</LinkIfEnabled>.)

## How gas meter abolishment works once your last gas appliance goes

Disconnecting your final gas appliance lets you close your gas account and avoid <Money id="vic-gas-supply-saving" /> per year in fixed gas supply charges (<SourceLink id="energyvic-induction" label="Energy Victoria — Induction cooktop discounts" />).

Under Essential Services Commission rules, Victorian gas distributors must publish clear customer information on disconnection and permanent abolishment (<SourceLink id="esc-gas-distribution-code" label="ESC — Gas Distribution System Code of Practice" />). You request abolishment through your **gas retailer**:

- **AusNet example timeframes:** AusNet lists **2 business days** for meter removal and **up to 15 business days** for a permanent service cut-and-cap (<SourceLink id="ausnet-gas-abolishment" label="AusNet — Requesting a gas disconnection, reconnection or abolishment" />); other gas distributors (Australian Gas Networks and Multinet Gas Networks) may differ and publish their own regulated fee schedules.
- **Timeline & compliance certificates:** See <a href="/victoria/how-long-does-an-upgrade-take/">how long a VEU upgrade and gas abolishment take</a>, and check your current appliances in the <a href="/victoria/eligibility-checker/">Victoria Rebate Eligibility Checker</a>.
