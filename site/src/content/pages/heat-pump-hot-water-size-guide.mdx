## How Energy Victoria's tank sizing guide works

Unlike instantaneous gas wall heaters that heat water on demand, a heat pump water heater stores heated water in an insulated tank. Energy Victoria's consumer factsheet (<SourceLink id="energyvic-hw-factsheet" label="Energy Victoria — VEU water heating consumer factsheet (PDF)" />) matches storage capacity to bedrooms first, then advises sizing up for heavy hot water routines or daytime-only solar timers.

## Cold climates, noise, and the 425-litre STC threshold

Before finalising a model with your plumber or VEU accredited provider:

- **Cooler climates and frost protection:** In cooler Victorian regions (such as <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/ballarat/">Ballarat</LinkIfEnabled>, <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/macedon-ranges/">Macedon Ranges</LinkIfEnabled>, and other zone 5 postcodes), Energy Victoria advises considering a system with **frost protection** (<SourceLink id="energyvic-existing-homes" label="Energy Victoria — Existing residential buildings" />), and the consumer factsheet notes electric booster elements can assist cold-weather recovery.
- **Operating noise and placement:** Energy Victoria's factsheet notes that heat pumps produce noise comparable to an air conditioner while heating water, so position the tank and compressor clear of bedroom windows and neighbour fences.
- **Under 425 L for federal STCs:** Under Clean Energy Regulator rules, Small-scale Technology Certificates apply to eligible air-source heat pumps with a volumetric capacity **under 425 L** (<SourceLink id="cer-heat-pumps" label="Clean Energy Regulator — Solar water heaters and air source heat pumps" />).
