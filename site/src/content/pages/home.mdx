## How Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) discounts work for homes

The Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) program provides upfront, point-of-sale discounts when Victorian households replace older heating, cooling, and hot water equipment with approved high-efficiency electric alternatives. Rather than applying for a cash refund after you pay, a VEU accredited provider calculates the Victorian Energy Efficiency Certificates (VEECs) your upgrade creates and deducts that value from your quote.

<Callout type="caution" title="Involve an accredited provider before any work starts">
  Energy Victoria strongly advises contacting a VEU accredited provider (or using an installer who works with one) before you buy or install any equipment. Retrospective discounts after installation work has started are typically not possible. Read our detailed guide on <a href="/victoria/reverse-cycle-vs-gas-heater/">the VEU timing rule</a>.
</Callout>

## Heat pump and solar-boosted hot water discounts

- Replacing an **inefficient electric** storage water heater with an approved heat pump has an indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-electric-to-heatpump" />.
- Replacing an **inefficient gas** water heater with an approved heat pump has an indicative VEU discount of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-gas-to-heatpump" />.

Every VEU hot water upgrade requires a minimum customer co-contribution of <Money id="vic-veu-hw-min-contribution" />, and the building must be at least two years old. ## Victoria's 1 March 2027 gas hot water replacement rule

Under the currently legislated rule, from **1 March 2027**, when a mains gas water heater (including a gas pool heater) in an existing Victorian home reaches the end of its life and needs replacing, it cannot be replaced with another mains gas water heater and must be replaced with an efficient electric system such as a heat pump or solar-boosted water heater, unless an exemption applies. Under the currently legislated 2027 rules, bottled LPG systems are not affected, and owner-occupiers are not required to replace existing gas space heating or gas cooktops.

For rental properties, currently legislated minimum efficiency standards also begin on **1 March 2027** when a hot water unit or main living area heater fails beyond repair, and for main living area cooling at the start of a new lease. Read our full breakdown in the <a href="/victoria/gas-to-electric-hot-water-2027/">1 March 2027 Gas to Electric Hot Water guide</a>.
