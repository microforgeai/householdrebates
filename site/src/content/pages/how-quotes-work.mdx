## Roles: licensed installers vs VEU accredited providers

Two parties are typically involved in a household upgrade under the Victorian Energy Upgrades program:

1. **The licensed installer (electrician, plumber, or HVAC technician):** Measures your home, supplies and installs the approved reverse-cycle air conditioner or heat pump water heater, decommissions the old system, and issues plumbing/electrical compliance certificates.
2. **The VEU accredited provider (accredited person):** Authorised by the Essential Services Commission (ESC) to create Victorian Energy Efficiency Certificates (VEECs) once the upgrade evidence is verified. Some large installers are accredited providers themselves; many local installers partner with an accredited provider.

<Callout type="info" title="You must know who the accredited provider is">
  Under the Essential Services Commission's lead generation and marketing rules (<SourceLink id="esc-leadgen-factsheet" label="ESC Lead Generation Fact Sheet v1.2" />), you must be told the name of the accredited person creating the certificates for your upgrade.
</Callout>

<h2 id="your-rights">Your rights under the VEU code of conduct</h2>

Every accredited provider, installer, and lead generator participating in the Victorian Energy Upgrades program must follow the **VEU code of conduct** enforced by the Essential Services Commission:

- **Ban on cold calls and doorknocking:** Telemarketing cold calls have been banned since **1 May 2024**, and unsolicited doorknocking has been banned since **1 August 2024**, unless you have given **express prior consent** (<SourceLink id="esc-bans-faq" label="ESC Telemarketing and Doorknocking Bans FAQ v1.1" />).
- **3-month consent limit:** When you give express prior consent via an online enquiry form, that consent lasts for **3 months** unless you withdraw it sooner or explicitly agree to a different period.
- **Channel and recipient specificity:** You choose whether you consent to be contacted by phone, email, and/or a home visit to measure and quote, and the consent form names the installation partner who will contact you.
- **Accurate, honest information:** Marketers and installers must not claim to work for the Victorian Government, the VEU program, or the Essential Services Commission, and must not promise a discount before checking your eligibility.
