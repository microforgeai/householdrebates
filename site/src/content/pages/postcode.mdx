<h2 id="what-each-column-means">What each postcode column means</h2>

Every lookup card above reads directly from Table A (`Location Variable List`) of the <SourceLink id="vic-veu-spec-v25" label="VEU Specifications v25.0 PDF" /> and matches suburbs and councils using <SourceLink id="abs-asgs-allocation" label="ABS ASGS Edition 3 allocation files" />:

- **VEU area (`Metropolitan` or `Regional`):** Sets the regional factor in VEU certificate formulas—for example `0.98` for Metropolitan postcodes and `1.04` for Regional postcodes in Part 48 ceiling insulation. Postcodes near Melbourne's outer boundary do not always match council lines, whereas inner postcodes such as <a href="/victoria/melbourne/">inner Melbourne</a> sit uniformly in Metropolitan Mild.
- **Reticulated (mains) gas (`Yes` or `No`):** Shows whether Table A lists piped mains gas in that postcode. Where Table A records no reticulated mains gas, homes with gas appliances typically run on bottled LPG rather than a street gas main—compare electric upgrade order in our <a href="/victoria/get-off-gas-planner/">Get-Off-Gas Planner</a> or <LinkIfEnabled href="/victoria/lpg-homes/">check our bottled LPG homes guide</LinkIfEnabled>.
- **VEU climatic region (`Mild`, `Cold`, or `Hot`):** Sets climate-dependent greenhouse gas saving rates across space conditioning and building-shell activities. Under Part 48 ceiling insulation, annual greenhouse gas savings are `0.00576` (Mild), `0.00771` (Cold), or `0.00546` (Hot) tonnes CO₂-e per m² per year.
- **Heat pump zone (`zone 4` or `zone 5`):** Sets the hot water climate zone used in VEU water heating certificate calculations. Colder zone 5 postcodes experience lower winter ambient temperatures—use our <a href="/victoria/heat-pump-hot-water-size-guide/">Heat Pump Hot Water Sizing Guide</a> when comparing cold-climate tank sizes.
- **Localities and council (LGA):** Derived from Australian Bureau of Statistics mesh-block allocation files (`POA 2021`, `SAL 2021`, and `LGA 2025`), weighted by 2021 Census dwelling counts. Postcodes that cross council boundaries list each council's dwelling share, and non-residential PO-box postcodes with zero Census dwellings show an indicative council.
