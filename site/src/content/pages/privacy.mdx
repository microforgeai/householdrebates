## 1. Who we are

This website (`householdrebates.com.au`, **Household Rebates**) is operated by **Agenor Services Pty Ltd** (ABN `29 644 447 263`), located in Melbourne, Victoria, Australia (**we**, **us**, **our**).

`<<PRIVACY_ACT_OPT_IN_OR_BASIS>>` Because installers pay us a fee for each enquiry, we only pass on your details with your express consent (<SourceLink id="oaic-trading" label="OAIC — Trading in personal information" />; <SourceLink id="oaic-small-business" label="OAIC — Small business" />).

## 2. APP 5 Collection Notice (when you submit a quote form)

When our quote enquiry form is active and you choose to submit it:

- **What we collect:** Your first name, optional last name, email address, phone number, Victorian postcode, property tenure (owner, renter, or landlord), whether the building is over 2 years old, current heating/hot water system, upgrade interest, whether you've already bought a unit or started work, whether your landlord has approved the work (renters), preferred contact channels (phone, email, and/or home visit), optional notes, how you heard about us, the page you sent the form from, and the exact wording and timestamp of your consent.
- **What we do not collect:** We do not ask for your street address, date of birth, income, concession card numbers, or government identifiers on our form.
- **Why we collect it:** To pass your quote enquiry to the **named participating installer(s)** shown on the form so they can contact you via your selected channel(s) about a quote, and the installer may share your details with its named accredited provider only to create the VEU certificates for your installation, and to maintain an auditable record of your express prior consent under the VEU code of conduct (<SourceLink id="esc-leadgen-factsheet" label="ESC — Lead generation and marketing fact sheet" />).
- **How we use it:** We use your enquiry details only to pass your enquiry to the installer(s) you agreed to and to keep the consent record. We don't send you marketing, add you to our alert list, or sell or give your details to anyone else.
- **Commercial benefit disclosure:** The participating installer pays Agenor Services Pty Ltd a fee for each valid enquiry we send them.
- **If you do not provide this information:** We cannot pass a quote enquiry to a participating installer. You can still browse all guides, use the Eligibility Checker anonymously, or contact an accredited provider directly from Energy Victoria's website.

## 3. Express prior consent and how to withdraw it (3-month limit)

In accordance with the Essential Services Commission's rules under the VEU code of conduct (<SourceLink id="esc-bans-faq" label="ESC Telemarketing and Doorknocking Bans FAQ v1.1" />):

- Your express prior consent to be contacted by the named installer lasts for **3 months** from the date you submit the form, unless you withdraw it earlier.
- **How to withdraw consent:** You can withdraw your consent at any time by replying to your confirmation email or emailing `privacy@householdrebates.com.au` with your email address or reference number (`HR-XXXXXX`). When you withdraw consent, we record your withdrawal timestamp and notify each installer who received your enquiry to cease contact immediately.

## 4. Email alerts (rule-change notifications)

When our rule-change email alert sign-up is active and you choose to subscribe:

- **Single purpose:** We collect your email address (together with your unticked consent snapshot, the page path you subscribed from, and temporary anti-abuse metadata) solely to email you when official Victorian or federal energy rebate rules change.
- **Legal basis and double opt-in:** Processing is based on your express consent (`alerts-v2`) under the *Spam Act 2003* (Cth) (<SourceLink id="acma-spam" label="ACMA — Avoid sending spam" />). Submitting the form sends one confirmation email with a link valid for **72 hours**. No rule-change alert emails are sent unless you click that confirmation link, and unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted once the 72-hour window expires.
- **Checker answers stay private:** If you subscribe from the Eligibility Checker result screen, the alert form sends only your email address and consent. Your checker answers stay in your browser and are never transmitted with the alert sign-up.
- **No sharing with installers:** Alert subscriber addresses are stored in a dedicated database separate from quote enquiries and are never shared with participating installers, Victorian Energy Upgrades accredited providers, or commercial sponsors.
- **Email service provider and overseas storage:** Transactional confirmation emails and rule-change alerts are sent for us by our email service provider, `<<EMAIL_PROVIDER>>`, which may store email delivery data in `<<EMAIL_PROVIDER_COUNTRIES>>` (see Section 8 below and the APP 5 collection notice directly above the sign-up button).
- **Unsubscribing:** Every alert email includes a one-step unsubscribe link and mail-client one-click unsubscribe support that take effect immediately, or you can reply to an email or write to `privacy@householdrebates.com.au` (processed promptly and always within 5 business days). When you unsubscribe or ask for erasure, we keep only a non-reversible keyed cryptographic hash (`HMAC-SHA256`) of your email address on a suppression list so we never email you again unless you sign up again and confirm.

## 5. Cookies, analytics, and server logs

- **No tracking cookies:** We do not set analytics or advertising cookies in your browser, and we do not load third-party tracking scripts.
- **Eligibility Checker:** Your answers in the Eligibility Checker stay in your browser (`sessionStorage` only if you proceed to the quote form on the same visit).
- **Server logs and rate limiting:** Our web server keeps standard access logs that include your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the referring page. We keep them for 30 days and then delete them. Separately, a log of visits referred by AI assistants keeps no IP addresses and is kept for 180 days. When you submit a form, we store a one-way cryptographic hash (`HMAC-SHA256`) of your IP address solely to enforce anti-spam rate limits; your raw IP address is never stored in our database.

## 7. Access, correction, deletion, and complaints

You may request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal information held by us at any time by emailing `privacy@householdrebates.com.au`. We respond to all privacy requests within 30 days.

If you have a complaint about how we have handled your personal information, please contact `privacy@householdrebates.com.au` first. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 5 business days, look into it, and give you a written answer within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
