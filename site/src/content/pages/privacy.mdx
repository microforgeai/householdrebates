## 1. Who we are

This website (`householdrebates.com.au`, **Household Rebates**) is operated by **Agenor Services Pty Ltd** (ABN `29 644 447 263`), located in Melbourne, Victoria, Australia (**we**, **us**, **our**).

Under the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth), disclosing personal information to another organisation for a commercial benefit (such as a lead generation fee) is classified as "trading in personal information" (<SourceLink id="oaic-trading" label="OAIC — Trading in personal information" />). Accordingly, we comply with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) and obtain your express consent before passing any enquiry to a participating installer.

## 2. APP 5 Collection Notice (when you submit a quote form)

When our quote enquiry form is active and you choose to submit it:

- **What we collect:** Your first name, optional last name, email address, phone number, Victorian postcode, property tenure (owner, renter, or landlord), whether the building is over 2 years old, current heating/hot water system, upgrade interest, preferred contact channels (phone, email, and/or home visit), optional notes, how you heard about us, and the exact wording and timestamp of your consent.
- **What we do not collect:** We do not ask for your street address, date of birth, income, concession card numbers, or government identifiers on our form.
- **Why we collect it:** To pass your quote enquiry to the **named participating installer(s)** shown on the form (who work with the named Victorian Energy Upgrades accredited provider) so they can contact you via your selected channel(s) about a quote, and to maintain an auditable record of your express prior consent under the VEU code of conduct.
- **Commercial benefit disclosure:** The participating installer pays Agenor Services Pty Ltd a fee for each valid enquiry we send them.
- **If you do not provide this information:** We cannot pass a quote enquiry to a participating installer. You can still browse all guides, use the Eligibility Checker anonymously, or contact an accredited provider directly from Energy Victoria's website.

## 3. Express prior consent and how to withdraw it (3-month limit)

In accordance with the Essential Services Commission's rules under the VEU code of conduct (<SourceLink id="esc-bans-faq" label="ESC Telemarketing and Doorknocking Bans FAQ v1.1" />):

- Your express prior consent to be contacted by the named installer lasts for **3 months** from the date you submit the form, unless you withdraw it earlier.
- **How to withdraw consent:** You can withdraw your consent at any time by replying to your confirmation email or emailing `privacy@householdrebates.com.au` with your email address or reference number (`HR-XXXXXX`). When you withdraw consent, we record your withdrawal timestamp and notify each installer who received your enquiry to cease contact immediately.

## 4. Email alerts (rule-change notifications)

When our rule-change email alert sign-up is active and you choose to subscribe:

- **Single purpose:** We collect your email address (together with your unticked consent snapshot, the page path you subscribed from, and temporary anti-abuse metadata) solely to email you when official Victorian or federal energy rebate rules change.
- **Legal basis and double opt-in:** Processing is based on your express consent (`alerts-v1`) under the *Spam Act 2003* (Cth) (<SourceLink id="acma-spam" label="ACMA — Avoid sending spam" />). Submitting the form sends one confirmation email with a link valid for **72 hours**. No rule-change alert emails are sent unless you click that confirmation link, and unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted once the 72-hour window expires.
- **Checker answers stay private:** If you subscribe from the Eligibility Checker result screen, the alert form sends only your email address and consent. Your checker answers stay in your browser and are never transmitted with the alert sign-up.
- **No sharing with installers:** Alert subscriber addresses are stored in a dedicated database separate from quote enquiries and are never shared with participating installers, Victorian Energy Upgrades accredited providers, or commercial sponsors.
- **Email service provider and overseas storage:** Transactional confirmation emails and rule-change alerts are sent for us by our configured email service provider named in the APP 5 collection notice directly above the sign-up button. Where our configured provider may store or process delivery data outside Australia, that overseas disclosure is stated in the collection notice before you submit the form.
- **Unsubscribing:** Every alert email includes a one-step unsubscribe link and mail-client one-click unsubscribe support that take effect immediately, or you can reply to an email or write to `privacy@householdrebates.com.au` (processed promptly and always within 5 business days). When you unsubscribe or ask for erasure, we keep only a non-reversible keyed cryptographic hash (`HMAC-SHA256`) of your email address on a suppression list so your address cannot be re-added.

## 5. Cookies, analytics, and server logs

- **No tracking cookies:** We do not set analytics or advertising cookies in your browser, and we do not load third-party tracking scripts.
- **Eligibility Checker:** Your answers in the Eligibility Checker stay in your browser (`sessionStorage` only if you proceed to the quote form on the same visit).
- **Server logs and rate limiting:** Our web server records standard access logs (kept for 30 days) and a separate AI-referrer log that excludes IP addresses (kept for 180 days). When you submit a form, we store a one-way cryptographic hash (`HMAC-SHA256`) of your IP address solely to enforce anti-spam rate limits; your raw IP address is never stored in our database.

## 6. Data storage, security, and retention periods

All enquiry, alert, and consent records are stored in access-restricted SQLite databases on our Australian-managed server environment with encrypted daily backups (`age` encryption).

| Record category | Retention period | Action at end of period |
|---|---|---|
| Quote enquiry contact details (name, email, phone, message) | 12 months from creation | Permanently nulled by our automated nightly purge job (`status = purged`) |
| `ip_hash` and `user_agent` on enquiries and alert sign-ups | 30 days from creation | Nulled by our automated nightly purge jobs |
| Quote consent audit record (version, SHA-256 hash, snapshot, scopes, timestamps) | 24 months after expiry or withdrawal | Deleted after the VEU audit window closes |
| Delivery and invoicing metadata (installer ID, timestamp, fee) | 7 years | Retained for Australian taxation compliance (contains no personal contact details after purge) |
| Confirmed email alert subscriber (`email`, `email_hash`, timestamps) | Until you unsubscribe or request erasure | Unsubscribe marks `status = unsubscribed` and adds `email_hash` to our suppression list; erasure nulls `email`, `ip_hash`, and `user_agent` |
| Unconfirmed email alert sign-up (`pending` or `expired`) | 72 hours from sign-up | Deleted by our automated nightly alert purge job |
| Email alert consent audit record (`alerts-v1` snapshot, SHA-256 hash, timestamps) | 24 months after unsubscribe | Deleted by our automated nightly alert purge job |
| Email alert suppression list (`HMAC-SHA256` email hash only) | Indefinitely | Non-reversible hash retained permanently so unsubscribed or erased addresses are never emailed again |

## 7. Access, correction, deletion, and complaints

You may request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal information held by us at any time by emailing `privacy@householdrebates.com.au`. We respond to all privacy requests within 30 days.

If you have a complaint about how we have handled your personal information, please contact `privacy@householdrebates.com.au` first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the **Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)** at `www.oaic.gov.au` or on `1300 363 992`.
