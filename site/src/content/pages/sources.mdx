## How we verify figures and rules

1. **Primary sources only for figures:** Our build pipeline enforces that every figure in `figures/vic-veu.yaml` links to a source marked `official: true`.
2. **Scenario accuracy:** Where an official table shows a range or an "up to" maximum across multiple system sizes, we reproduce the full scenario table so readers can see how room gas replacements differ from whole-home ducted replacements.
3. **45-day staleness gate:** If any figure's `checkedOn` date is older than 45 days, our automated build check fails until we re-read the official Energy Victoria page and confirm the numbers.
4. **Keeping unverified items off pages:** Where a claim comes only from a third-party summary or stale summary page rather than a primary official source, we log it internally and keep it off our published pages until verified against the primary source.
