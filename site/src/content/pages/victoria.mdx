## Which upgrades does Victorian Energy Upgrades cover?

Under Energy Victoria's current household product list (<SourceLink id="energyvic-veu-products" label="Energy Victoria — VEU products" />), accredited providers offer upfront point-of-sale discounts on approved:

- **Heating and cooling:** high-efficiency reverse-cycle split, multi-split, and ducted air conditioners.
- **Hot water systems:** air-source heat pump and solar-boosted water heaters replacing inefficient electric or gas systems.
- **Ceiling insulation:** under-insulated or uninsulated ceilings (for example, <Money id="vic-veu-insulation-metro" /> on an average 150 m² metropolitan Melbourne house).
- **Induction cooktops:** built-in or freestanding units replacing an existing gas cooktop.
- **Building envelope and appliances:** weather sealing (draught proofing), window glazing, efficient showerheads, clothes dryers, fridges and freezers, in-home displays, and televisions.

## What changes in Victoria in 2027?

Three currently legislated Victorian Government rule changes begin in 2027 (<SourceLink id="energyvic-electrification" label="Energy Victoria — Electrification and efficiency standards" />, <SourceLink id="solarvic-pv-into-veu" label="Solar Victoria — Solar PV support moving into VEU" />):

1. **1 January 2027 — All-electric new homes:** Under currently legislated rules, new residential buildings requiring a building permit must be built all-electric without reticulated mains gas connections.
2. **1 March 2027 — Existing gas hot water replacements and rental standards:** Under currently legislated rules, when a mains gas water heater in an existing home fails and needs replacing from 1 March 2027, it must switch to an efficient electric alternative unless an exemption applies. Currently legislated phased minimum energy efficiency standards for rental properties also start from 1 March 2027.
3. **1 July 2027 — Solar PV moves into VEU:** Residential solar panel support transitions from Solar Homes into the Victorian Energy Upgrades program, with current Solar Homes solar panel rebates continuing until 30 June 2027.
