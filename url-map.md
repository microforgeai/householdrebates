## Rule changes and corrections found (13 Nov 2026)

1. **New VEU co-payments from 30 Sep 2026.** Ducted reverse-cycle (any size) now needs at least **$3,000 incl GST** from the customer (was $1,000). Multi-split ≥ 10 kW: $3,000. Multi-split < 10 kW: $1,000. Single split < 10 kW: still $200. Source: [VEU Specifications v25.0](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0041/795488/Victorian-Energy-Upgrades-Specifications-2018-Version-25.pdf), Part 6, and the [Energy Vic specifications page](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades/installers/industry-specifications). Energy Vic's consumer page doesn't mention it yet. **Existing pages `/victoria/air-conditioner-rebate/` and `/victoria/reverse-cycle-vs-gas-heater/`, and the checker's ducted results, need this added.**
2. **2027 gas hot water exemptions are now verified.** solution-design.md §1 lists them as UNVERIFIED (third-party). I read the regulation text: [SR 140/2025 reg 6, new reg 45E (AustLII)](https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/num_reg/bapar2025n140o2025463/s6.html); authority: [legislation.vic.gov.au](https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/as-made/statutory-rules/building-and-plumbing-amendment-regulations-2025). Full list in the `/victoria/gas-hot-water-ban-exemptions/` row. It includes contracts signed before 1 Mar 2027 if the work is done within 3 months.
3. **New VEU product: ceiling insulation**, up to **$1,500** in metro Melbourne and **$2,100** in regional Victoria (150 m² example). Stage 2 is open to all eligible homes. Sources: [Energy Vic](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades/products/insulation-discounts) and [Solar Victoria](https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/victorians-cut-energy-bills-ceiling-insulation-discounts). Not in the current pack; added as a Wave 1 money page.
4. **Solar Homes income cap cut to $150,000** (from $210,000) on 1 Jul 2026, for PV and hot water. [Source](https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/solar-homes-program-continuing-provide-targeted-assistance). Existing `/victoria/heat-pump-rebate/` should state it.
5. **Solar PV support moves into VEU from 1 Jul 2027.** Current PV rebates (up to $1,400 plus a loan) continue to 30 Jun 2027. [Source](https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/solar-support-continue-through-victorian-energy-upgrades-program). This affects the reserved `/solar-battery/` section.
6. **Stale figures on official pages. Don't use these:** Energy Vic's ['Latest discounts and rebates'](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/households/latest-discounts-and-rebates) page (updated 24 Jul 2026) says ducted savings 'up to $1,410' a year (the product page says $1,140) and PV 'up to $2,800' (the PV page says $1,400). The [Solstice page](https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/supporting-solstice-customers) also says $2,800. Rule: product pages beat summary pages.
7. **Solar for Apartments is mid-change.** The news (26 Oct 2026) says buildings of up to 150 lots and no property value limit, but the [program page](https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/apartments) still says 3–50 lots.
8. **VEET Act amended** (Act No. 37/2026). On proclamation it renames the Act to the 'Victorian Energy Upgrades Target Act' and VEECs to 'energy upgrade certificates'. Watch for wording changes. [ESC legislation page](https://www.esc.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades-program/about-victorian-energy-upgrades-program/veu-program-legislation).
9. **Policy risk:** the Victorian state election is on 28 Nov 2026. DEECA's [work program](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades/installers/industry-market-update-work-program) also lists pending responses on the water heating and space heating reviews, plus a new hydronic activity. Any of these could change the hot water or heating figures. Re-check right after the election.
10. **Commercial flag (business side):** the state-owned SEC launched [Easy Electric SEC](https://www.secvictoria.com.au/news/going-electric-at-home-is-now-easy-as-with-sec) on 25 May 2026, a free quote-matching service with endorsed installers. Councils (Whittlesea, Yarra Ranges) route residents to it. It competes directly with our lead-fee model. I recommend mentioning it openly on `/victoria/find-an-accredited-provider/`, and Guri should weigh it in the installer and lead-fee decisions.
11. **LPG:** VEU hot water Part 3 covers 'gas or LPG' water heaters ([spec](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0041/795488/Victorian-Energy-Upgrades-Specifications-2018-Version-25.pdf)), but Energy Vic's table shows only a 'gas' figure. The LPG amount is UNVERIFIED, so say 'provider confirms'.
12. **Confirmed unchanged:** gas → heat pump $560; electric → heat pump $630; solar-boosted $910/$700; room gas → RCAC $630–$1,610; ducted gas up to $2,520/$4,200/$5,530; combined with Solar Homes up to $1,560/$1,960.

## Method notes for Indie Builder

- **VEU postcode table:** SPEC25's 'Location Variable List' (Table A) gives every postcode as Metropolitan/Regional, reticulated gas yes/no, climatic region Mild/Cold/Hot, and heat pump climatic zone 4/5. I parsed 719 rows from the v25.0 PDF. Area pages should quote this table for their postcodes, and the size guide can use it for 'cold area' notes.
- **NCC climate zones:** checked by point-in-polygon against the [ABCB climate zone shapefile](https://data.gov.au/data/dataset/australian-climate-zone-map) (CC BY 4.0, attribution required) at town-centre coordinates. Results: Ballarat 7, Yarra Ranges (Lilydale, Healesville, Olinda, Warburton) 7, Shepparton 4, Bendigo 6, and Melbourne, Geelong, Frankston, Mornington, Wyndham, Melton, Casey and Whittlesea all 6. On the page, tell readers to confirm by postcode on the [ABCB Climate Map](https://www.abcb.gov.au/abcb-climate-map).
- **Provider counts** (Energy Vic regional lists, 13 Nov 2026), hot water / heating-cooling: Barwon South West 30/50, Gippsland 19/38, Grampians 31/46, Greater Melbourne 79/116, Hume 27/60, Loddon Mallee 26/42. They change often; re-check monthly or drop the numbers.
- **Checker gaps** for Indie Builder to scope: no insulation, induction or weather-sealing paths; ducted results need the $3,000 co-payment; the LPG row needs 'provider confirms'.
- energy.vic.gov.au is behind Cloudflare and rate-limits parallel fetches. `check-sources.mjs` should fetch one page at a time.
